First visit?

Your first simulator reservation is a required 30-minute orientation. After your instructor records the orientation as complete, you may reserve either Free Flight or an available Instructor-Led Session.

Before You Reserve

How simulator checkout works

1

Choose an open time

Only instructor-approved 30-minute slots are shown. A slot disappears once it has been reserved.

2

Complete orientation first

If you have not completed orientation, the system will reserve your selected time as an Orientation session.

3

Choose your session

After orientation, choose a 30-minute Free Flight slot or one of the instructor-scheduled guided sessions.

Orientation

First-Time User Session

  • 30 minutes
  • Required before independent simulator use
  • Includes controls, seating/setup, startup/shutdown, simulator care, and checkout expectations
  • Orientation is not considered complete until recorded by the instructor
Returning User

Free Flight

  • 30 minutes
  • Available only after orientation is complete
  • Use the simulator only during your reserved period
  • Leave the station ready for the next student
Returning User

Instructor-Led Session

  • Available only after orientation is complete
  • Offered on specific dates and times set by the instructor
  • Focuses on guided flight skills, procedures, or scenarios
  • Follow instructor directions for the entire scheduled session

Reservation Expectations

Use your reservation responsibly

Reserve only times you can attend. If you cannot attend, cancel the reservation so another student can use the simulator. Simulator access may be limited or suspended for repeated missed reservations or failure to follow simulator procedures.
