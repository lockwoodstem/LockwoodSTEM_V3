Instructor-Led Flight Series • F.2

Taxi, Takeoff & Climb

Focus question: How does a pilot transition from ground operations to a stable climb?

30-Minute Session

Instructor-led simulator lesson

Complete the required Orientation before taking this lesson. Your instructor controls the scenario, setup, and pacing.

Lesson Overview

Learning Targets

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

  • Use rudder steering and braking to control the aircraft on the ground.
  • Recognize the basic sequence from engine/start setup through takeoff.
  • Establish a stable climb after liftoff.

Instructor Setup

  • Position the aircraft at a parking or ramp location near the selected runway.
  • Use visual meteorological conditions and light wind for the first attempt.
  • Set the simulator so the student can complete taxi, runway alignment, takeoff, and climb within the session.
30-Minute Plan

Session Timeline

0–5 min

Brief the sequence

Review engine/start setup, taxi, braking, runway alignment, flap setting, takeoff roll, rotation, and initial climb.

5–10 min

Instructor demonstration

Demonstrate smooth taxi control, centerline tracking, takeoff acceleration, rotation, and climb attitude.

10–23 min

Student flight

Student taxis to the runway, lines up, completes the takeoff roll, rotates, and establishes a climb.

23–27 min

Second attempt or refinement

Repeat the takeoff or restart from runway position to improve directional control and climb stability.

27–30 min

Debrief

Discuss centerline control, rotation timing, airspeed awareness, and climb corrections.

Student Mission

Flight Tasks

  1. Taxi at a controlled speed using rudder steering and brakes.
  2. Stop and align with the runway before beginning the takeoff roll.
  3. Maintain directional control during acceleration.
  4. Rotate smoothly and avoid excessive pitch input.
  5. Establish a stable climb while monitoring airspeed and heading.
Completion Check

Ready to Move On?

  • Maintains reasonable taxi control and avoids abrupt braking.
  • Tracks the runway centerline during takeoff.
  • Uses smooth rotation and climb inputs.
  • Monitors airspeed and heading during the climb.

Instructor decision: Repeat, practice during Free Flight, or continue to the next instructor-led lesson.

Guide basis: The RealSimGear function checklist specifically verifies engine start, taxi and brake operation, run-up/powerplant checks, acceleration, nose-wheel/rudder steering, flap operation, and takeoff behavior.
