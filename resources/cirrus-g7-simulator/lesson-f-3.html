Instructor-Led Flight Series • F.3

Turns, Climbs & Descents

Focus question: How do bank angle, pitch, power, and coordination work together during basic maneuvers?

30-Minute Session

Instructor-led simulator lesson

Complete the required Orientation before taking this lesson. Your instructor controls the scenario, setup, and pacing.

Lesson Overview

Learning Targets

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

  • Perform controlled turns while maintaining awareness of altitude and airspeed.
  • Use coordinated pitch and power changes for climbs and descents.
  • Observe how configuration and power changes affect aircraft attitude.

Instructor Setup

  • Begin airborne at a comfortable altitude in visual conditions.
  • Use calm or light-wind conditions.
  • Choose an assigned altitude and starting heading for repeatable maneuver practice.
30-Minute Plan

Session Timeline

0–5 min

Maneuver brief

Review shallow and medium-bank turns, climb entry, descent entry, level-off, and trim.

5–10 min

Instructor demonstration

Demonstrate a coordinated turn, a 500-ft climb, a level-off, and a controlled descent.

10–22 min

Student practice

Practice left/right turns, climbs, descents, and level-offs while monitoring the PFD.

22–27 min

Sequence challenge

Fly the assigned heading/altitude sequence without pausing the simulator.

27–30 min

Debrief

Discuss coordination, bank control, vertical speed, and how power changes affected pitch and airspeed.

Student Mission

Flight Tasks

  1. From the assigned heading, make a 20–30° bank turn to a new heading.
  2. Climb approximately 1,000 ft while maintaining a stable heading.
  3. Level off and stabilize airspeed.
  4. Make a second turn, then descend approximately 1,000 ft.
  5. Return to straight-and-level flight.
Completion Check

Ready to Move On?

  • Uses a controlled bank angle and avoids excessive overcorrection.
  • Maintains awareness of heading while climbing or descending.
  • Levels off without large altitude overshoot.
  • Can explain the effect of power/configuration changes on pitch and airspeed.

Instructor decision: Repeat, practice during Free Flight, or continue to the next instructor-led lesson.

Guide basis: The QAG verifies normal and steep turns, climb performance, uncoordinated turn demonstrations, and configuration/power changes with corresponding pitch effects.
