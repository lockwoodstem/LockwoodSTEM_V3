IED Unit 0

Engineering Foundations & Rocket Launch Pad Challenge

Build engineering habits, safety routines, documentation practices, and early design-process skills through a hands-on aerospace challenge.

Unit Project

Rocket Launch Pad Design Challenge

Design and build a launch pad that fits and supports the provided 3D printed rocket, stands on its own, uses approved classroom materials, and is justified through sketches, testing, documentation, and presentation.

Final Evidence

Engineering Safety Readiness + Unit 0 Portfolio

Evidence includes notebook organization, problem definition, concepts, a build plan, prototype documentation, testing, improvements, a design review, and reflection.
