Unit Challenge

Lightweight Bridge Structural Design Challenge

Design and manufacture a lightweight bridge that spans a fixed 400 mm gap between two supports. The focus is load paths, truss and beam behavior, stiffness, mass, materials, joints, fabrication quality, and test evidence.

400 mmRequired clear span between supports.
65 mm maximumMaximum overall bridge width.
100 NRequired centered design load.

Fabrication + Testing Rules

Review the complete approved-material, laser-cutting, M3 fastening, and printed-connector limits below before fabrication. A one-piece laser-cut open-web plywood side truss is allowed; acrylic and fully printed bridges are not.

Final Evaluation

What Success Means

Qualify

Carry the 100 N centered design load while keeping midspan deflection at or below 10 mm and meeting dimensional and mass rules.

Measure

Record load vs. deflection, maximum supported load, actual mass, failure mode, and structural efficiency.

Defend

Use calculations, materials data, design evidence, manufacturing records, and test results to explain why the bridge behaved as it did.

POE UNIT 2 · REQUIRED DESIGN RULES

Bridge Materials, Connections & Manufacturing Specifications

Use these limits when selecting materials, designing joints, creating toolpaths, estimating mass, and requesting fabrication approval.

Bridge performance

  • 400 mm clear span between supports
  • 65 mm maximum overall width, including connectors and hardware
  • 100 N centered qualification load; deflection no more than 10 mm at 100 N
  • 150 g maximum completed bridge mass including adhesive, M3 hardware, and printed parts

Approved construction

  • Balsa, basswood, craft sticks, and plywood for primary structural members
  • Laser-cut complete open-web side trusses from a single plywood sheet are permitted
  • No acrylic, solid plywood side panels, or fully 3D-printed bridges
  • Connections: PVA wood glue, CA glue, interlocking joints, plywood gussets, approved printed fittings, or M3 nuts and bolts (M3 washers permitted)
  • No screws, other-size bolts, nails, staples, wire, hot glue, or epoxy

Laser-cut reinforcement

  • Approved plywood only, up to 3.2 mm (1/8 in) thick
  • Local gussets/reinforcement plates: at most 40 × 25 mm and 800 mm² plan area
  • At most two gusset layers at one connection, one per side
  • Maximum 20 mm overlap onto each connected structural member
  • The gusset limits do not restrict primary member lengths or complete open-web side trusses

3D-printed connectors

  • PLA or PETG fittings for joints, gussets, brackets, or mounts only; no printed chords, beams, verticals, diagonals, or decks
  • Maximum 35% infill; 4 wall loops; 5 top and 5 bottom layers; 0.28 mm layer height; 0.4 mm nozzle
  • Maximum 50 mm length and 15 g per individual printed part; 40 g total printed-part mass
  • Do not join printed parts to form prohibited continuous members
  • Only M3 nut-and-bolt fastening; captive-nut pockets allowed, printed threads prohibited

Design evidence

  • Fusion CAD or dimensioned fabrication drawings, plus a bill of materials and mass estimate
  • Explain load transfer at two critical joints and identify all fasteners and adhesives
  • For printed parts: submit CAD models, slicer settings, and measured part masses
  • For laser-cut parts: submit cut profiles and verify minimum web/kerf suitability
  • Inspect against these requirements before official testing; no repairs after official testing begins
