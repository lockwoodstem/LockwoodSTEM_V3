POE Unit 2

Aerospace Structures & Materials

Unit mission: Analyze how structures carry loads, how materials behave, and how evidence supports a payload support design.

Lesson MapResourcesBack to POE Hub
Unit Focus

Design structures that balance strength, stiffness, mass, materials, and load performance.

AnalyzeTestDefend

Every lesson builds toward a payload support structure supported by calculations, material evidence, and load-test data.

Unit Overview

What Unit 2 Builds

Students investigate loads, free body diagrams, vectors, moments, equilibrium, trusses, centroids, stiffness, materials, density, testing, and structural design evidence.

Structural Analysis

Students identify force effects and use diagrams, vectors, moments, and equilibrium to explain structural behavior.

Materials + Testing

Students compare material families, density, structural efficiency, and test behavior using controlled evidence.

Design Challenge

Students plan, build, load-test, iterate, and defend an aerospace payload support structure.

Unit Project

Aerospace Payload Support Structure

Design and test a lightweight structure that supports a payload under defined criteria and constraints.

Design goal

Support the required payload safely while using material and geometry efficiently.

Required evidence

Problem statement, criteria and constraints, sketches, calculations, decision matrix, prototype photos, load data, and final design review.

Performance data

Teams collect repeated measurements such as supported load, mass, deflection, failure point, structural efficiency, and revision evidence.
