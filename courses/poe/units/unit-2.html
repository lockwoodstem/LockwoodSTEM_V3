Bridge Structures & Materials

Driving question: How can engineers design a lightweight bridge that safely carries load while minimizing mass and deformation?

18-Lesson Design Sequence

Learn structural engineering concepts by using them to engineer a real bridge.

Every lesson produces evidence that becomes part of the final bridge engineering design review.

Unit Challenge

Lightweight Bridge Structural Design Challenge

Design and manufacture a lightweight bridge that spans a fixed 400 mm gap between two supports. The focus is load paths, truss and beam behavior, stiffness, mass, materials, joints, fabrication quality, and test evidence.

400 mmRequired clear span between supports.
65 mm maximumMaximum overall bridge width.
100 NRequired centered design load.
≤ 10 mmMaximum midspan deflection at design load.
≤ 150 gMaximum finished structural mass.
Standard SupportsCommon teacher-provided support locations.

Fabrication + Testing Rules

Use approved teacher-supplied structural materials and adhesive. Laser-cut gussets, deck plates, and joint plates are allowed. Small approved 3D-printed connectors may be used, but the complete bridge may not simply be 3D printed. Final testing uses the standard supports and centered load block with progressive loading. Testing beyond the design load occurs only under teacher direction.

Final Evaluation

What Success Means

Qualify

Carry the 100 N centered design load while keeping midspan deflection at or below 10 mm and meeting dimensional and mass rules.

Measure

Record load vs. deflection, maximum supported load, actual mass, failure mode, and structural efficiency.

Defend

Use calculations, materials data, design evidence, manufacturing records, and test results to explain why the bridge behaved as it did.
