POE Unit 1 • Lesson 1.13

Final Mechanism Design Review

Engineering focus: How do engineers use calculations, test data, and revision evidence to defend a design?

Start HereUnit 1 Overview
Engineering Challenge

Finish Unit 1 by presenting the engineering story: requirement, concept, mechanism, test, failure, revision, and evidence-based conclusion.

Start Here

What You Need to Do

Work On

  • Organize the final design-review evidence.
  • Prepare a concise team presentation.
  • Show the final mechanism and how its subsystems work.
  • Explain calculations and test data.
  • Defend the major design revision.
  • Complete an individual reflection.

Submit

  • Final design-review presentation
  • Final prototype or final prototype photos
  • Mechanism diagram
  • Calculation evidence
  • Baseline/post-revision test evidence
  • Individual reflection

Done When

Your team can explain what the system was required to do, how it works, how it performed, what changed during iteration, and what the evidence says about the final design.

Final Review

Engineering Design Review

Present your final combined-mechanism system as an engineering team. Your job is not simply to show that it works; your team must explain the design decisions, calculations, test evidence, failures, and revisions that led to the final solution.

Presentation Structure

Required Design Review Content

1. Mission & Requirements

State the task and the measurable requirements your system had to satisfy.

2. Final Design

Show the complete system and identify the two mechanical subsystems.

3. Engineering Concepts

Explain at least two relevant concepts such as gear ratio, torque, mechanical advantage, work, power, friction, alignment, or stability.

4. Testing Evidence

Show the baseline and revised test data, including averages and reliability.

5. Revision

Identify the problem you targeted, what changed, and why.

6. Final Evaluation

Use evidence to explain what performed well and what you would improve with more time.

Evidence

What Must Be Visible

  • Initial concept/system architecture
  • Final prototype photos or live prototype
  • Labeled mechanism diagram
  • At least one relevant calculation
  • Baseline and post-revision data
  • Before/after comparison
  • Documented engineering revision
  • Final conclusion supported by evidence
Presentation

Team Design Review Expectations

  • Every team member must participate.
  • Use engineering vocabulary accurately.
  • Explain the evidence rather than reading numbers from the screen.
  • Be prepared to answer questions about failure modes, tradeoffs, and design choices.
  • End with one specific improvement your team would make next.
Individual Reflection

Final Unit Reflection

Individually explain which engineering concept most changed the way you approached mechanism design during Unit 1. Use one specific example from your team's builds or tests.
