What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can research mechanism options and use a decision matrix to justify a concept direction.

Evidence

Research notes and decision matrix

Success Looks Like

Research evidence informs the criteria and scores, weights reflect mission priorities, and the selected concept has the strongest justified total.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

This page highlights the lesson elements you need for practice, evidence, and submission.

1. Research mechanism options

Collect relevant examples, equations, component limits, and failure considerations.

2. Build the matrix

Weight criteria and score each concept using a consistent scale.

3. Check sensitivity

Determine whether a small weight or score change would alter the decision.

Lesson Interactive

Run a Weighted Concept Decision

Adjust criterion weights, review the fixed evidence-based scores, and select the concept with the highest weighted total.
