POE Unit 1 • Lesson 1.11

Combined Mechanisms Design Challenge

Engineering focus: How can multiple mechanisms be integrated into one reliable engineering system?

Start HereUnit 1 Overview
Engineering Challenge

Move beyond a single mechanism: design a system in which two mechanical subsystems work together to complete one payload-transfer mission.

Start Here

What You Need to Do

Work On

  • Define the START and DELIVERY positions.
  • Create a system architecture showing at least two mechanical subsystems.
  • Build the combined VEX mechanism.
  • Transfer the standardized payload through the required horizontal and vertical distances.
  • Complete three consecutive successful cycles.

Submit

  • System architecture diagram
  • Labeled concept sketch
  • Completed combined-mechanism prototype
  • Mechanism justification
  • Three-cycle performance evidence

Done When

The system uses at least two distinct mechanical subsystems to transfer a 250 g payload at least 300 mm horizontally with at least 150 mm of height change, without manual payload movement, for three consecutive cycles.

Design Brief

Combined Mechanisms Design Challenge

Design and build a VEX system that uses at least two different mechanical subsystems to transfer a standardized 250 g payload from a START position to a DELIVERY position. The payload must move at least 300 mm horizontally and experience a change in height of at least 150 mm during the complete cycle.

System Requirements

What Counts as Two Mechanisms?

Mechanism 1

One subsystem may use gears, a winch, pulleys, chain and sprockets, levers, or guided linear motion.

Mechanism 2

The second subsystem must perform a different mechanical function or use a different mechanism type.

Controlled Transfer

The payload must remain controlled by the system throughout the transfer.

Clear Sequence

Your team must be able to explain what happens first, what happens second, and how the two subsystems interact.

VEX Structure

The primary structure and mechanical components must use the assigned VEX materials unless approved otherwise.

Repeatability

The final system must complete three consecutive successful transfer cycles.

Before Building

System Architecture Required

  • Draw the START and DELIVERY positions.
  • Label Mechanism 1 and Mechanism 2.
  • Show the input, motion transfer, and output for each subsystem.
  • Use arrows to show the path of the payload.
  • Identify where speed, torque, mechanical advantage, alignment, or friction may affect the design.
Design Decisions

Explain Why Your Mechanisms Fit the Mission

Subsystem Choice

Why is each selected mechanism appropriate for its specific job?

Interaction

How does the output of one subsystem connect to or support the next?

Tradeoff

What tradeoff did your team make involving speed, torque, complexity, mass, or reliability?

Performance Check

Final System Requirements

  • 250 g standardized payload
  • At least 300 mm horizontal movement
  • At least 150 mm change in height during the cycle
  • At least two distinct mechanical subsystems
  • No manual movement of the payload during the official cycle
  • Three consecutive successful cycles
