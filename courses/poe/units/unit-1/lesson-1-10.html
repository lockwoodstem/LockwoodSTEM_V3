Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can explain voltage, current, and resistance and use Ohm’s law to solve simple circuit problems.

Evidence

Ohm’s law practice and circuit notes

Success Looks Like

Ohm’s law solutions use consistent units and explain what the voltage, current, and resistance mean for a real component.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

The page highlights only the student-facing elements that make sense outside the full slide deck.

1. Identify circuit quantities

Locate the source voltage, component resistance, and current path.

2. Apply Ohm’s law

Solve for the missing variable and convert units when needed.

3. Check component limits

Compare predicted current and power with safe operating values.

Today’s Student Work

Main Deliverable

Ohm’s Law Practice and Circuit Notes

Solve a short set of rover-circuit problems and annotate one circuit diagram.

  • Show substitutions, units, and final values for voltage, current, resistance, and power.
  • Write one sentence explaining how changing resistance affects current at constant voltage.
