POE Unit 1 | Mission Brief 1.4

Power-Limited Winch Design

Design a winch that lifts the greatest useful load without exceeding a motor or power limit.

Advanced2-3 class periodsEngineering Challenge
Back to Challenge HubDownload Challenge Brief (PDF)
Engineering Design Brief

Work independently or with an approved partner.

Read the complete brief before gathering materials or beginning construction.

Background

Engineering Context

A drone recovery station has a small motor but must lift equipment safely. Your team must balance drum radius, speed, torque, and structural support.

Objectives

What You Will Practice

  • Relate torque, radius, force, speed, and power
  • Use calculations to guide design
  • Test a mechanism under load
  • Evaluate design tradeoffs
Materials

What You Need

  • Low-voltage motor or hand-crank simulation
  • Spools of different radii
  • String
  • Masses
  • Timer
  • Structural materials
  • Engineering notebook
Procedure

Student Instructions

  1. Record the available motor speed or hand-input condition.
  2. Create at least three drum-radius concepts.
  3. Predict which radius will lift fastest and which will lift the greatest load.
  4. Build a supported winch with one selected drum.
  5. Test a standard load and record lift time.
  6. Increase load in approved increments until the design reaches the limit.
  7. Calculate output work and average power for one successful lift.
  8. Revise the drum or support and repeat the test.
Requirements

Engineering Requirements

  • Lift through a fixed height
  • No uncontrolled dropping or slipping
  • Include a stable axle support and secure string attachment
  • Use at least one calculation before selecting the final drum
  • Final design must complete three repeatable lifts
Constraints

Constraints & Safety

  • Use instructor-approved voltage and masses
  • Keep hands clear of winding string
  • Do not stall a motor for extended time
  • Use a catch zone below the load
Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Concept comparison
  • Torque/radius calculations
  • Test data
  • Final winch photo or CAD
  • Design tradeoff explanation
Reflection

Reflection Questions

  1. How did drum radius affect speed and lifting force?
  2. What limited the design first: motor, structure, traction, or string?
  3. Where was power lost?
  4. What safety factor would be appropriate for real cargo?

Engineering Tip

A smaller drum increases available line force but requires more rotations to lift the same distance.

Common Mistake

Calculating torque without using consistent units is a common source of error.

Stretch Goal

Add a ratchet or brake that prevents rollback during a power loss.

Career Connection

Actuation engineers design winches, reels, and deployment systems for aircraft recovery equipment and spacecraft mechanisms.

Before You Submit
  • Notebook evidence included
  • Requirements checked
  • Testing documented
  • Reflection completed
