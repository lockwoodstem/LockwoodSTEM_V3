What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can categorize energy sources and explain how energy is stored, converted, transported, and used in an aerospace system.

Evidence

Aerospace energy source comparison and conversion diagram

Success Looks Like

The energy chain correctly identifies storage, conversion, transfer, and useful output without skipping intermediate forms.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

This page highlights the lesson elements you need for practice, evidence, and submission.

1. Identify stored energy

Determine where the system begins and what form of energy is available.

2. Trace each conversion

Follow energy through electrical, mechanical, gravitational, elastic, thermal, or other forms.

3. Evaluate the source

Compare energy density, control, safety, duration, and mission suitability.
