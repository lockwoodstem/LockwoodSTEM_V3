Lesson Interactive

Organize a Defensible Final Design Review

Reorder the sections so the presentation tells a coherent engineering story from mission need to tested conclusion.

Review Goal

The audience should be able to trace why the team chose the mechanism, how calculations informed it, what happened during prototyping, and whether the final evidence supports the claim.

Reorder the Story

  1. Present final test data and criterion results.
  2. Define the mission problem, criteria, and constraints.
  3. Explain revisions made from evidence.
  4. Show concept alternatives and the justified decision.
  5. State the final claim, limitations, and next improvement.
  6. Show prototype construction and observed issues.
  7. Present ratio, work, power, or circuit calculations.
Move the sections into an evidence-based engineering narrative.
Today’s Work

Main Deliverable

Final Design Review Presentation and Reflection

Present the rover mechanism and submit a concise engineering reflection.

  • Include requirements, concept rationale, calculations, prototype evidence, test data, and final performance claims.
  • Explain one successful decision, one limitation, and the next evidence-based revision.
