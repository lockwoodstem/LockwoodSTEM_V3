Use controlled testing, repeated trials, graphs, statistics, or kinematics to make the result measurable.

Interactive Checks

Questions to Use During the Lesson

Use the reveal boxes after students vote, discuss, or write a quick response.

Which choice best supports a mission-performance decision?

Options: one impressive trial; repeated trials with variables and observations; a guess; or an unlabeled graph.

Reveal answer

Best answer: repeated trials with variables, data, and observations. This gives the team repeatability, context, and evidence that can support a decision.

What should the engineer do when repeated trials are inconsistent?

A team gets results close to the target, but the trials vary more than expected.

Reveal answer

Review variables and measurement setup, collect notes, and revise the test or system. Inconsistency is evidence that something needs investigation.
