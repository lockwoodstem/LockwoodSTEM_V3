What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can calculate acceleration and connect it to the shapes and slopes of motion graphs.

Evidence

Success Looks Like

Acceleration uses change in velocity over time, and graph interpretations match the direction and sign of motion.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the data, motion, or testing reasoning required for today’s deliverable.

1. Calculate acceleration

Use initial velocity, final velocity, and elapsed time.

2. Read the velocity graph

Interpret slope as acceleration.

3. Connect to position

Explain how changing velocity changes the slope of position-time.
