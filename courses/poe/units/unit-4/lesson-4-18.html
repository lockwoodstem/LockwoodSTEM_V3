Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can synthesize investigation evidence into a qualified design recommendation.

Evidence

Final mission-performance review

Success Looks Like

The review answers the mission question, uses correct analysis, compares results with criteria, and states limitations.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the data, motion, or testing reasoning required for today’s deliverable.

1. Select the evidence

Use the most relevant table, graph, calculations, and observations.

2. Evaluate performance

Compare accuracy, consistency, reliability, and criteria.

3. Defend the recommendation

State what should be used or revised and acknowledge limitations.

Lesson Interactive

Build the Final Performance Argument

Apply the lesson concept to a mission-performance scenario. Use the feedback to correct your reasoning before completing the main deliverable.

Final Design Comparison

Design A: 8/10 successes, mean range 4.96 m, standard deviation 0.18 m.
Design B: 9/10 successes, mean range 5.30 m, standard deviation 0.62 m.

Criterion: Land at 5.0 ± 0.3 m with consistent performance.

Order the Evidence Story

  1. Calculate and interpret the relevant statistics.
  2. State the design recommendation.
  3. Restate the mission question and criteria.
  4. Acknowledge a limitation and next test.
  5. Use the graph to identify the performance pattern.
  6. Summarize variables, controls, and test method.
  7. Present the repeated-trial data.
Build the argument from question and method through evidence, analysis, recommendation, and limitation.
