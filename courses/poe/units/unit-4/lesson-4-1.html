Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can distinguish weak observations from repeatable evidence in an aerospace performance investigation.

Evidence

Mission performance evidence map

Success Looks Like

The evidence chain uses repeated controlled trials, readable data displays, and a claim limited to the results.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the data, motion, or testing reasoning required for today’s deliverable.

1. Compare evidence

Rank one trial, repeated trials, and controlled repeated trials by decision value.

2. Trace the argument

Connect measurement, graph, statistic, and claim.

3. Define readiness

State what evidence would justify continuing, revising, or retesting.

Lesson Interactive

Build a Defensible Evidence Chain

Apply the lesson concept to a mission-performance scenario. Use the feedback to correct your reasoning before completing the main deliverable.

Mission Claim

“The rover can reliably stop inside the landing zone.” Select the evidence features needed before the team can defend that claim.

Reminder: A dramatic single success may be useful for troubleshooting, but it is not reliability evidence.
Choose the combination that supports a repeatable, readable, and appropriately limited claim.
