Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can identify measurement offset, apply a correction, and decide whether equipment is ready for testing.

Evidence

Setup evidence and calibration record

Success Looks Like

The team documents a standard check, calculates offset correctly, and resolves calibration problems before official data collection.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the data, motion, or testing reasoning required for today’s deliverable.

1. Inspect the system

Check structure, alignment, fasteners, and test area.

2. Check standards

Compare each measuring tool with a known reference.

3. Document readiness

Record offsets, corrections, and any repair or recalibration.
