POE Unit 0

Engineering Readiness & Aerospace Systems Foundations

Unit mission: Establish the safety, documentation, Fusion, VEX, and systems-thinking practices required for POE project work.

Lesson MapBack to POE Hub
Unit Focus

Prepare to document evidence, use tools safely, model intentionally, and test connected systems.

PreparePracticeProve

Every lesson produces a readiness artifact or certification evidence that supports later POE work.

Unit Overview

What Unit 0 Builds

Twelve focused lessons prepare you for mechanisms, structures, controls, data investigations, and the final capstone.

Safety + Documentation

You will practice hazard control, certification workflows, and engineering records that another person can reproduce.

Fusion + Systems

Two Fusion refreshers reinforce stable sketches, components, assemblies, and manufacturing exports. Systems thinking connects hardware, software, energy, and feedback.

Readiness Challenge

A short plan-build-test-reflect challenge verifies that you can work safely and defend a decision with evidence.
