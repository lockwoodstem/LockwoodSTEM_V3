POE Unit 0

Engineering Readiness & Aerospace Systems Foundations

Unit mission: Build the safety, documentation, CAD, VEX, and systems-thinking habits needed for Principles of Engineering project work.

Lesson MapResourcesBack to POE Hub
Unit Focus

Prepare to work safely, document evidence, use core tools, and think in connected aerospace systems.

PrepareModelTest

Every lesson builds readiness for later mechanisms, structures, robotics, data, and capstone work.

Unit Overview

What Unit 0 Builds

Students establish safe routines, notebook documentation habits, Fusion 360 refreshers, VEX/rover readiness, and a short aerospace readiness mini-challenge.

Safety + Tools

PPE, FabLab expectations, hand tools, laser cutting, and 3D printing certification routines prepare students for controlled build work.

CAD + Systems

Fusion 360 refreshers and systems-thinking activities connect digital models to aerospace design decisions.

Mini-Challenge

Students plan, build, test, and reflect on a small aerospace support system using criteria, constraints, and evidence.
