What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

Evidence

PPE and FabLab safety certification evidence

Success Looks Like

Every scenario response controls the hazard before anyone continues using tools or equipment.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

1. Identify common FabLab hazards

Establish the engineering purpose and vocabulary.

2. Choose the safest immediate response

Apply the idea to a specific readiness decision.

3. Complete the required safety certification pathway

Produce evidence that can be checked or used later.
