POE Unit 0 | Mission Brief 0.2

Precision Measurement Verification

Measure a set of parts, compare repeated readings, and decide whether the measurements are trustworthy.

Intermediate1 class periodSkill Builder
Back to Challenge HubDownload Challenge Brief (PDF)
Engineering Design Brief

Work independently or with an approved partner.

Read the complete brief before gathering materials or beginning construction.

Background

Engineering Context

Replacement rover components arrived without complete inspection records. Your team must verify critical dimensions before the parts are released for assembly.

Objectives

What You Will Practice

  • Use rulers and calipers correctly
  • Evaluate repeatability and measurement variation
  • Record measurements with appropriate precision
  • Make an evidence-based accept/recheck decision
Materials

What You Need

  • Three to five instructor-selected parts
  • Metric ruler
  • Digital or dial caliper
  • Measurement data sheet
  • Engineering notebook
Procedure

Student Instructions

  1. Identify the dimensions that are most important to fit or assembly.
  2. Measure each critical dimension three times without looking at previous results.
  3. Have a partner independently measure the same dimensions.
  4. Calculate the range for each set of measurements.
  5. Compare your readings with the partner readings and any reference dimension provided.
  6. Mark each dimension Accept, Recheck, or Reject and explain why.
  7. Write one improvement to the measurement procedure.
Requirements

Engineering Requirements

  • Record units and instrument used for every dimension
  • Use three repeated measurements per dimension
  • Measurements must be readable and traceable to a labeled feature
  • Decisions must cite numerical evidence
Constraints

Constraints & Safety

  • Do not force caliper jaws onto a part
  • Use only approved parts
  • Do not treat instrument display resolution as guaranteed accuracy
Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Labeled part sketch
  • Measurement table
  • Range calculations
  • Accept/recheck/reject decisions
  • Procedure improvement note
Reflection

Reflection Questions

  1. What caused the largest measurement variation?
  2. Which instrument was most appropriate for each feature?
  3. How would temperature or surface damage affect results?
  4. What measurement would you verify first before assembly?
Download PDF
Engineering Tip

Use the same contact points and instrument orientation each time you repeat a measurement.

Common Mistake

Changing where you measure a feature can create apparent variation that is actually a setup difference.

Stretch Goal

Design a simple go/no-go gauge concept for one critical dimension.

Career Connection

Quality engineers and metrology technicians verify incoming components before they enter aircraft or spacecraft assemblies.

Before You Submit
  • Notebook evidence included
  • Requirements checked
  • Testing documented
  • Reflection completed
