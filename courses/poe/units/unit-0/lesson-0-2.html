Objective

Work toward the lesson goal: Engineering Notebook & Documentation Refresher. Use the directions and success criteria in the activity below.

Materials

For the online work: a browser and your engineering notebook. Follow the activity’s materials list for any physical build, and get teacher approval before using equipment.

Plan your time

Suggested work block: 45–60 minutes. Physical builds and projects may span several classes; follow your teacher’s schedule.

Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can organize a notebook entry so another engineer can reproduce the work and understand the conclusion.

Evidence

Complete engineering notebook setup entry

Success Looks Like

The entry has identifying information, labeled visuals, measurements with units, and an evidence-based conclusion.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

1. Audit an incomplete notebook entry

Establish the engineering purpose and vocabulary.

2. Repair missing documentation elements

Apply the idea to a specific readiness decision.

3. Apply the standard to today’s entry

Produce evidence that can be checked or used later.
