Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can complete a rover preflight covering power, structure, drivetrain, software, and the test environment.

Evidence

VEX or rover readiness checklist

Success Looks Like

The robot and the test zone are verified before motion, and faults are corrected one subsystem at a time.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

1. Identify rover subsystems and hazards

Establish the engineering purpose and vocabulary.

2. Complete a preflight inspection

Apply the idea to a specific readiness decision.

3. Practice controlled first-motion testing

Produce evidence that can be checked or used later.
