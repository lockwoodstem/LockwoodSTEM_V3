POE Unit 5 | Mission Brief 5.2

Rover Design Trade Study

Compare three rover concepts using mission-weighted criteria and recommend one for detailed design.

Advanced2 class periodsEngineering Challenge
Back to Challenge HubDownload Challenge Brief (PDF)
Engineering Design Brief

Work independently or with an approved partner.

Read the complete brief before gathering materials or beginning construction.

Background

Engineering Context

The project team has several promising rover concepts but limited time and budget. A structured trade study is required before committing to hardware.

Objectives

What You Will Practice

  • Select decision criteria
  • Assign defensible weights
  • Score concepts consistently
  • Perform sensitivity analysis
Materials

What You Need

  • Three concept descriptions or team sketches
  • Decision matrix template
  • Mission requirements
  • Calculator or spreadsheet
  • Engineering notebook
Procedure

Student Instructions

  1. Review the mission requirements and identify six to eight decision criteria.
  2. Assign weights totaling 100%.
  3. Define a 1-5 scoring scale for every criterion before scoring concepts.
  4. Score all three concepts and document evidence.
  5. Calculate weighted totals.
  6. Change the two largest weights by plus/minus 10 percentage points and recalculate.
  7. Identify whether the winner changes.
  8. Recommend a concept and state the largest unresolved risk.
Requirements

Engineering Requirements

  • At least three concepts and six criteria
  • Weights total 100%
  • Scoring definitions written before final scoring
  • All scores supported by evidence or assumptions
  • Include one sensitivity analysis
Constraints

Constraints & Safety

  • Do not change criteria solely to make a preferred concept win
  • Label uncertain assumptions
  • Use the same scoring standard for all concepts
Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Criteria and weights
  • Scoring definitions
  • Completed trade matrix
  • Sensitivity comparison
  • Recommendation memo
Reflection

Reflection Questions

  1. Which criterion drove the decision most strongly?
  2. Did sensitivity analysis change confidence in the winner?
  3. What missing test data would reduce uncertainty?
  4. Where might personal bias have affected scoring?
Download PDF
Engineering Tip

Write scoring anchors such as “5 = meets requirement with at least 20% margin.”

Common Mistake

Assigning numbers without defined scoring rules creates a false appearance of precision.

Stretch Goal

Add cost and schedule uncertainty using best-case and worst-case scores.

Career Connection

Systems engineering teams use formal trade studies to select architectures, sensors, propulsion, and mission concepts.

Before You Submit
  • Notebook evidence included
  • Requirements checked
  • Testing documented
  • Reflection completed
