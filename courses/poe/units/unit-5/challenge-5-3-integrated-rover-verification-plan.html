POE Unit 5 | Mission Brief 5.3

Integrated Rover Verification Plan

Create a verification plan that proves the rover meets its mission requirements without unnecessary testing.

Advanced2 class periodsSkill Builder
Back to Challenge HubDownload Challenge Brief (PDF)
Engineering Design Brief

Work independently or with an approved partner.

Read the complete brief before gathering materials or beginning construction.

Background

Engineering Context

Before final demonstration, the project manager needs a coordinated plan showing how each rover requirement will be inspected, analyzed, demonstrated, or tested.

Objectives

What You Will Practice

  • Map requirements to verification
  • Sequence tests safely
  • Define objective pass/fail criteria
  • Plan data collection
Materials

What You Need

  • Final requirement list
  • Rover design information
  • Verification matrix template
  • Engineering notebook
Procedure

Student Instructions

  1. Number every mission requirement.
  2. Choose inspection, analysis, demonstration, or test for each.
  3. Write an objective pass/fail statement.
  4. Identify equipment, setup, data, and responsible team role.
  5. Arrange verification activities from low risk to high risk.
  6. Identify requirements that can be verified in one combined test.
  7. Create a retest rule for failed or inconclusive results.
  8. Conduct a tabletop review with another team.
Requirements

Engineering Requirements

  • Every requirement has one or more verification methods
  • Every method has a measurable pass/fail criterion
  • Plan includes setup, equipment, and data to record
  • Safety checks precede powered testing
  • Retest criteria are defined
Constraints

Constraints & Safety

  • Do not schedule destructive tests before non-destructive evidence is collected
  • Avoid using demonstration when quantitative data are required
  • Identify assumptions and unavailable equipment
Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Verification matrix
  • Test sequence
  • Data-recording plan
  • Peer-review corrections
  • Final readiness statement
Reflection

Reflection Questions

  1. Which requirement was most difficult to verify?
  2. What tests could be combined without confusing results?
  3. How will you handle an inconclusive result?
  4. What requirement creates the greatest test risk?
Download PDF
Engineering Tip

Test one requirement under one clearly controlled condition unless a combined test is deliberately planned.

Common Mistake

“It worked” is not a pass/fail criterion. State the measured threshold and condition.

Stretch Goal

Create a verification dashboard that automatically marks requirements complete.

Career Connection

Verification and validation engineers plan evidence that proves aerospace systems meet requirements before delivery.

Before You Submit
  • Notebook evidence included
  • Requirements checked
  • Testing documented
  • Reflection completed
