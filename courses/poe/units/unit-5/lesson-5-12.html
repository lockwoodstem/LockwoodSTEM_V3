Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can define variables, trials, measurement methods, stop conditions, and risk controls for capstone testing.

Evidence

Approved capstone test protocol and risk review

Success Looks Like

The protocol changes one planned factor, records measurable outcomes, repeats trials, and controls significant hazards.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step produces a decision, artifact, or test result needed for the capstone.

1. Define evidence needs

Connect each test to a requirement and success criterion.

2. Design repeatable trials

Specify variables, controls, instruments, units, and trial count.

3. Review risk

Identify hazards, controls, stop criteria, and responsibilities.
