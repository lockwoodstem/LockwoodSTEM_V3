Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can defend claims with multiple evidence types, answer questions, acknowledge limitations, and reflect on growth.

Evidence

Capstone design review and portfolio reflection

Success Looks Like

The review uses qualified claims, traceable evidence, clear limitations, specific next steps, and reflection across multiple skills.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step produces a decision, artifact, or test result needed for the capstone.

1. Present and demonstrate

Communicate the solution and evidence to the review audience.

2. Respond professionally

Use requirements, data, and documentation to answer questions.

3. Reflect and transfer

Identify growth, remaining gaps, and lessons for future engineering work.

Today’s Work

Main Deliverable

Capstone design review and portfolio reflection

Create one focused engineering artifact that demonstrates today’s capstone progress.

  • Complete the final design review with the required demonstration and evidence package.
  • Submit a portfolio reflection addressing technical growth, teamwork, evidence use, and a specific next learning goal.
