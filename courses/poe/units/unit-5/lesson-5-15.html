Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can compare before-and-after data, verify requirements, and justify either another iteration or a design freeze.

Evidence

Iteration comparison and design-freeze decision

Success Looks Like

The decision references measurable improvement, unresolved risk, schedule impact, and verification status.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step produces a decision, artifact, or test result needed for the capstone.

1. Select a targeted revision

Change the cause of the highest-priority performance gap.

2. Retest consistently

Use the same metric and conditions for a fair comparison.

3. Conduct the freeze review

Confirm requirements, risks, interfaces, and documentation status.

Lesson Interactive

Decide Whether to Freeze the Design

Apply the lesson concept to a capstone aerospace-systems scenario. Use the feedback to correct your reasoning before completing the main deliverable.

Iteration Evidence

Baseline success rate: 72%. Revised success rate: 92%. Required success rate: at least 90%. All critical safety and interface checks now pass.

Compare both versions to the requirement and account for unresolved critical risk before freezing.
Today’s Work

Main Deliverable

Iteration comparison and design-freeze decision

Create one focused engineering artifact that demonstrates today’s capstone progress.

  • Compare baseline and revised performance using the same metric.
  • Complete a design-freeze decision that identifies any deferred issues and their consequences.
