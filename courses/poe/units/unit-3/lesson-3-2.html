01 / Mechanical Systems

How Rover Motion Happens

Students work with real VEX V5 parts to discover how a motor's rotation reaches the wheels.

From electrical energy to ground motion

The V5 battery supplies electrical energy through the control system. The motor creates shaft rotation. Gears can transmit torque and change rotational speed, while supported shafts carry rotation to a wheel. Traction between the wheel and floor allows the rover to move.

POWERBattery + Brain
ACTUATEMotor
TRANSMITGears + shafts
MOVEWheel + ground

Important: The Brain and battery control and supply the motor. They are not mechanical shafts in the gear train.

V5 Smart Motor

Produces controlled rotation. Its output drives a shaft or gear through a compatible connection.

Gears

Two properly meshed gears transmit rotation. Their tooth counts affect rotational speed and torque; externally meshed gears rotate in opposite directions.

Shafts & bearings

Shafts transmit rotational motion. Bearings support shaft alignment and help components rotate without binding.

Wheels & tread

Wheel diameter affects distance traveled per rotation. Surface grip affects how much force can be transferred before slipping.

Collars & spacers

Keep gears, wheels and shafts positioned so they do not rub on neighboring parts or detach.

Connection to the rover: Lesson 3.3 begins building the standardized Clawbot chassis. Students may later change wheel type and drivetrain gearing, but do not assemble the arm and claw before Lesson 3.12.

02 / Classroom Sequence

45-Minute Lesson Plan

5 min — Connect

Review the five robot subsystems from 3.1 and identify the drivetrain parts that transmit motion.

10 min — Identify

Examine instructor-provided VEX motor, gears, shafts, bearings, wheel and retaining parts. Explain their individual functions.

20 min — Investigate

Construct a temporary, unpowered, supported gear-and-shaft demonstration. Turn the input by hand, observe output movement, record gear teeth and diagnose any binding.

5 min — Analyze

Document the power-to-motion path and what gear engagement and bearing alignment accomplish.

5 min — Export

Complete the troubleshooting analysis and export your individual 20-point investigation as a PDF.

Gear ratio helper

Driven ÷ driving teeth = Enter both tooth counts

For a simple pair of externally meshed gears: output rotations per input rotation = input teeth ÷ output teeth. This is a prediction, not a measured result.

Part D — Mechanical Troubleshooting

4 points

Scenario: The input shaft turns, but the output gear occasionally skips teeth and the wheel shaft rubs against the frame. Diagnose the issue and propose a safe correction.
