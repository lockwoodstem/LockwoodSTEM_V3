01 / Goals

Today's Engineering Goal

Learning Target

I can sort vex gears, shafts, bearings, wheels and motors by function and document the resulting engineering evidence.

Materials

VEX V5 Clawbot system and parts for this stage; VEXcode V5 Python; engineering notebook; teacher-approved testing area and measurement equipment as needed.

Safety & Constraints

Use only the standard Clawbot base chassis with permitted wheel and drivetrain gear-ratio changes; do not install the arm or claw, add ballast or increase motor count. Stop power before adjusting the drivetrain.

02 / Engineering Work

Build, Code & Test

Follow the sequence and collect evidence while you work.

Procedure

  1. 1. Sort VEX gears, shafts, bearings, wheels and motors by function.
  2. 2. Trace motor-to-wheel power path.
  3. 3. Sketch the drivetrain and note alignment risks.

Engineering notebook: Record the actual configuration, decisions, observations and measurements. Save program revisions before changing working code.

03 / Verify

Check Your Rover

Function

Demonstrate the capability described in today's procedure. If it fails, isolate a variable and retest.

Engineering Reasoning

Explain the mechanical or programming decision you made. Compare your prediction to observed performance using a measured result or specific behavior.

Next Improvement

Identify one realistic change that could improve reliability without violating the build requirements.

04 / Deliverable

What to Submit

Lesson 3.2 Evidence

Labeled drivetrain diagram; mechanical parts/function table.

Include team members, class period, lesson number and clearly labeled photos, code, calculations or data relevant to this assignment. Submit through Google Classroom as directed.
