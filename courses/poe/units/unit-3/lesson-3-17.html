Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the control-systems reasoning needed for today’s deliverable.

1. Run controlled trials

Hold conditions constant and record mission events.

2. Analyze performance

Calculate success rate, timing, error, and repeatability.

3. Revise one cause

Choose a change linked to the strongest evidence and retest.
