01 / Prepare

Chassis Build Briefing

5 min • Safety5 min • Parts25 min • Build5 min • Inspect5 min • Log

Today's learning target

I can assemble the structural Clawbot base with accurate alignment, inspect its joints and document a repeatable construction checkpoint.

Materials: standard VEX V5 Clawbot kit, supplied V5 Speed Build chassis instructions, approved VEX tools, square or ruler, engineering notebook, and a camera-enabled device.

Assembly constraints

Use the standard Clawbot chassis geometry. Do not redesign the frame or install an arm or claw. Wheels and gear ratios may be changed later, but this lesson focuses on structure.

Power: Keep batteries disconnected. Mechanical parts are inspected by hand only.

Build checkpoint: The goal is a correctly assembled base frame—not a powered, driving robot. Chassis motor and wheel assembly is the focus of Lesson 3.4. If the official build instructions use slightly different steps, follow the instructor-approved kit revision.

02 / Technical Concepts

What Makes a Reliable Chassis?

Symmetry

Matching left and right structure helps wheel locations and drivetrain loads stay consistent.

Squareness

A distorted rectangle may cause shaft misalignment and make the rover drift or bind.

Fastener integrity

Loose joints allow motion between parts; excessive tightening can damage hardware.

Serviceability

A well-organized chassis makes it easier to route wires and later remove or adjust drivetrain parts.
