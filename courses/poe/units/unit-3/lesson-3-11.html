Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can compare pneumatic and hydraulic systems using medium, compressibility, force, control, and risk.

Evidence

Fluid-power comparison

Success Looks Like

The comparison selects the appropriate system for a task and identifies the major safety concern.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the control-systems reasoning needed for today’s deliverable.

1. Compare the media

Relate gas compressibility and liquid incompressibility to behavior.

2. Analyze the task

Consider force, speed, precision, mass, leakage, and environment.

3. Manage risk

Identify pressure, hose, and stored-energy hazards.

Fluid-System Characteristics

Choose for the Mission

Compressibility affects stiffness and control; both systems require pressure-rated components and safe energy isolation.

  • Recommend one system for a ground-support task and defend the choice.
