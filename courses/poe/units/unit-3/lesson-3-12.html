Pressure, Force, and Pascal’s Law

Focus question: How do pressure and piston area determine the force available from a fluid-power actuator?

Pascal’s Law

Pressure transmitted through a confined fluid acts over piston area to create force.

Verify the Calculation

Area grows with diameter squared, so diameter changes force more strongly than a simple one-to-one ratio.
