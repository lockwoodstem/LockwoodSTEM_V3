POE Unit 3 • Lesson 3.14

Qualification 2: Sample Retrieval

Engineering question: Can our rover safely retrieve and deliver three samples under driver control?

Lesson PlanInteractive AssignmentUnit 3 Overview
Practical Qualification • 30 Points

Demonstrate accurate driving, gripping, lifting, transporting and releasing three simulated samples.

PrepareTestVerify

The instructor verifies actual sample-handling performance. A PDF alone does not establish qualification.

01 / Learning & Preparation

Three-Sample Retrieval Qualification

5 min — Briefing and safety5 min — Course orientation25 min — Three sample trials5 min — Analyze results5 min — PDF export

Standardized sample field

The instructor marks pickup locations, a delivery zone and a defined start position; all teams use the same course and sample types.

Three sample attempts

For each numbered sample, approach, grip, lift, carry to the delivery zone and release. Record success and actual time.

Safety and retests

Instructor verifies secure arm/claw, neutral-button stopping, safe limits and clear field before attempts. Students may retest with approval.

Scoring is performance-based

Successful retrieval and controlled release matter more than speed. Instructor verifies the physical demonstration; the PDF is documentation.

Safety: Inspect the arm, claw and drivetrain; verify neutral-button stopping and safe travel limits. Only operate on an approved, clear field under instructor supervision. Stop if a sample jams, a motor stalls or the robot behaves unexpectedly.
