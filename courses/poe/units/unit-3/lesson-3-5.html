Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can predict open-loop rover motion and explain the limitations of time-based commands.

Evidence

Timed-motion prediction

Success Looks Like

The prediction uses consistent units and identifies why wheel slip or battery change can cause error.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the control-systems reasoning needed for today’s deliverable.

1. Calibrate motion

Measure distance traveled for a known speed and time.

2. Predict a command

Use the calibration to select a travel time.

3. Evaluate error

Compare predicted and actual motion and identify disturbance sources.
