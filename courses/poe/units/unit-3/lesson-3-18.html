Engineering question: Is the complete Clawbot ready to accept sensors and advanced control in Unit 4?

Rover Qualification

Final Rover Qualification Challenge

PlanTestImprove

Use the same robot in Unit 4, when sensors and feedback are added.

01 / Prepare

Goal & Materials

I can qualify the rover through manual precision driving, sample retrieval and basic autonomous movement.

Materials: Completed VEX V5 Clawbot with working arm and claw, VEXcode V5 Python, engineering notebook, marked challenge field and measurement tools.

Previous LessonUnit 3 Overview
02 / Activity

Build, Code & Test

  1. 1. Pass manual precision-driving station.
  2. 2. Collect and deliver a sample using the claw.
  3. 3. Complete a short autonomous movement sequence.
  4. 4. Repeat critical trials.
  5. 5. Defend engineering changes and readiness for Unit 4 sensors.

Safety: Run manual and autonomous trials in supervised stations; keep the field clear and verify the stop procedure.

03 / Verify

Check Your Engineering

Function

Demonstrate the required new capability without assistance during official trials.

Engineering Check

What new sensor could improve a specific error you observed during qualification?

Improvement

Identify or test a change and support the choice with observations or measurements.

04 / Deliverable

What to Submit

Lesson 3.18 Evidence

Submit integrated rover qualification record, working Python code, measured test data and engineering design review (50 points).

Submit code, data, sketches and relevant labeled photographs through Google Classroom. Include team names and class period.

Assessment: Technical functionality, repeatable performance and justified improvements matter more than speed or winning.
