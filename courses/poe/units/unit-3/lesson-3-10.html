Qualification 1: Precision Driving

Engineering question: How consistently can a driver guide a rover through a controlled course and stop near a target?

01 / Instructor-Supervised Qualification

Precision Driving Course

The instructor sets one marked, repeatable course for all students, with the same start, turning markers and target stopping line.

4 / STOPStop with the front reference edge near the target line.

Course standardization

The instructor marks the start, route, obstacles, finish line and travel direction. All students use the same layout, unless an approved accommodation requires a modification. Record any course modifications.

Stopping error: measure the signed distance from the front reference edge of the rover to the target line in centimeters. An overshoot is positive; a short stop is negative.

Qualification procedure

Complete three documented driving trials. Record trial time, stopping error, contacts with markers and whether the prescribed route was completed. Repeat attempts after instructor feedback when time and safety allow.

Accuracy is based on absolute stopping error; shorter times are secondary and do not outweigh safe control or precision.

Required equipment

Standard Clawbot drivetrain, working VEX V5 controller program, tape or cones, a ruler or measuring tape, timer, level floor and instructor-approved test area.

Safety and retests

Inspect the robot, verify a safe neutral-stick stop and keep bystanders behind the course perimeter. Stop the trial if the robot behaves unexpectedly. Students may revise control settings and retest with instructor approval; the best verified performance may count.

Assessment principle: The instructor awards the qualification grade from observed performance and the student's accurate records. The website calculates summary statistics, but it does not independently certify driving proficiency.
