Aerospace Material Families and Selection

Focus question: How do engineers select materials when no single material is best in every category?

Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can compare material families using weighted criteria tied to an aerospace structural need.

Evidence

Material trade study

Success Looks Like

Criteria weights reflect the mission, scores are evidence-based, and the selected material matches the calculated result.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Define the mission priorities

Choose the properties and constraints that matter for the component.

2. Compare material families

Score metals, polymers, composites, or woods using consistent evidence.

3. Defend the selection

Use the weighted result and acknowledge one limitation.

Today’s Work

Main Deliverable

Aerospace Material Trade Study

Select a material family for one payload-support component.

  • Use at least three weighted criteria and evidence-based scores for three material options.
  • Identify the selected material and one tradeoff or limitation that remains.
