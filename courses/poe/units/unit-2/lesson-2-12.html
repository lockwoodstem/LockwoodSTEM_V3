POE Unit 2 • Materials & Testing • Lesson 2.12

Structural Failure

Engineering question: What causes real structures and members to stop working?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Phase 2: Materials knowledge

Build evidence through experiments

Learn properties and test methods using varied structures before beginning bridge fabrication.

Learning Targets

  • Distinguish tension, compression, shear, bending and torsion
  • Identify buckling, cracking, yielding and joint failure
  • Separate observed evidence from inferred failure cause

Learn the Concept

  • An applied action is not the same thing as a failure mechanism
  • Slender members can buckle in compression before crushing
  • Fracture and joint separation require different redesign strategies

Practice Together

Diagnose a buckled chair leg, a bending shelf and a separated glued corner using observation/evidence.

Show your reasoning, annotate diagrams and use correct units.

Apply the Skill

Analyze three unrelated structural failures and recommend a specific, physically justified improvement.

Check your understanding: Why does a broken joint not necessarily mean the member material is weak?

Student Evidence

Failure analysis case study. Provide clearly identified assumptions, measurements and conclusions.
