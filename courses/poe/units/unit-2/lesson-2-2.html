Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can classify structural loads and predict the deformation or failure they may cause.

Evidence

Load-type analysis

Success Looks Like

Each scenario is classified by the dominant load and connected to a reasonable deformation or failure concern.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Observe the loading

Identify where forces enter and leave the structure.

2. Classify the effect

Distinguish axial, transverse, rotational, and twisting behavior.

3. Predict the risk

Connect the load to a likely deformation or failure mode.
