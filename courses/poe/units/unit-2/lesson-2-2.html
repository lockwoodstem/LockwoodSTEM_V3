POE Unit 2 • Mechanics Foundations • Lesson 2.2

Centroids & Center of Gravity

Engineering question: How can a designer locate the geometric center of a shape?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Foundational instruction

Learn it before the bridge project

Use varied engineering examples to master the mechanics, then apply the technique to your final structural design later in the unit.

Learning Targets

  • Locate centroids of rectangles and triangles
  • Find a composite centroid using weighted areas
  • Explain how the centroid affects a supported object

Learn the Concept

  • Rectangle: x̄ = b/2, ȳ = h/2
  • Triangle: centroid lies 1/3 of the height from its base
  • Composite: x̄ = Σ(Aᵢxᵢ)/ΣAᵢ and ȳ = Σ(Aᵢyᵢ)/ΣAᵢ

During instruction: Sketch the forces, geometry, variables or joints discussed and record units for every quantity.

Practice Together

Find the centroid of a 100 mm × 40 mm rectangular plate. Then describe what happens if an extra 20 mm × 40 mm plate is attached to its right edge.

Show: a labeled diagram, a mathematical model when relevant, and a final statement interpreting the result.

Apply the Skill

Sketch a composite L-shape as two rectangles. Label dimensions, areas, individual centroid coordinates and compute its combined centroid.

Check your understanding: Why is the centroid of an uneven shape not necessarily in the middle of its bounding box?

Student Evidence

Centroid calculations and clearly labeled sketch. Use your engineering notebook or teacher-provided guided notes; include dimensions, units and a conclusion.

Looking ahead: These mechanics techniques will be revisited when students analyze the bridge design in Lessons 2.15–2.20.
