Centroids and Structural Cross-Sections

Focus question: How does the location of area affect balance, bending analysis, and structural design?

Structures Mission

Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can calculate the centroid of a composite cross-section using component areas and centroid locations.

Evidence

Composite-area centroid calculation

Success Looks Like

The section is divided without overlap, component areas are correct, and the weighted averages locate the centroid within the shape.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Divide the section

Break the cross-section into non-overlapping simple shapes.

2. Create the area table

Record each area and its centroid coordinates from one datum.

3. Calculate and check

Find the weighted average and confirm the result lies within the section.

Composite Centroid Analysis
