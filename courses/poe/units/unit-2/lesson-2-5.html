Learn the Concept

What You Need to Know

Use these ideas as your lesson notes. Focus on explaining the behavior, not memorizing vocabulary by itself.

Moment

A moment measures the turning effect of a force about a point. It increases when either the force increases or the perpendicular distance increases.

Moment arm

Use the shortest perpendicular distance from the reference point to the force's line of action—not simply any visible distance on the drawing.

Static equilibrium

A structure that is not accelerating must satisfy both force balance and moment balance. In 2D, engineers commonly use ΣFₓ = 0, ΣFᵧ = 0, and ΣM = 0.

Wing-root consequence

Every outboard load creates a moment about a support. The root connection and primary spar must transfer that moment into the fixture/fuselage.

Worked example: required wing load

The challenge applies 100 N at 200 mm from a support. Convert 200 mm to 0.200 m, then calculate M = 100 N × 0.200 m = 20.0 N·m. If the same 100 N load were only 50 mm from a support, the moment would be 1.0 N·m. Moving the load five times farther out creates five times a support moment.
