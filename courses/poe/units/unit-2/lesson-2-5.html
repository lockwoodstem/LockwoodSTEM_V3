Moments, Torque, and Structural Rotation

Focus question: How do force magnitude and distance from a pivot determine rotational effect?

Structures Mission

A force creates a moment according to its perpendicular distance from the pivot, and opposing moments determine rotational balance.

Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can calculate moments, apply direction signs, and determine the net rotational effect.

Evidence

Moment balance calculation

Success Looks Like

Each moment includes force, perpendicular distance, units, and clockwise or counterclockwise direction.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Locate the pivot

Identify the point about which rotation is evaluated.

2. Calculate each moment

Multiply force by perpendicular moment arm and apply direction.

3. Determine the net effect

Add signed moments and predict rotation or balance.

Main Deliverable

Structural Moment Analysis

Analyze the moments acting on one bracket, beam, or lever.

  • Show the pivot, force locations, perpendicular distances, and moment directions.
  • Calculate the net moment and state whether the structure rotates or balances.
