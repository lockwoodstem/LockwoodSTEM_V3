Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can evaluate material-test data quality and calculate a defensible summary result.

Evidence

Materials lab data record

Success Looks Like

The record includes controlled conditions, repeated measurements, units, anomaly notes, and an appropriate summary statistic.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Control the procedure

Keep specimen geometry, loading rate, and measurement method consistent.

2. Record complete data

Capture raw results, units, observations, and trial conditions.

3. Evaluate reliability

Calculate summaries, identify anomalies, and decide whether retesting is needed.

Today’s Student Work

Main Deliverable

Materials Test Data Record

Complete one organized data record for the assigned material test.

  • Include raw trial data, units, controlled variables, and observations for each specimen.
  • Calculate the required summary and document any anomaly or retest decision.
