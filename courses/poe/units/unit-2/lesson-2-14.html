POE Unit 2 • Materials & Testing • Lesson 2.14

Structural Efficiency

Engineering question: How do engineers compare load capacity while accounting for weight?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Phase 2: Materials knowledge

Build evidence through experiments

Learn properties and test methods using varied structures before beginning bridge fabrication.

Learning Targets

  • Calculate density and component mass
  • Compute supported load divided by structural mass
  • Identify the limits of a one-number efficiency comparison

Learn the Concept

  • Mass estimate = density × volume for uniform material
  • Structural efficiency = measured supported load (N) / completed mass (g)
  • A design must meet mandatory safety and deflection criteria before efficiency rankings matter

Practice Together

Compare designs rated 120 N at 100 g and 150 N at 140 g; calculate and explain their load-to-mass efficiencies.

Show your reasoning, annotate diagrams and use correct units.

Apply the Skill

Prepare an initial mass budget for a generic truss, including adhesives, fittings and fasteners.

Check your understanding: Why does high N/g not compensate for exceeding a deflection limit?

Student Evidence

Structural efficiency calculation and mass budget. Provide clearly identified assumptions, measurements and conclusions.
