Structural Materials & Properties

Engineering question: How do engineers select a material for a load-bearing job?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Phase 2: Materials knowledge

Build evidence through experiments

Learn properties and test methods using varied structures before beginning bridge fabrication.

Learning Targets

  • Distinguish strength, stiffness, toughness and density
  • Compare grain direction and material variability
  • Match materials to specific functional needs

Learn the Concept

  • Stiffness is resistance to deflection; strength is resistance to failure
  • Density helps estimate mass; low mass alone is not evidence of safety
  • Manufacturing process and connections affect usable material performance

Practice Together

Compare a wood strip, plywood sample and polymer sample for stiffness and mass without assuming their properties are identical.

Show your reasoning, annotate diagrams and use correct units.

Apply the Skill

Complete a selection matrix for a shelf, a support post and a gusset plate. Justify your rankings.

Check your understanding: Why might the lightest material be a poor choice for a compression member?

Student Evidence

Material property comparisons. Provide clearly identified assumptions, measurements and conclusions.
