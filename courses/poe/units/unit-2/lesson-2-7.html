Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Identify joints and members

Treat connections as joints and members as two-force elements.

2. Trace the load

Follow the applied force through the triangular geometry to the supports.

3. Classify members

Determine which members pull in tension and which push in compression.
