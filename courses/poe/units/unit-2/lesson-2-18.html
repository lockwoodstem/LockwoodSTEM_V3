POE Unit 2 • Bridge Design Challenge • Lesson 2.18

Bridge Fabrication

Engineering question: How can we manufacture and inspect a bridge safely and accurately?

LearnPracticeApplyFabrication Rules
Bridge capstone

Use the engineering design process

Apply mechanics, materials, analysis and evidence from earlier lessons.

Learning Targets

  • Approved fabrication processes
  • Assembly quality
  • Physical compliance checks

Learn the Concept

Primary members must be wood or plywood. Single-piece open-web plywood trusses allowed; acrylic, solid plywood side panels, fully 3D-printed bridges, screws, epoxy and hot glue prohibited.

Practice Together

Inspect joints, measure the complete bridge's 65 mm overall width and 150 g maximum mass, and confirm the intended 400 mm span.

Apply & Submit

Complete a build log, photographed inspection and dimensional checklist before testing.

Complete bridge manufacturing and connection specifications
