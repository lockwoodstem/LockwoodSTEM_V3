POE Unit 2 • Bridge Capstone • Lesson 2.16

Bridge Engineering Analysis

Engineering question: What do calculations predict about competing bridge concepts?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Phase 3: Apply mechanics to the bridge

Engineering decisions must be supported by evidence

Apply the mechanics and materials knowledge from Lessons 2.2–2.14 to a manufacturable, testable bridge.

Learning Targets

  • Draw a bridge-wide FBD
  • Estimate axial member forces and deflection risks
  • Evaluate mass and likely joint failures

Learn the Concept

  • Calculate support reactions under the 100 N midspan load
  • Use method-of-joints where a statically determinate simplified model applies
  • Estimate mass and note where modeling assumptions differ from actual glue joints

Practice Together

For a symmetric 100 N central load, determine ideal support reactions and identify the first joints to analyze.

Show your reasoning, annotate diagrams and use correct units.

Apply the Skill

Analyze at least one concept using calculations or approved simulation; compare risk locations and mass estimates.

Check your understanding: Which failure mode is not captured by an ideal pin-jointed truss model?

Student Evidence

Bridge analysis calculations and risk register. Provide clearly identified assumptions, measurements and conclusions.

Review complete challenge and fabrication specifications.
