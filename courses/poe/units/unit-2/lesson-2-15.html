Lesson Interactive

Parse the Payload-Support Design Brief

Classify the design-brief statements and connect performance requirements to verification.

Brief Excerpt

Design a freestanding support that holds a 25 N payload at least 180 mm above the base. Use no more than 50 g of supplied material. Submit a dimensioned concept, mass record, calculations, and three load-test trials.

Criteria tell the team how success is measured. Constraints limit resources or solution space. Deliverables are products submitted. Evidence verifies a claim.

Classify Each Statement

Hold a 25 N payload
Reach at least 180 mm
Use no more than 50 g
Submit a dimensioned concept
Provide three load-test trials
Separate measurable performance, design limits, submitted products, and proof.
