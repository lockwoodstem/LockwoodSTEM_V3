POE Unit 2 • Bridge Capstone • Lesson 2.15

Bridge Design Research & Concepts

Engineering question: Which open-web bridge layouts are worth developing under the challenge limits?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Phase 3: Apply mechanics to the bridge

Engineering decisions must be supported by evidence

Apply the mechanics and materials knowledge from Lessons 2.2–2.14 to a manufacturable, testable bridge.

Learning Targets

  • Research common bridge/truss forms
  • Generate three distinct preliminary structural concepts
  • Identify design constraints before CAD

Learn the Concept

  • Bridge specifications: 400 mm clear span, 65 mm maximum overall width, 100 N centered qualification, deflection ≤ 10 mm, total mass ≤ 150 g
  • Primary structure: wood or plywood; single-piece laser-cut open-web plywood trusses allowed; acrylic, solid side plates and fully printed bridges prohibited
  • Different concepts should change structure, not merely decoration

Practice Together

Sketch and compare Pratt, Warren and Howe-inspired side trusses. Identify load paths and likely compression members.

Show your reasoning, annotate diagrams and use correct units.

Apply the Skill

Generate three labeled concepts with approximate dimensions, cross-bracing and primary connection choices.

Check your understanding: What technical evidence would justify selecting one truss arrangement over another?

Student Evidence

Three bridge concept sketches with research notes. Provide clearly identified assumptions, measurements and conclusions.

Review complete challenge and fabrication specifications.
