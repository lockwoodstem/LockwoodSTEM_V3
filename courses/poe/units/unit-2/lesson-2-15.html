Learn the Concept

What You Need to Know

Detailed geometry

Final drawings or CAD should define 400 mm clear span, bridge width, chords/girders, verticals, diagonals, deck/cross members, support interfaces, and critical joints.

Joint definition

Specify how loads cross every important connection: adhesive area, gusset, tab, fastener, printed fitting, or another approved method.

Bill of materials

A BOM identifies materials, quantities, sizes, and purposes and supports a mass estimate.

Manufacturing sequence

Plan cutting, dry fitting, alignment, bonding, curing, and inspection in an order that supports accuracy.

Test plan

Define load locations, increments, measurements, stop conditions, PPE, exclusion zone, and required data before testing.

Build-ready test

A teammate who did not invent the concept should be able to answer: what gets made, what size it is, what material it uses, how it connects, how it is assembled, and how it will be verified.

Apply It to Your Bridge

Create Engineering Evidence

  1. Create final dimensioned drawings or CAD.
  2. Finalize materials and joint details.
  3. Prepare a BOM and predicted mass.
  4. Write the manufacturing sequence and inspection checks.
  5. Prepare the load-test procedure and safety rules.
  6. Submit for teacher approval before fabrication.
Lesson Deliverable

Bridge manufacturing design package

  • Dimensioned drawing or CAD.
  • BOM/material list and predicted mass.
  • Joint and support-interface details.
  • Manufacturing sequence.
  • Load-test plan with safety and stop conditions.

Format: Engineering notebook, class template, digital file, photos, CAD, spreadsheet, or teacher-approved equivalent.

Self-Check

Can You Explain It?

1. Why calculate predicted mass before building?

It can reveal a likely violation of the 150 g limit before changes become expensive or difficult.

2. Why plan assembly order?

Sequence affects alignment, access, curing, dimensional accuracy, and joint reliability.

Engineering thinking · Lesson 2.15

Document joints and process settings before building

Detail plywood cut geometry and gussets (40 × 25 mm maximum, 800 mm² area, 20 mm maximum member overlap). For any PLA/PETG connectors, verify the 50 mm/15 g part and 40 g total limits, slicer settings and M3 hardware.

Check your understanding: Can another team manufacture your bridge from your files without guessing the assembly sequence?

Bridge challenge connection: Record calculations, design choices, inspection measurements or test evidence in your team's design portfolio. Review the complete material, connection and fabrication requirements.
