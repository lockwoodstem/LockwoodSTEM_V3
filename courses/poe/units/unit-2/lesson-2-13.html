Materials Testing Lab

Engineering question: How can controlled tests support material decisions?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Phase 2: Materials knowledge

Build evidence through experiments

Learn properties and test methods using varied structures before beginning bridge fabrication.

Learning Targets

  • Control specimen shape, span and loading method
  • Measure force and displacement
  • Compare results while acknowledging measurement uncertainty

Learn the Concept

  • Change only one independent variable in a comparative test
  • Repeat trials when possible, and report units and dimensions
  • Use teacher-approved equipment and safety rules; avoid placing hands near loaded members

Practice Together

Plan a deflection test of strips with equal span and different orientations; identify controlled variables.

Show your reasoning, annotate diagrams and use correct units.

Apply the Skill

Collect and graph force-versus-deflection measurements; note failure or permanent deformation if observed.

Check your understanding: Can two specimens be compared fairly if their lengths differ?

Student Evidence

Materials lab report and data table. Provide clearly identified assumptions, measurements and conclusions.
