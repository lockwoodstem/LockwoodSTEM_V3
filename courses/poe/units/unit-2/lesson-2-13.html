Lesson Targets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Learning Target

I can interpret key regions and points on an engineering stress-strain curve.

Evidence

Stress-strain curve interpretation

Success Looks Like

The interpretation distinguishes elastic and plastic behavior and correctly identifies yield, ultimate strength, and fracture.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Read the axes

Connect stress to load per area and strain to normalized deformation.

2. Identify key points

Locate elastic slope, yield, ultimate strength, and fracture.

3. Connect to design

Explain what the curve implies for stiffness, strength, and ductility.

Lesson Interactive

Interpret the Stress-Strain Curve

Use the labeled training curve to identify yielding, ultimate tensile strength, fracture, and elastic behavior.

Training Curve

The early straight region represents elastic behavior. After yield, unloading may leave permanent deformation.

Identify the Points

Yield begins permanent deformation, the maximum stress is ultimate strength, and the final point is fracture.
Today’s Work

Main Deliverable

Stress-Strain Interpretation

Analyze one provided material stress-strain curve.

  • Label the elastic region, yield point, ultimate strength, and fracture point.
  • State one design implication related to stiffness, permanent deformation, strength, or ductility.
