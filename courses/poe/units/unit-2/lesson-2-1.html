Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Decode the mission

Separate what the structure must do from the limits placed on the design.

2. Trace the load path

Show how the payload force travels through members and connections to the supports.

3. Define proof

Choose calculations and tests that could verify performance.

Lesson Interactive

Decode the Payload-Support Mission

Classify each statement so the team knows what defines success, what limits the solution, and what evidence must be collected.

Challenge Statements

Mission: Support a 20 N payload 150 mm above the base using no more than 45 g of classroom material. The structure must survive three tests and be documented with calculations and photos.
