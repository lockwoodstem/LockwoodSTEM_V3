Moments, Equilibrium & Bridge Reactions

Use force and moment equilibrium to determine how a bridge load is shared by two supports.

Name: ____________________

1

Reference

M = F × d⊥

Moment is force times perpendicular distance.

ΣFy = 0    ΣM = 0

For a stable bridge, upward reactions balance the downward load and moments balance about any point.

2

Worked Centered Case

100 N is applied at the center of a 0.400 m span. Take moments about the left support:

RR(0.400) = 100(0.200) → RR = 50 N. Then ΣFy=0 gives RL = 50 N.

Current Bridge Challenge Manufacturing Limits

400 mm clear span; 65 mm maximum width; 100 N centered load; deflection ≤ 10 mm; total mass ≤ 150 g. Primary structure: approved wood or plywood, including one-piece laser-cut open-web plywood side trusses. No acrylic, solid side plates or fully printed bridges.

Joints: PVA/CA glue, interlocking plywood, plywood gussets, PLA/PETG printed fittings, or M3 nuts/bolts (M3 washers permitted); no screws, epoxy or hot glue. Plywood ≤ 3.2 mm thick; joint gussets ≤ 40 × 25 mm, ≤ 800 mm², ≤ 20 mm overlap on members, and at most two layers (one per side).

Printed connectors only (not primary members): PLA/PETG, ≤ 35% infill, ≤ 4 walls, ≤ 5 top/bottom layers each, ≤ 0.28 mm layer height, 0.4 mm nozzle, ≤ 50 mm long and ≤ 15 g per part, ≤ 40 g printed total.
