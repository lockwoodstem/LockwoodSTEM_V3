Additional Free-Body Diagram Practice

For every scenario: isolate the body, draw only external forces and reactions, label known values/directions, and include useful dimensions.

1. Hanging Equipment Case

50 N load supported by one vertical cable.

2. Crate on a Level Floor

Weight = 120 N; horizontal push = 35 N right; ignore friction.

3. Crate With Friction

Weight = 100 N; pull = 40 N right; friction = 12 N left.

4. Cantilever Practice Beam

25 N downward force at 0.40 m from the fixed wall.

5. Practice Beam With Two Loads

10 N at 0.15 m and 15 N at 0.35 m from the fixed support.

6. Unit 2 Bridge

Simply supported 400 mm bridge; 100 N downward load at midspan.

Current Bridge Challenge Manufacturing Limits

400 mm clear span; 65 mm maximum width; 100 N centered load; deflection ≤ 10 mm; total mass ≤ 150 g. Primary structure: approved wood or plywood, including one-piece laser-cut open-web plywood side trusses. No acrylic, solid side plates or fully printed bridges.

Joints: PVA/CA glue, interlocking plywood, plywood gussets, PLA/PETG printed fittings, or M3 nuts/bolts (M3 washers permitted); no screws, epoxy or hot glue. Plywood ≤ 3.2 mm thick; joint gussets ≤ 40 × 25 mm, ≤ 800 mm², ≤ 20 mm overlap on members, and at most two layers (one per side).

Printed connectors only (not primary members): PLA/PETG, ≤ 35% infill, ≤ 4 walls, ≤ 5 top/bottom layers each, ≤ 0.28 mm layer height, 0.4 mm nozzle, ≤ 50 mm long and ≤ 15 g per part, ≤ 40 g printed total. Submit CAD, print settings and measured masses when printed parts are used. Full unit specifications.
