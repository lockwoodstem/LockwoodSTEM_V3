Density, Mass & Structural Efficiency

Engineering question: How can engineers compare structures that carry different loads and have different masses?

Learn the Concept

What You Need to Know

Density relationship

Density connects mass and volume: ρ = m/V.

Mass budget

The 75 g limit is a maximum, not a target. A lighter qualified wing can be preferable.

Structural efficiency

For this unit, compare maximum supported load with actual structure mass in N/g.

Requirements first

A high efficiency score does not rescue a wing that fails the 20 N load or exceeds the 20 mm deflection limit.

Worked comparison

Wing A: 70 g, 45 N maximum → 0.64 N/g. Wing B: 45 g, 40 N maximum → 0.89 N/g. Wing A carries more total load, but Wing B carries more load per gram.

Apply It to Your Wing

Create Engineering Evidence

  1. Complete density/mass calculations.
  2. Compare at least three hypothetical wings using N/g.
  3. Identify which is strongest, lightest, and most efficient.
  4. Explain why those labels can describe different designs.
Lesson Deliverable

Structural efficiency analysis

  • Density/mass calculations.
  • Efficiency calculations for at least three structures.
  • An evidence-based comparison of strength, mass, and efficiency.

Format: Engineering notebook, class template, digital file, or teacher-approved equivalent.
