Learn the Concept

What You Need to Know

Density relationship

Density connects mass and volume: ρ = m/V.

Mass budget

The 150 g limit is a maximum, not a target. A lighter qualified bridge can be preferable.

Structural efficiency

For this unit, compare maximum supported load with actual structure mass in N/g.

Requirements first

A high efficiency score does not rescue a bridge that fails the 100 N load or exceeds the 10 mm deflection limit.

Worked comparison

Bridge A: 70 g, 45 N maximum → 0.64 N/g. Bridge B: 45 g, 40 N maximum → 0.89 N/g. Bridge A carries more total load, but Bridge B carries more load per gram.

Model the Bridge Before You Build It

  1. Open the Unit 2 mass-budget spreadsheet and enter each planned bridge component, material, quantity, and estimated mass.
  2. Use the automatic totals to compare projected mass with the 150 g limit.
  3. Compare the provided example designs and determine which is strongest, lightest, most efficient, and actually qualified.
  4. As fabrication and testing progress, replace estimates with measured mass, deflection, and maximum-load data.
  5. Use the calculated N/g value only after checking the 100 N load and 10 mm deflection requirements.
