Calculating Moments

Engineering question: How do force magnitude and perpendicular distance produce rotation?

LearnPracticeApplySubmitUnit 2
Foundational instruction

Learn it before the bridge project

Use varied engineering examples to master the mechanics, then apply the technique to your final structural design later in the unit.

Learning Targets

  • Calculate scalar moments about a pivot
  • Apply a consistent clockwise/counterclockwise sign convention
  • Use ΣM = 0 to determine support reactions

Learn the Concept

  • Moment magnitude M = Fd⊥; unit N·m
  • A force whose line of action passes through the pivot makes zero moment about that pivot
  • In static equilibrium, ΣM = 0 along with ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0

During instruction: Sketch the forces, geometry, variables or joints discussed and record units for every quantity.

Practice Together

A 30 N force acts downward 0.40 m to the right of a pivot. Find its moment and rotational direction.

Show: a labeled diagram, a mathematical model when relevant, and a final statement interpreting the result.

Apply the Skill

For a 4.0 m simply supported beam with 80 N acting 1.0 m from the left, calculate the right and left reactions.

Check your understanding: Why is the reaction at a chosen pivot often convenient when writing a moment equation?

Student Evidence

Moment calculations and checked support reactions. Use your engineering notebook or teacher-provided guided notes; include dimensions, units and a conclusion.

Looking ahead: These mechanics techniques will be revisited when students analyze the bridge design in Lessons 2.15–2.20.
