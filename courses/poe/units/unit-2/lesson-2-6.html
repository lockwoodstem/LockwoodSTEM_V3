Lesson Targets

What Students Should Be Able To Do

Learning Target

I can use force and moment equilibrium to calculate unknown support reactions.

Evidence

Equilibrium solution

Success Looks Like

The support reactions satisfy both force balance and moment balance with correct signs and units.

Activity Flow

How the Lesson Moves

Each step builds the structural reasoning needed for the lesson deliverable.

1. Model the structure

Create a free body diagram with known loads and unknown reactions.

2. Apply moment equilibrium

Choose a pivot that reduces the number of unknowns.

3. Verify force balance

Use the calculated reactions to confirm the net force is zero.

Lesson Interactive

Loaded Beam

Reaction Forces

Use symmetry or moment equilibrium, then verify that the vertical force sum equals zero.
Today’s Student Work

Main Deliverable

Support-Reaction Solution

Solve one statically determinate support problem.

  • Show the free body diagram and equations for force and moment equilibrium.
  • Report the support reactions with units and verify both equilibrium conditions.
