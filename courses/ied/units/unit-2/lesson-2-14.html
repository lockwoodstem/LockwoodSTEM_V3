Assignment

FabLab 3D Printer Certification

Before operating a classroom 3D printer independently, you must complete the existing LockwoodSTEM 3D Printer Certification. This lesson does not replace the certification process.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open the existing certification: Navigate to the current LockwoodSTEM 3D Printer Certification used by the class.
  2. Review required safety procedures: Pay particular attention to hot surfaces, moving printer components, material handling, print removal, failed prints, and when to ask the instructor for help.
  3. Complete all certification requirements: Finish every required question, demonstration, or approval step. Do not skip sections because you have used a 3D printer before.
  4. Verify your status: Confirm that your certification shows as passed/approved before attempting independent printer operation.
  5. Ask for correction if needed: If the system does not record your result correctly, notify the instructor before moving on.

Before You Submit

  • Certification status shows passed/approved.
  • You understand that certification is required before independent printer use.
  • No independent printer operation occurs before approval.
Submission

What You Will Submit

  • Certification completion: successfully complete the existing LockwoodSTEM 3D Printer Certification and make sure your certification status shows as passed/approved.

File requirements: No uploaded file is required for this lesson unless the instructor requests one. Completion is verified through the existing certification system.
