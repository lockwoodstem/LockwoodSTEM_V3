Fast prints may use less detail, strength, or surface quality. Settings must balance time, material, accuracy, strength, and support needs.

Your Task

Compare Slicer Settings and Trade-Offs

Open the exported model in the slicer and evaluate how orientation, layer height, infill, walls, and supports affect the planned print.

  1. Import the correct mesh file and confirm scale.
  2. Choose and justify the print orientation.
  3. Record the assigned layer, wall, infill, and support settings.
  4. Review the preview for unsupported areas and estimate print time/material use.

Quality check: The slicer preview is free of obvious errors and the selected settings match the purpose of the part.
