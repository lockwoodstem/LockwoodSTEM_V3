IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.14

FabLab 3D Printer Certification

Essential question: What must you demonstrate before independently operating the classroom 3D printers?

Lesson goalComplete the existing LockwoodSTEM 3D Printer Certification.
DeliverableExisting 3D Printer Certification
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Existing LockwoodSTEM certification system

Instructor Demonstration

  1. No new Fusion demonstration is required.
  2. Review where to access the certification and how completion is recorded.
  3. Clarify that certification is required before independent printer operation.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Complete any practice/review built into the existing certification.

Assignment

  1. Complete the existing 3D Printer Certification and verify that the certification status is recorded.

Before You Submit

  • All certification components are complete.
  • Certification status is visible/recorded.
  • Student understands that independent printer use requires certification.

What You Submit

Existing 3D Printer Certification

Submit the file(s), screenshots, measurements, or written evidence specified by your instructor for this lesson.
