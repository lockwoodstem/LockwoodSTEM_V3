Learning Targets

  • Review 3D printer safety expectations.
  • Identify proper setup, monitoring, and cleanup behaviors.
  • Connect certification to independent printer access.

Success Looks Like

You understand that certification protects people, equipment, materials, and the print queue.

LaunchLearnModelCheckSubmit
Opening Prompt

Why do students need certification before independent printer use?

Think first, then share a response that connects today’s CAD skill to accuracy, editability, printability, or evidence quality.

Reveal: useful CAD thinking move

Certification ensures students understand safety, setup, monitoring, cleanup, and queue expectations before using shared equipment.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Certification protects people, tools, and materials.

Key Idea 2

Safe printer use includes setup, monitoring, and cleanup.

Key Idea 3

Responsible use means respecting the queue and documenting prints.

Interactive Check

Which behavior best protects the FabLab print queue?

ASubmitting files with clear names and checking settings
BStarting prints without permission
CRemoving someone else’s print early
DIgnoring failed prints
Reveal Answer

A — Submitting files with clear names and checking settings

Clear files and checked settings reduce wasted time and materials for everyone.

Engineering Workflow

Use These Moves

  1. Review printer safety expectations.
  2. Identify what to do before, during, and after a print.
  3. Check print request or certification requirements.
Student Task

Apply the Skill

Complete the 3D printer certification preparation or required certification activity.

Checkpoint: be ready to show the CAD evidence that proves your work is controlled, accurate, printable, or ready to revise.

Evidence Challenge

What evidence would you need?

Claim: “My fablab 3d printer certification work is accurate and ready to move forward.”

AA screenshot or model view that shows the feature
BA statement that it looks good
CRelevant dimensions, settings, or test results
DA random file name
Reveal Answer

A + C are the strongest evidence pair. They show what was created and whether it meets the requirement.

Scenario Response

What should the engineer do next?

A student notices a print starting to fail. What should they do?

AReport it and follow the teacher’s printer workflow
BWalk away because the printer is automatic
CTouch hot parts immediately
DHide the issue until class ends
Reveal Answer

A — Report it and follow the teacher’s printer workflow

The strongest next step records the issue, identifies the likely cause, and turns the result into useful CAD evidence.

Student Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Certification evidence is complete.
  • Student can describe safe printer behavior.
  • Student understands print queue expectations.
  • Exit response connecting the skill to CAD evidence or model quality
