IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.7

Fillets, Chamfers, and Design Intent — Bracket 3

Essential question: How do edge features improve the function, manufacturability, and appearance of an engineered part?

Lesson goalFinish Bracket 3 and verify the complete model against the original engineering documentation.
Deliverable2.7 Bracket 3 Final CAD Model
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

FilletChamferTimeline EditInspect/Measure

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Compare a sharp edge, fillet, and chamfer on a simple sample part.
  2. Discuss practical reasons for edge treatments: safety, fit, stress, manufacturing, and appearance.
  3. Apply and edit fillets/chamfers from the Timeline.
  4. Use Inspect/Measure to perform a final model check.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Add one edge treatment to Bracket 3 with the instructor and confirm the correct value.

Assignment

  1. Complete all remaining Bracket 3 edge features.
  2. Compare the finished model to the original Lesson 1.5 documentation and verify critical dimensions.

Before You Submit

  • Fillets/chamfers match the documentation.
  • Overall part geometry is correct.
  • Critical dimensions are verified.
  • Timeline is readable and logically ordered.
