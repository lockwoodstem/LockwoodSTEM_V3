Opening Prompt

Why are holes and slots often more than just empty space?

Connect your response to the decision, evidence, or quality standard required for Create Functional Cuts and Holes.

Reveal: useful CAD thinking move

Cuts create functional features: clearance for fasteners, alignment, weight reduction, access, or fit between parts.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Cuts create holes, slots, pockets, and clearance features.

Key Idea 2

Functional openings must be placed and dimensioned accurately.

Key Idea 3

Cut direction and depth affect whether the feature works.

Lesson Interactive

Choose the Correct Cut Extent

Match each functional feature to the cut extent that will remain reliable if the part thickness changes later.

FeaturePurposeCut extent
Bolt holeMust pass completely through the plate
Shallow pocketLeaves 4 mm of material below
Label recessEngraves text 0.6 mm deep
Use through-all for a feature that must always pass through; use a controlled distance for partial-depth features.
Your Task

Create Functional Cuts and Holes

Add a pocket, slot, or hole that removes material for a clear functional reason.

  1. Select the face or plane where the removed feature begins.
  2. Sketch and locate the cut profile.
  3. Choose the correct cut depth or through-all condition.
  4. Inspect the result from at least two views.

Quality check: The removed feature is located, sized, and cut in the intended direction without damaging other geometry.

What You Will Submit

Cut Feature Model

  • A saved model with one correctly located cut, slot, or hole.
  • A short note stating the function of the removed feature.
