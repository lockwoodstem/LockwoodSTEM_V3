IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.13

Slicer Basics and Print Settings

Essential question: How do slicer settings affect print time, material use, and expected part quality?

Lesson goalUse Bambu Studio to compare manufacturing settings through a controlled slicing investigation.
Deliverable2.13 Slicer Settings Investigation
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Bambu StudioSlice PlatePreviewLayer HeightInfillWallsOrientation

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Import the standardized model and select the correct printer/nozzle/material profile.
  2. Create the baseline slice and read estimated print time and filament use.
  3. Use Preview to inspect layers and toolpaths.
  4. Change one variable at a time: layer height, infill, wall count, then orientation.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Record the baseline results and one guided comparison trial.

Assignment

  1. Complete all assigned controlled slicing trials.
  2. Use the results to recommend settings for the assigned manufacturing scenario.

Before You Submit

  • Only one variable changes between comparison trials.
  • Time and filament estimates are recorded.
  • Orientation tradeoffs are considered.
  • Final recommendation cites evidence from the investigation.
