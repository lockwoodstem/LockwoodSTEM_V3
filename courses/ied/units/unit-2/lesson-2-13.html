IED Unit 2 • Lesson 2.13 • Puzzle Cube Challenge Day 5

Puzzle Cube Manufacturing & Final Verification

Focus question: How can engineers prepare a multi-part CAD design for manufacturing and verify that the physical result matches the design intent?

Go to AssignmentVideo HelpWhat to SubmitBack to Unit 2
20 Points • Day 5 + Post-Print Check

Prepare the final Fusion and STL files for teacher batch printing, then demonstrate the printed puzzle after manufacturing.

Expected result: Five print-ready pieces that can be manufactured without supports and assembled into the final 60 mm cube.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Verify a CAD design before manufacturing.
  • Export individual bodies as correctly named STL files.
  • Evaluate whether each piece has a support-free print orientation.
  • Compare a finished physical product with the original digital design.

Two-Stage Grade

The digital manufacturing package is graded first. After the instructor batch-prints the pieces in five colors, the final points are earned by checking the physical pieces and assembling the completed puzzle.

Puzzle Cube Challenge

Project Requirements

These requirements apply throughout Lessons 2.10–2.13.

Finished Cube

3 × 3 × 3 Unit Cube

  • 27 total unit cubes.
  • Each unit cube is exactly 20 mm × 20 mm × 20 mm.
  • The assembled puzzle must be 60 mm × 60 mm × 60 mm.
  • The final puzzle contains exactly 5 pieces.
Piece Geometry

Five Connected Pieces

  • Each piece contains at least 3 unit cubes.
  • Every piece must be one continuous solid with cubes connected face-to-face.
  • At least 1 of the 5 pieces must span at least 2 unit cubes in X, Y, and Z.
  • Pieces may repeat the same shape.
Color & Printing

Plan for Manufacturing

  • Use five different colors consistently in the sketch, Fusion model, and final printed puzzle.
  • Each piece must have a practical flat-face orientation that can print without support material.
  • No additional clearance is required; use the exact 20 mm unit geometry.
Assignment

Prepare the Puzzle Cube for 3D Printing

Complete a final quality check, export each body as its own STL file, and submit the manufacturing package. The instructor will handle batch slicing and printing.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open the assembled Fusion model: Verify the design still contains exactly five separate bodies and that none were accidentally joined during Lesson 2.12.
  2. Perform the final size check: Use Inspect/Measure to confirm the complete puzzle is exactly 60 mm × 60 mm × 60 mm and the design is based on exact 20 mm units.
  3. Recheck the project rules: Confirm each piece contains at least three unit cubes, each piece is face-connected, and at least one piece satisfies the X/Y/Z span requirement.
  4. Check printability: Inspect each body and identify a flat-face orientation that will allow the piece to print without support material. If a piece cannot be printed support-free, revise the design while preserving the required puzzle geometry.
  5. Save the final Fusion design: Make sure the five pieces are assembled and the bodies are clearly named Piece A through Piece E.
  6. Export Piece A: Select only Piece A and use Save as Mesh/Export Mesh to create an STL file.
  7. Export Pieces B–E: Repeat the process so there are exactly five separate STL files. Do not export the entire assembled cube as one STL.
  8. Name the files clearly: Include the piece letter and intended print color in each STL filename so the instructor can batch-print the correct colors.
  9. Submit the digital manufacturing package: Upload the final Fusion archive plus all five STL files. The instructor will handle slicing and printing.
  10. After printing: Inspect your five colored physical pieces and confirm they match the intended Piece A–E geometry.
  11. Demonstrate the final puzzle: Assemble all five printed pieces into one 60 mm × 60 mm × 60 mm cube for the instructor.

Before You Submit

  • Exactly five separate STL files are included.
  • Each STL contains only one puzzle piece.
  • Every piece has a support-free print orientation.
  • The final .f3d file contains all five assembled bodies.
  • STL filenames clearly identify the piece and intended color.
Submission

What You Will Submit

  • Final Fusion archive: LastName_FirstName_2_13_PuzzleCube_Final.f3d.
  • Five STL files: one file each for Pieces A–E, named in the format LastName_FirstName_PieceA_Color.stl.
  • Physical verification after printing: show the instructor the five printed pieces and successfully assemble them into the completed 60 mm cube.

Teacher printing: Students are responsible for correct print-ready geometry and STL exports; the instructor will handle batch slicing and printing. Printer failures or manufacturing defects outside the student's control do not reduce the grade.
