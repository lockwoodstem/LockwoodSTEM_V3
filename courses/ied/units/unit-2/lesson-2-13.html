Your Task

Complete a controlled slicer investigation using the same teacher-provided model for every trial.

  1. Create the standardized baseline slice.
  2. Change layer height while holding other settings constant.
  3. Repeat the process for infill and wall count.
  4. Compare a second orientation and complete the manufacturing scenario recommendation.

Quality check: Your settings recommendation is supported by slicer evidence rather than guesswork.

What You Will Submit

Required Evidence

  • Completed slicer investigation table.
  • Recorded print-time and filament estimates.
  • Final recommended settings for the assigned manufacturing scenario.
  • Evidence-based justification using results from the investigation.

Submission standard: Evidence must clearly show the finished work and the verification required by this lesson.
