Your Task

Repair five broken sketches and revise the Lesson 2.2 mounting plate using appropriate geometric constraints.

  1. Inspect geometry by dragging it.
  2. Apply Horizontal/Vertical, Coincident, Parallel, Perpendicular, Tangent, Equal, Concentric, Midpoint, or Symmetry as appropriate.
  3. Re-test the geometry after each repair.
  4. Leave dimensional freedom intentionally for Lesson 2.4.

Quality check: Your geometry keeps the intended relationships when moved.

What You Will Submit

Required Evidence

  • Five repaired sketches.
  • Revised mounting plate with appropriate geometric constraints.
  • Short response identifying one remaining degree of freedom and what information is missing.

Submission standard: Evidence must clearly show the finished work and the verification required by this lesson.
