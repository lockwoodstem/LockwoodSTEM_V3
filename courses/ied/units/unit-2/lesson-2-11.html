IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.11

CAD Model Check and Revision

Essential question: How can engineers systematically evaluate and improve a CAD model before manufacturing?

Lesson goalReview the Lesson 2.10 model for accuracy, robustness, and model organization.
Deliverable2.11 CAD Design Review & Revision
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Inspect/MeasureTimelineSketch EditFeature Edit

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Show two visually identical models: one robust and one poorly structured.
  2. Edit an early dimension in each model to demonstrate why feature relationships matter.
  3. Inspect sketches for unconstrained geometry, redundant features, and unnecessary Fix constraints.
  4. Rename important Timeline features and remove unnecessary model elements.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Run the three assigned stress tests on the Lesson 2.10 model and record pass/fail results.

Assignment

  1. Complete the CAD Health Check, peer review, and at least one meaningful revision to the model.

Before You Submit

  • Critical dimensions match the drawing.
  • Stress-test values are returned to the original specification.
  • At least one meaningful revision is documented.
  • Final model remains stable and readable.
