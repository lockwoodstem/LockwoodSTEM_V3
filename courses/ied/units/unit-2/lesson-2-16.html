IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.16

Tolerance Testing & Manufacturing Recommendations

Essential question: How can physical test results be used to improve future CAD designs?

Lesson goalUse physical testing to establish evidence-based fit and heat-set insert design recommendations.
Deliverable2.16 Manufacturing Recommendations
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Physical testerHeat-set insertsInstallation toolFusion model for reference

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Inspect a printed tester for defects that could invalidate results.
  2. Demonstrate safe heat-set insert installation.
  3. Show how each physical test feature maps back to a modeled CAD dimension.
  4. Explain why recommendations must include the printer/material/nozzle/layer conditions used.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Test one fit and one insert hole as a class example before students complete their own data table.

Assignment

  1. Classify the peg/hole results.
  2. Install and evaluate the standardized heat-set inserts.
  3. Compare predictions with actual results.
  4. Record recommended CAD values and the manufacturing conditions under which they were obtained.

Before You Submit

  • Print defects are documented before testing.
  • Fit classifications are complete.
  • Heat-set insert installation and retention are evaluated.
  • Recommendations are supported by physical evidence.
  • Manufacturing conditions are documented.
