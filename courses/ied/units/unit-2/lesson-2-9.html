Reveal: useful CAD thinking move

A nose cone is rotationally symmetric. A 2D side profile can rotate around a centerline to form the full 3D part.

Your Task

Model a Rotational Rocket Feature

Create a nose cone or nozzle form by revolving a half-profile around a center axis.

  1. Sketch a clean half-profile of the rotational shape.
  2. Create or select the intended axis of rotation.
  3. Revolve the profile through the required angle.
  4. Inspect the resulting solid for gaps, self-intersections, or an incorrect axis.

Quality check: The revolved body is continuous and matches the intended axial shape.
