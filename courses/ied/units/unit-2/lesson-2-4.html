Your Task

Control a Sketch with Constraints

Add geometric relationships so the sketch keeps its intended shape when geometry is moved or edited.

  1. Identify lines or points that should remain horizontal, vertical, connected, parallel, or equal.
  2. Apply only the constraints needed to communicate those relationships.
  3. Drag-test the sketch to find uncontrolled geometry.
  4. Correct conflicts or missing relationships and save the sketch.

Quality check: The sketch maintains its intended relationships during a drag test without unnecessary or conflicting constraints.
