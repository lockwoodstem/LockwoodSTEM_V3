IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.4

Dimensioned CAD Sketches

Essential question: How do dimensions and constraints work together to create a precise CAD sketch?

Lesson goalCreate a fully defined sketch from engineering documentation using dimensions and geometric constraints.
Deliverable2.4 Precision Sketch Challenge
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Sketch DimensionLinear DimensionDiameterRadiusAngle

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Add linear, diameter, radius, angular, and location dimensions.
  2. Show why dimensions and geometric constraints do different jobs.
  3. Create an over-constrained example and resolve the warning.
  4. Verify that a fully constrained sketch also matches the required numerical values.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Complete a short guided drawing-to-sketch example.
  2. Use constraints first, then dimensions, then verify the sketch.

Assignment

  1. Create the assigned precision sketch from the dimensioned technical drawing.
  2. The final sketch must be fully constrained and dimensionally correct.

Before You Submit

  • Geometry matches the drawing.
  • Dimensions match the specification.
  • Sketch is fully constrained.
  • No redundant dimensions or constraints remain.
