Your Task

Create the Precision Sketch Challenge from a dimensioned technical drawing.

  1. Create the required geometry.
  2. Apply geometric relationships first.
  3. Add dimensions that define size and position.
  4. Resolve any over-constraint warnings and verify the final values.

Quality check: Your sketch is both fully constrained and dimensionally correct.

What You Will Submit

Required Evidence

  • Completed Fusion sketch from the drawing.
  • Screenshot showing dimensions and fully constrained status.
  • Fusion file.
  • Short explanation of one constraint and one dimension and why both are needed.

Submission standard: Evidence must clearly show the finished work and the verification required by this lesson.
