Creating Rotational Parts with Revolve

Focus question: How can engineers create rotationally symmetric 3D parts from a 2D profile?

Today’s Plan

Use this page with the teacher demonstration, your engineering notebook, and the assigned CAD or manufacturing evidence.

Learning Targets

  • Identify when Revolve is more efficient than Extrude.
  • Create a dimensioned half-profile and center axis.

Opening Prompt

How can engineers create rotationally symmetric 3D parts from a 2D profile?

Use what you already know from engineering documentation, CAD, or manufacturing to make an initial prediction before the demonstration begins.

Reveal: engineering habit for this lesson

Plan the feature or manufacturing decision before clicking commands. Then verify the result instead of assuming the model is correct because it looks right.

Your Task

Model a standalone rotational manufactured component such as an instrument/control knob.

  1. Identify the rotational axis and half-profile from the technical drawing.
  2. Create and fully constrain the profile.
  3. Use a construction centerline as the revolve axis.
  4. Revolve 360° and complete the required parametric change test.

Quality check: The rotational part is driven by a clean half-profile and updates correctly when dimensions change.

What You Will Submit

Required Evidence

  • Fusion model of the rotational component.
  • Screenshot of the fully constrained half-profile.
  • Screenshot of the completed solid.
  • Verification of critical diameter and length dimensions.

Submission standard: Evidence must clearly show the finished work and the verification required by this lesson.
