IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.8

Creating Rotational Parts with Revolve

Essential question: How can engineers create rotationally symmetric 3D parts from a 2D profile?

Lesson goalModel a standalone rotational component from a dimensioned half-profile.
Deliverable2.8 Parametric Rotational Component
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Construction LineRevolveAxisAngle

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Compare a part that is efficient to Extrude with one that is efficient to Revolve.
  2. Create a simple half-profile and construction centerline.
  3. Use Revolve 360° to create the solid.
  4. Edit the profile dimensions and show the resulting parametric update.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Complete a short stepped-shaft Revolve example.

Assignment

  1. Model the assigned control knob or other rotational manufactured component from its dimensioned cross-section.
  2. Complete the required dimension-change test without rebuilding the model.

Before You Submit

  • Half-profile is fully constrained.
  • Construction axis is appropriate.
  • Revolve produces the intended solid.
  • Critical length and diameter values are verified.
