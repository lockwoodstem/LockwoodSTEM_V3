IED Unit 2 • Lesson 2.10

Condensation-Draining Coaster Mini-Project

Focus question: How can multiple CAD features work together to create a functional product that solves a simple everyday problem?

Assignment & SubmissionBack to Unit 2
Lesson Goal

Design a two-part coaster with a removable patterned insert and a lower condensation collection tray.

Expected result: Complete the required work and verify the CAD or manufacturing evidence before submitting.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Apply multiple Unit 2 CAD tools in one independent design.
  • Create two parts that fit together within a controlled design envelope.
  • Use repeated geometry to create a functional drainage pattern.
  • Prepare two manufacturing files for instructor printing.

How to Approach the Work

Use the instructor demonstration to understand the tool or design decision, then complete the assigned work independently and verify the result before submission.

LearnModelVerifyReviseSubmit
Project Example

Two-Part Condensation Coaster

The upper insert supports the cup and allows condensation to drain into the lower collection tray.

Design Constraints

Required Limits

80 mm maximum

Overall width or diameter.

15 mm maximum

Total assembled thickness.

Two parts

Outer collection tray and removable insert.

Patterned insert

Repeated openings must allow condensation to drain.

Design Requirements

What the Parts Must Do

Outer Collection Tray

  • Contain a recessed collection area.
  • Include a ledge or support surface for the insert.
  • Remain stable on a flat surface.
  • Allow the insert to be removed without damaging either part.

Patterned Insert

  • Support a cup or glass.
  • Include a connected repeated pattern created with Mirror or Pattern.
  • Allow condensation to pass through to the tray.
  • Optionally include simple text, initials, or a logo at the center.

Assignment & Submission

Complete the Work

  1. Create a concept sketch showing the outer tray, removable insert, drainage pattern, and optional center personalization.
  2. Keep the complete coaster at or below 80 mm in overall width/diameter and 15 mm in total assembled thickness.
  3. Model the outer tray with a recessed condensation collection area and an internal ledge or support surface for the insert.
  4. Model the removable insert with a connected drainage pattern created using Mirror, Rectangular Pattern, Circular Pattern, or another appropriate repeated-feature method.
  5. Optionally add simple text, initials, or a logo in the center of the insert.
  6. Use Inspect/Measure to verify the overall size, assembled thickness, and fit relationship before exporting.
  7. Export both parts as separate manufacturing files for instructor printing.

Submit to Google Classroom

  • Concept sketch: attach a clear photo, scan, or PDF named LastName_FirstName_2_10_Concept.
  • Editable Fusion design: export the complete two-part design as LastName_FirstName_2_10_Coaster.f3d.
  • Outer tray manufacturing file: export as LastName_FirstName_2_10_Tray.3mf.
  • Insert manufacturing file: export as LastName_FirstName_2_10_Insert.3mf.
  • Screenshot 1: assembled/isometric view. Save as LastName_FirstName_2_10_Assembled.png.
  • Screenshot 2: insert shown clearly with the drainage pattern visible. Save as LastName_FirstName_2_10_InsertPattern.png.
  • Private comment: state the final overall width/diameter, total assembled thickness, and the feature you predict is most likely to need revision after printing.

File submission instructions: For the editable model, use File → Export → Fusion 360 Archive Files (*.f3d). For each printable part, select the tray body/component in the Browser, right-click it, choose Save as Mesh, choose 3MF as the format, and save it with the required filename; repeat for the insert. Attach the concept sketch, .f3d, both .3mf files, and both PNG screenshots in Google Classroom. The instructor will print from the submitted manufacturing files.
