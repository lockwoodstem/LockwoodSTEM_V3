Design for 3D Printing

Mission Briefing

Today’s Plan

Use this page with the teacher demonstration, your engineering notebook, and the assigned CAD or manufacturing evidence.

Learning Targets

  • Identify geometry that creates FDM printing challenges.
  • Evaluate orientation tradeoffs involving support, strength, surface quality, time, and stability.
  • Modify geometry to improve printability and export a manufacturing mesh.

Reveal: engineering habit for this lesson

Plan the feature or manufacturing decision before clicking commands. Then verify the result instead of assuming the model is correct because it looks right.

Your Task

Complete a printability review and improve a teacher-provided challenge model for FDM manufacturing.

  1. Analyze several example parts for printability.
  2. Compare possible orientations for the same component.
  3. Modify at least two problematic features in the challenge model.
  4. Export the corrected model in the required manufacturing format.

Quality check: You can distinguish a CAD-valid model from one that is ready to manufacture effectively.

What You Will Submit

Required Evidence

  • Completed printability analysis.
  • Orientation decision with engineering justification.
  • Before/after screenshots showing two design modifications.
  • Correctly exported manufacturing file.

Submission standard: Evidence must clearly show the finished work and the verification required by this lesson.
