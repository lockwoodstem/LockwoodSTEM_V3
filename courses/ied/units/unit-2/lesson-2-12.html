IED Unit 2 · Lesson 2.12

Design for 3D Printing

Essential question: How does the 3D-printing process influence the way engineers design a part?

Lesson goalEvaluate whether a CAD model is appropriate for FDM manufacturing and improve problematic geometry.
Deliverable2.12 Printability Review
Instructor DemonstrationAssignmentVideo Help

New Skills and Tools

Inspect/MeasureOrientationMesh Export

Instructor Demonstration

  1. Use simple accurate CAD examples to show overhangs, bridges, thin/small features, unsupported geometry, and awkward horizontal openings.
  2. Rotate the same component into multiple print orientations and discuss tradeoffs.
  3. Modify a teacher-provided challenge model for improved printability.
  4. Export the corrected part as a manufacturing mesh.
During the demonstration: Follow along in Fusion only when directed. The goal is to understand the modeling decision, not simply copy clicks.

Practice

  1. Classify sample components as Print As-Is, Reorient, Modify Geometry, or Supports Likely Required.

Assignment

  1. Choose the best orientation for the assigned component and justify the decision.
  2. Modify at least two problematic features in the Printability Challenge Part.
  3. Export the corrected model.

Before You Submit

  • Orientation choice is justified with manufacturing tradeoffs.
  • At least two useful geometry changes are present.
  • Original design intent is preserved.
  • Manufacturing file exports correctly.

What You Submit
