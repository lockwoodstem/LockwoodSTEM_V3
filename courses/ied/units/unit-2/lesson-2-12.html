IED Unit 2 • Lesson 2.12

Design for 3D Printing

Focus question: How does the 3D-printing process influence the way engineers design a part?

Go to AssignmentVideo HelpBack to Unit 2
Lesson Goal

Evaluate CAD geometry for FDM manufacturing and modify problematic features before printing.

Expected result: Complete the required work and verify the CAD or manufacturing evidence before submitting.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Identify common FDM geometry challenges.
  • Evaluate print-orientation tradeoffs.
  • Modify geometry to improve printability while preserving design intent.

How to Approach the Work

Use the instructor demonstration to understand the tool or design decision, then complete the assigned work independently and verify the result before submission.

LearnModelVerifyReviseSubmit
Assignment

Design for 3D Printing

  1. Classify the sample models as Print As-Is, Reorient, Modify Geometry, or Supports Likely Required.
  2. Compare multiple orientations for the same component.
  3. Modify at least two problematic features in the teacher-provided challenge model.
  4. Export the corrected manufacturing file.
Submission

What You Will Submit

  • Completed printability analysis.
  • Orientation decision with engineering justification.
  • Before/after evidence showing two design changes.
  • Correctly exported manufacturing file.
