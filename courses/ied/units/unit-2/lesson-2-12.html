Why is checking a CAD model before printing usually faster than fixing a bad print?

Reveal: useful CAD thinking move

CAD changes are usually faster and cheaper than wasting print time, material, and queue space on a part with avoidable errors.

Your Task

Complete a CAD Model Quality Review

Inspect the completed model for geometry, feature order, naming, dimensions, and fabrication readiness before export.

  1. Review the model from standard and isometric views.
  2. Check the browser and timeline for extra bodies, failed features, or unclear organization.
  3. Verify critical dimensions against the assignment requirements.
  4. Correct and document at least one issue or confirm the model passed each check.

Quality check: The model is organized, matches the requirements, and contains no known geometry errors.
