MINI CHALLENGE

What Evidence Supports a Concept?

  • A labeled concept sketch showing rocket support
  • B
  • A statement that the design looks like NASA
  • C
  • Notes connecting features to criteria
  • D
  • A teammate saying it should work
  • E
  • A list of colors for decoration
  • Vote first. Then we will reveal the best evidence pair.
