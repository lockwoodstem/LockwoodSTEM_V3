Opening Prompt

What does a launch pad need to do?

Look past appearance and identify functions: support, stability, access, alignment, protection, and organization.

Quick class share: Name one launch-pad feature and explain the need it serves.

Reveal the challenge mindset

Research is useful when you connect a feature to its function.

Do not copy a real launch pad at full complexity. Adapt useful ideas to the classroom challenge.

Challenge Brief

Design and build a launch pad

The first complete Unit 0 challenge begins here.

Design Challenge

Your team will create a launch pad that:

  • Fits and supports the provided 3D-printed rocket
  • Stands on its own
  • Includes research-informed launch-pad features
  • Uses only approved classroom materials
  • Is justified through sketches, documentation, testing, and presentation
Visual Research

Observe structure and function

Use the image to identify support, access, stability, and ground-system features.

Requirements

Keep the design target visible

Every concept should be checked against the same requirements.

Fit + Support

The provided rocket must sit securely.

Stability

The pad must stand without tipping or collapsing.

Realistic Features

Include features connected to launch-pad functions.

Materials

Use approved classroom materials; no custom 3D-printed parts.

Documentation

Use sketches, notes, tests, and explanations.

Scenario Response

How should the team choose a direction?

Vote before opening the answer.

Four students have different launch-pad concepts. One student wants the team to use only their sketch because it looks the most complete.

  1. AUse the most complete sketch without discussion.
  2. BVote immediately and discard the other ideas.
  3. CShare all sketches, identify useful features, and combine them into one team concept.
  4. DAsk the teacher to choose the design.
Reveal the strongest team process

Best answer: C

The team should combine useful features from multiple concepts. This increases participation and creates a design that can be justified against criteria and constraints.
