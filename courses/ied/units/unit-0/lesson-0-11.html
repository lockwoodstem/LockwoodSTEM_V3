Slide 3

OPENING PROMPT

How do you defend a final design?

  • Use this as a quick class discussion while attendance is taken.
  • Think first. Why does design review matter in engineering work?
  • Quick class share
  • Raise your hand and share one way design review could improve a project.
  • Student answers might include: clearer decisions, better tests, stronger evidence, or better communication.
  • We will reveal a working definition on the next slide.
Reveal answer / debrief

OPENING DEBRIEF

  • Design Review
  • A working definition for this lesson.
  • Design Review helps engineers make work clear, testable, and defendable.
  • Clarify
  • Make the goal and evidence easier to understand.
  • Plan
  • Decide what should happen before building.
  • Check
  • Use criteria, data, or feedback to judge the work.
  • Explain
  • Communicate the decision with evidence.
  • Today's mission: practice design review so the next design step is stronger.

VOTE REVEAL

Slide 11

MINI CHALLENGE

What Evidence Belongs in a Review?

  • Read the claim. Select the two strongest supports.
  • Claim:
  • “Our final launch pad meets the challenge criteria and constraints.”
  • Vote for the two strongest supports.
  • A
  • Final labeled sketch and prototype photo
  • B
  • A statement that the team worked hard
  • C
  • Test results for fit, support, and stability
  • D
  • A list of favorite colors
  • E
  • A teammate saying the design is the best
  • Vote first. Then we will reveal the best evidence pair.
Reveal answer / debrief

EVIDENCE REVEAL

  • What Evidence Belongs in a Review?
  • The best support connects directly to the claim.
  • Best support: A + C
  • A
  • Final labeled sketch and prototype photo
  • Shows the final design clearly.
  • C
  • Test results for fit, support, and stability
  • Shows performance against the criteria.
  • Useful, but not enough alone: effort and opinions do not prove the design meets the requirements.
Slide 13

SCENARIO RESPONSE

What Should the Team Do Next?

  • Choose the strongest next step and explain your reasoning.
  • A team has a finished prototype but their design review does not explain why changes were made.
  • A
  • Present only the final prototype.
  • B
  • Say the changes are obvious.
  • C
  • Add evidence showing the original problem, test results, changes, and final justification.
  • D
  • Remove the old evidence because it shows mistakes.
  • Vote first. Then we will reveal the strongest next step.
Reveal answer / debrief

SCENARIO REVEAL

  • What Should the Team Do Next?
  • The best next step keeps the process evidence-based.
  • Best answer: C
  • Add evidence showing the original problem, test results, changes, and final justification.
  • Organize
  • Gather evidence from the process.
  • Explain
  • Connect changes to test results.
  • Justify
  • Show how the final design meets criteria.
  • Engineering move: a design review defends the final design by showing the evidence behind it.
