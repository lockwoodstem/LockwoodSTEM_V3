OPENING PROMPT

How do you build carefully from a plan?

  • Use this as a quick class discussion while attendance is taken.
  • Think first. Why does prototype building matter in engineering work?
  • Quick class share
  • Raise your hand and share one way prototype building could improve a project.
  • Student answers might include: clearer decisions, better tests, stronger evidence, or better communication.
  • We will reveal a working definition on the next slide.
Reveal answer / debrief

OPENING DEBRIEF

  • Prototype Building
  • A working definition for this lesson.
  • Prototype Building helps engineers make work clear, testable, and defendable.
  • Clarify
  • Make the goal and evidence easier to understand.
  • Plan
  • Decide what should happen before building.
  • Check
  • Use criteria, data, or feedback to judge the work.
  • Explain
  • Communicate the decision with evidence.
  • Today's mission: practice prototype building so the next design step is stronger.

Slide 11

MINI CHALLENGE

What Evidence Shows Good Building?

  • Read the claim. Select the two strongest supports.
  • Claim:
  • “Our prototype was built carefully from the approved plan.”
  • Vote for the two strongest supports.
  • A
  • A photo or sketch of the prototype in progress
  • B
  • A statement that the team worked fast
  • C
  • Notes explaining one change from the plan
  • D
  • A photo of decorations only
  • E
  • No record until the end
  • Vote first. Then we will reveal the best evidence pair.
Reveal answer / debrief

EVIDENCE REVEAL

  • What Evidence Shows Good Building?
  • The best support connects directly to the claim.
  • Best support: A + C
  • A
  • A photo or sketch of the prototype in progress
  • Shows physical progress and structure.
  • C
  • Notes explaining one change from the plan
  • Shows how the team handled real build conditions.
  • Useful, but not enough alone: speed and decoration do not prove careful construction.

SCENARIO RESPONSE

What Should the Team Do Next?

  • A
  • Ignore it until the final test.
  • B
  • Add decoration to hide the weak area.
  • C

  • D
  • Throw away the prototype without noting the issue.
  • Vote first. Then we will reveal the strongest next step.
Reveal answer / debrief

SCENARIO REVEAL

  • What Should the Team Do Next?
  • The best next step keeps the process evidence-based.
  • Best answer: C
  • Pause, record the problem, reinforce the support, and document the change.
  • Pause
  • Do not continue building on a weak structure.
  • Record
  • Capture the problem as evidence.
  • Reinforce
  • Improve the prototype and explain why.
  • Engineering move: build problems become useful evidence when they are recorded and addressed.
