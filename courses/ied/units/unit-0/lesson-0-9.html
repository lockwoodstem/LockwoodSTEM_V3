Test & Improve

Focus Question: How can test evidence guide a meaningful design improvement?

Mission Briefing

Today’s Plan

Every task advances the same Rocket Launch Pad Design Challenge.

Success Looks Like…

Your team can point to a specific test result, explain what it revealed, and show one change made because of that evidence.

Project connection: Your work today becomes evidence used in the next stage of the Engineering Design Process.
Test

Use the same procedure for every team

DefineImagineSelectDevelopBuildTestImproveCommunicate

A test is useful when it produces evidence that can change a decision.

Qualification Sequence

Prototype testing

1. Dimensional Check

Footprint ≤ 12 in × 12 in; pad height ≤ 12 in without the rocket.

2. Rocket Fit

The provided rocket installs, remains upright, and can be removed without damage.

3. Level Stability

Rocket + pad remain freestanding and unsupported for 60 seconds.

4. Required Tilt

Hold at 10° for 5 seconds in forward, backward, left, and right directions.

5. Disturbance

Complete the instructor-controlled disturbance test without tipping, losing the rocket, or experiencing structural failure.

Analyze

Find the weakness that matters most

Use evidence

  • Where did movement first appear?
  • Did the base slide, rotate, or tip?
  • Did the rocket move inside its support?
  • Did a brace bend, buckle, or detach?
  • Which direction was least stable?

Choose one improvement

Make one targeted change that responds to a specific observation. Do not redesign the entire project.

Examples: move a support, reinforce a joint, add/reposition a brace, change a rocket-contact point, or modify the base geometry within the size limit.

Engineering Change Record

Evidence → Change → Expected Effect

Record the test evidence that triggered the change, describe exactly what you changed, and predict what should improve during the final competition.

Core Constraints

  • Use the provided rocket; do not modify it.
  • 12 in × 12 in maximum footprint.
  • 12 in maximum pad height, not including the rocket.
  • Rocket must be installable and removable.
  • Use only approved team-kit structural materials.
