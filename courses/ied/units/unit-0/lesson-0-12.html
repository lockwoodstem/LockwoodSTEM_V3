Opening Prompt

How can a portfolio show growth?

A portfolio is not a pile of finished pages; it is an evidence trail across the design process.

Quick class share: Which piece of Unit 0 evidence best shows a change in your thinking or design?

Reveal the portfolio purpose

A strong portfolio shows the path from idea to evidence-based improvement.

Reflection helps identify which habits should carry into the next challenge.

Portfolio Audit

Check the full evidence trail

Use the list to find missing, incomplete, or unclear work before writing the reflection.

Reflection

Use specific evidence—not generic praise

A reflection should explain decisions, challenges, improvements, and future actions.

Explain

  • How the team used the design process
  • Your strongest contribution
  • The biggest challenge
  • The improvement with the biggest impact
  • What you would change with more time

Support with

  • Specific examples
  • Test results or observations
  • Clear connection to notebook evidence
  • Honest discussion of problems
  • Recognition of teamwork
Quick Check

Which reflection is stronger?

Vote before opening the answer.

A. “The project went well and our team worked hard.”

B. “Our first base tipped during the stability test. I suggested widening it by 2 in, and the revised prototype remained upright in the retest.”

Reveal the stronger reflection

B is stronger.

It names the problem, contribution, change, and result. A may be true, but it does not provide evidence of learning or improvement.
