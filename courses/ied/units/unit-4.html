IED Unit 4

Aerospace Mechanisms, Materials & Electromechanical Systems

Unit mission: Investigate materials, mechanisms, motion, friction, data, statistics, circuits, motors, and electromechanical integration through an aerospace prototype challenge.

Lesson MapBack to IED Hub
Unit Focus

Build and defend a functional aerospace mechanism or electromechanical system prototype.

TestIntegrateRevise

Every lesson builds evidence for the final technical demonstration and design documentation.

Unit Overview

What Unit 4 Builds

You will connect physical science, materials, mechanisms, circuits, and data analysis to an engineering prototype.

Materials + Testing

You will compare material properties, test samples, interpret measurement error, and make evidence-based selections.

Mechanisms + Motion

You will explore linkages, hinges, cams, gears, pulleys, friction, forces, motion graphs, and system behavior.

Electromechanical Prototype

You will integrate simple circuits or motors, develop test protocols, analyze data, revise, and demonstrate a working system.
