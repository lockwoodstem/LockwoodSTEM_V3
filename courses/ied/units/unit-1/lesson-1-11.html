IED Unit 1 • Lesson 1.11

Rocket Assembly Reverse Engineering

Focus question: How can engineers learn from an existing rocket assembly by carefully taking it apart?

Download PresentationFlight PlanWhat You SubmitBack to Unit 1
Mission Focus

Disassemble an existing rocket carefully to discover its parts, order, and connections.

ObserveDisassembleReassemble

The rocket can be returned to its original condition using the recorded sequence.

Mission Briefing

45-Minute Flight Plan

Use this page with the lesson presentation and your engineering notebook.

Learning Targets

  • Use a controlled sequence to examine an existing assembly.
  • Identify parts, orientation, and connection methods during disassembly.
  • Verify that the assembly can be rebuilt without relying on memory.

Success Looks Like

The disassembly record preserves the order and orientation needed for successful reassembly.

LaunchLearnApplyCheckSubmit
Opening Prompt

What information could be lost if a rocket is taken apart too quickly?

Think first, then be ready to share a short response that connects the question to engineering communication.

Reveal: why this matters

Part order, orientation, small hardware, and connection details can be lost. A controlled process preserves evidence before each component is moved.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Reverse engineering studies an existing product to understand its parts and relationships.

Key Idea 2

Careful disassembly protects components and preserves the original order.

Key Idea 3

Reassembly is the final check that the observations were complete.

Interactive Check

What should happen immediately before a component is removed?

ADiscard the component name
BRecord its position, orientation, and connection
CRemove several parts at the same time
DDesign a replacement part
Reveal Answer

B — Record its position, orientation, and connection.

Observations made before removal preserve information that may be difficult to reconstruct later.

Student Task

Reverse Engineer the Rocket Assembly

Carefully disassemble the assigned rocket to discover its components and the order in which they fit together.

  1. Record the intact rocket with a labeled overview sketch or photo before removing parts.
  2. Remove one component at a time and record its temporary name, orientation, and connection method.
  3. Keep small parts organized in the order removed.
  4. Reassemble the rocket and note any step that was difficult to reconstruct.

Quality check: The record must support successful reassembly without depending on memory or verbal instructions.

Student Deliverables

Rocket Reverse-Engineering Record

Submit one sequence record and one component summary.

  • One numbered disassembly record with labeled sketches or photos showing part order and orientation.
  • One preliminary component list that includes quantities and a reassembly confirmation.

Submission standard: Focus on observation, order, orientation, connection evidence, and successful reassembly.
