Rocket Assembly Systems

Focus question: How do multiple rocket parts work together as one assembly?

Learning Targets

Opening Prompt

What information would someone need to rebuild or improve this rocket assembly without guessing?

Think first, then be ready to share a short response that connects the lesson skill to real engineering work.

Reveal: why this matters

Assembly documentation must preserve relationships, part identity, quantity, order, and design intent so the work can be reused or improved.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A part is one individual component of a larger design.

Key Idea 2

A subassembly is a smaller group of parts that work together.

Key Idea 3

The full assembly depends on alignment, fit, order, and function.

Interactive Check

Which documentation choice best supports another engineering team?

AOnly take a finished photo
BRecord parts, order, relationships, measurements, and notes clearly
CKeep the process in your head
DSkip unclear parts because the team knows what they mean
Reveal Answer

B — record parts, order, relationships, measurements, and notes clearly.

Assembly documentation must preserve relationships, part identity, quantity, order, and design intent so the work can be reused or improved.

Engineering Workflow

Use These Moves

  1. Identify parts
  2. Record relationships
  3. Add notes
  4. Check evidence
  5. Propose improvement
Student Task

Apply the Skill

Use the rocket assembly to create evidence: part notes, assembly order, BOM details, exploded view information, or change documentation.

Record enough evidence that another person could understand what you did and why it matters.
