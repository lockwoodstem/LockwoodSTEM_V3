IED Unit 1 • Lesson 1.18

Rocket Exploded View

Focus question: How can engineers show rocket parts separated without losing how they fit together?

Download PresentationFlight PlanWhat You SubmitBack to Unit 1
Mission Focus

Separate components visually while preserving orientation, alignment, and assembly relationships.

ArrangeAlignReference

A reader can mentally return every component to its assembled position.

Mission Briefing

45-Minute Flight Plan

Use this page with the lesson presentation and your engineering notebook.

Learning Targets

  • Explain how an exploded view communicates part relationships.
  • Separate components along clear assembly axes without changing orientation.
  • Use item balloons and direction cues to identify and reconnect parts.

Success Looks Like

The separated parts remain aligned and their assembly order is clear.

LaunchLearnApplyCheckSubmit
Opening Prompt

Why should parts remain in their assembled orientation in an exploded view?

Think first, then be ready to share a short response that connects the question to engineering communication.

Reveal: why this matters

Orientation communicates how surfaces, holes, and interfaces fit together. Rotating parts only to fill the page can hide the true assembly relationship.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

An exploded view separates parts so each component can be seen.

Key Idea 2

Alignment preserves the relationship between separated components.

Key Idea 3

Item balloons and assembly-direction cues help readers identify and reconnect parts.

Interactive Check

Which exploded-view arrangement communicates assembly most clearly?

AParts scattered randomly and rotated to fit the page
BParts separated along clear axes while keeping their assembled orientation
COnly the outermost component shown
DAll item numbers removed
Reveal Answer

B — Parts separated along clear axes while keeping their assembled orientation.

Alignment and orientation allow the reader to understand where each component belongs.

Student Task

Draw a Functional Rocket Exploded View

Create an exploded drawing using the component names and item numbers established in Lesson 1.17.

  1. Choose a primary assembly axis and place the main components in their assembled orientation.
  2. Separate parts with enough spacing to see interfaces while preserving alignment.
  3. Add item balloons that match the established component numbers.
  4. Use direction arrows or brief notes where insertion direction is not obvious.

Quality check: A reader must be able to mentally reassemble the rocket by following the alignments and item references.

Student Deliverables

Rocket Exploded-View Drawing

Submit one exploded drawing and one concise assembly note.

  • One complete exploded view with correct orientation, spacing, alignment, and item balloons.
  • One short assembly-order or interface note for the connection most likely to be misunderstood.

Submission standard: Preserve part orientation and use the established item numbers consistently throughout the drawing.
