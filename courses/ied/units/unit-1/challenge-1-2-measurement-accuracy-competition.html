Measurement Accuracy Competition

Measure a set of mystery parts as accurately and consistently as possible.

Beginner45 minutesCompetition
Back to Challenge HubDownload Challenge Brief (PDF)
Engineering Design Brief

Work independently or with an approved partner.

Read the complete brief before gathering materials or beginning construction.

Background

Engineering Context

Quality inspectors must make fast, repeatable measurements and report them with the correct precision.

Objectives

What You Will Practice

  • Select appropriate measuring tools
  • Read scales and calipers correctly
  • Record units and precision
  • Analyze measurement variation
Materials

What You Need

  • 10-15 mystery objects
  • Ruler
  • Dial or digital calipers
  • Measurement data sheet
  • Calculator
Procedure

Student Instructions

  1. Create a data table with object ID, feature, tool, measurement, and units.
  2. Measure each assigned feature once without seeing the reference value.
  3. Repeat three selected measurements after a five-minute delay.
  4. Calculate the difference between repeated measurements.
  5. Compare your results with instructor reference values after all data are submitted.
  6. Calculate absolute error for each reference measurement.
  7. Identify the tool and feature associated with your largest error.
  8. Write a two-step plan for improving measurement reliability.
Requirements

Engineering Requirements

  • Measure at least ten features
  • Use the most appropriate available tool
  • Record all values with units and realistic precision
  • Repeat at least three measurements
  • Calculate error or variation correctly
Constraints

Constraints & Safety

  • Do not alter or mark the objects
  • Do not share measurements before the class reveal
  • Use safe caliper pressure on fragile parts
Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Completed measurement table
  • Repeated-measurement comparison
  • Absolute error calculations
  • Accuracy ranking or score
  • Improvement plan
Reflection

Reflection Questions

  1. When was a ruler sufficient?
  2. When were calipers necessary?
  3. Did your repeated measurements agree?
  4. What caused your largest error?
Download PDF
Engineering Tip

Close calipers gently. Excess force can compress a part or tilt the jaws.

Common Mistake

Extra decimal places do not make a measurement accurate. Report only the precision supported by the tool.

Stretch Goal

Calculate the class mean and range for three measurements and identify possible outliers.

Career Connection

Quality technicians, machinists, and manufacturing engineers rely on repeatable inspection data to approve parts.

Before You Submit
  • Notebook evidence included
  • Requirements checked
  • Testing documented
  • Reflection completed
