Final Rocket Assembly Proposal Presentation

Focus question: How can a team justify a proposed rocket assembly improvement using engineering evidence?

Opening Prompt

What makes an engineering presentation persuasive rather than merely polished?

Think first, then be ready to share a short response that connects the question to engineering communication.

Reveal: why this matters

The presentation connects a specific claim to credible evidence and explains how the proposed change will be implemented and tested.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A design proposal connects the current assembly, evidence, and recommended change.

Key Idea 2

Technical visuals should support the argument rather than decorate the slides.

Key Idea 3

A professional team can explain limitations and answer questions honestly.

Lesson Interactive

Match Claims to Technical Evidence

Plan a defensible final proposal by pairing each claim with the document that provides the strongest direct evidence.

Presentation ClaimStrongest Evidence
“The revised feature fits within the required size range.”
“The audience can understand how the rocket parts connect.”
“The proposed improvement addresses a documented problem.”
“The final package accounts for every component and quantity.”
Strong presentations establish the problem, present the change, and support each major claim with direct evidence.
Your Task

Present and Defend the Rocket Improvement Proposal

Assemble the strongest evidence from the unit into a professional team proposal.

  1. Organize the presentation around the current problem, evidence, proposed change, expected benefit, and verification plan.
  2. Select the drawings, measurements, BOM information, assembly evidence, and ECR details that directly support the argument.
  3. Assign meaningful speaking responsibilities to every team member.
  4. Rehearse within the time limit and prepare responses to likely questions about feasibility, risk, and evidence.

Quality check: Every major claim must be supported by a relevant technical document or documented observation.

What You Will Submit

Final Rocket Assembly Proposal

Submit one coordinated team package and one individual reflection.

  • One team presentation with the final ECR and the supporting technical evidence needed to justify the proposal.
  • One individual reflection identifying the strongest evidence used and one improvement for future technical presentations.

Submission standard: Remove information that does not help explain or justify the proposed change.
