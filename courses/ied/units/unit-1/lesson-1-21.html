IED Unit 1 • Lesson 1.21

Engineering Change Requests

Focus question: How do engineers document a proposed design change without losing control of the original design?

Mission Focus

Use documented evidence to propose and verify one controlled design change.

Another engineer can understand what changes, why it changes, and how success will be judged.

Mission Briefing

Today’s Plan

Use this page with the lesson presentation and your engineering notebook.

Learning Targets

  • Write a clear evidence-based problem statement.
  • Document a proposed change and identify affected parts or records.
  • Create measurable acceptance criteria and a verification method.

Success Looks Like

The request separates the current design from the proposed revision and includes a testable success condition.

LaunchLearnApplyCheckSubmit
Opening Prompt

Why should an engineering change be documented instead of made directly to the original design?

Think first, then be ready to share a short response that connects the question to engineering communication.

Reveal: why this matters

A controlled record preserves the current design, explains the reason for the change, identifies affected information, and makes the revision traceable and testable.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

An Engineering Change Request records a proposed revision without erasing the current design.

Key Idea 2

The proposal should be supported by observations, measurements, fit issues, or user needs.

Key Idea 3

Acceptance criteria define how the team will decide whether the change works.

Review question: Does the verification method define a measurable acceptance criterion, or only say that the test will be repeated?
A complete ECR can still require revision when its success criteria are not measurable.
Your Task

Develop an Engineering Change Request

Identify one evidence-based improvement to the rocket assembly and document it as a controlled proposal.

  1. Define the current problem using observations, measurements, fit issues, or user needs.
  2. Create a before-and-after sketch showing the proposed change and affected components.
  3. Identify impacts on relevant parts, materials, assembly steps, cost, risk, or performance.
  4. Write measurable acceptance criteria and a test or inspection method.

Quality check: The request must be specific enough for another engineer to implement and verify.

What You Will Submit

Rocket Engineering Change Request

Submit one controlled change document and one decision summary.

  • One completed ECR containing the problem, proposed change, reason, affected items, and a labeled before-and-after sketch.
  • One verification and recommendation summary stating the acceptance criteria and whether the team recommends approval, revision, or rejection.

Submission standard: Preserve the original design record and identify which documents would need a later revision.
