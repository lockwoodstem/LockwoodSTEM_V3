Rocket Bill of Materials

Focus question: How do engineers keep track of every part needed to build an assembly?

Mission Focus

Build a verified part list that matches both the physical rocket and its exploded view.

Mission Briefing

45-Minute Flight Plan

Use this page with the lesson presentation and your engineering notebook.

Learning Targets

  • Explain the purpose and structure of a Bill of Materials.
  • Create one row for each unique component and record the correct quantity.
  • Verify the list against the physical assembly and exploded view.

Success Looks Like

Every exploded-view item has one matching BOM row and the quantities describe one complete rocket.

LaunchLearnApplyCheckSubmit
Opening Prompt

Why should repeated identical parts appear in one BOM row rather than several separate rows?

Think first, then be ready to share a short response that connects the question to engineering communication.

Reveal: why this matters

A BOM lists each unique item once and uses the quantity field to show how many are required. This makes counting, ordering, and checking more reliable.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A Bill of Materials is a structured list of the unique parts in an assembly.

Key Idea 2

Item numbers connect the BOM to the exploded view.

Key Idea 3

Quantities must be verified against the physical assembly rather than guessed.

Which BOM issues are present?

Student Task

Build and Verify the Rocket Bill of Materials

Use the physical rocket and exploded view to create a complete parts list.

  1. Transfer each exploded-view item number into a separate BOM row.
  2. Record the quantity, standardized part name, description, and material when known.
  3. Count repeated components carefully for one complete assembly.
  4. Cross-check every row against both the exploded view and the physical rocket.

Quality check: Every ballooned component must have one BOM entry, and every BOM entry must represent a real component.

Student Deliverables

Verified Rocket Bill of Materials

Submit one verified table and one checking note.

  • One completed BOM with item number, quantity, part name, description, and known material information.
  • One verification note identifying how the physical assembly and exploded view were used to confirm the list.

Submission standard: Flag unknown information instead of guessing, and use one row per unique item.
