Core Ideas

What to Pay Attention To

Axis of a feature

A centerline shows the axis of a hole, cylinder, or other rotational feature.

Center of a circle

Two perpendicular centerlines can locate the exact center of a circular feature.

Symmetry

A centerline can show that geometry is intended to be symmetric about an axis.

Engineering Check

Stop and verify before moving on

A centerline represents an axis or center—it is not a physical edge. Do not replace visible or hidden edges with centerlines.

Your Task

Complete the Centerlines & Symmetry Worksheet

Work through the worksheet problems by identifying circular, cylindrical, and symmetric features. Add centerlines only where they communicate a useful center, axis, or line of symmetry.

  1. Identify every circular, cylindrical, or clearly symmetric feature.
  2. Decide whether a centerline is needed in each view.
  3. Draw centerlines using a consistent long-short-long pattern.
  4. Extend centerlines slightly beyond the feature when appropriate.
  5. Review the drawing to make sure line types are visually distinct.

Use evidence: Keep sketches, measurements, observations, and revisions visible. Unit 1 is building one connected engineering documentation package.
