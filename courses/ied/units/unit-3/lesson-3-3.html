Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Inserted parts are not automatically assembled.

Key Idea 2

Orientation errors can hide until later if they are not checked early.

Key Idea 3

Reference geometry gives a controlled starting point.

Student Task

Insert and Rough-Position Rocket Components

Bring the major rocket parts into the assembly and place them in a recognizable starting arrangement.

  1. Insert the body and establish it as the assembly reference.
  2. Insert the remaining major parts from the correct files.
  3. Use move or align tools to place components near their intended locations.
  4. Inspect the assembly for missing or duplicated parts.

Quality check: Every required component is present once and oriented consistently with the rocket body.
