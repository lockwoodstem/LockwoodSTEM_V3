03 / Practical walkthrough

Follow the lesson workflow

Guided sequence from the assignment

  1. 1. Open the hinge assembly and identify the revolute joint in the Browser.
  2. 2. Edit the revolute-joint limits to the instructor-assigned minimum and maximum angles.
  3. 3. Drive or drag the hinge to both limits; verify it cannot continue outside the allowed range.
  4. 4. Open the slider assembly and apply the instructor-assigned minimum and maximum travel distances.
  5. 5. Drive the carriage to each endpoint and check that it stops without unintended rotation.
  6. 6. Capture the joint-limit settings and the resulting endpoint behavior.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Identify physical end stops and allowable travel.
  • Set revolute-joint angular limits.
  • Set slider-joint linear limits.
  • Use joint motion to verify both end positions.

Unit 3 Progression

With revolute and slider joints working, the next step is restricting motion to a realistic range.

Assignment

Constrain the Hinge and Slider Travel

Open your saved mechanisms from Lessons 3.1 and 3.2. Your instructor will provide the allowed hinge angles and carriage travel distance; do not guess the final stop values.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open the hinge assembly and identify the revolute joint in the Browser.
  2. Edit the revolute-joint limits to the instructor-assigned minimum and maximum angles.
  3. Drive or drag the hinge to both limits; verify it cannot continue outside the allowed range.
  4. Open the slider assembly and apply the instructor-assigned minimum and maximum travel distances.
  5. Drive the carriage to each endpoint and check that it stops without unintended rotation.
  6. Capture the joint-limit settings and the resulting endpoint behavior.

Before You Submit

  • Revolute limits match the specified angles.
  • Slider limits match the specified travel distances.
  • Both mechanisms reach and stop at their intended endpoints.
Submission

What to Submit

Upload the following specific items to the Lesson 3.3 assignment in Google Classroom:

  • Joint-limit evidence (.pdf): submit LastName_FirstName_3_3_JointLimits.pdf containing four labeled images: hinge joint-limit settings, hinge at its maximum stop, slider joint-limit settings, and slider at its maximum stop. Add the assigned min/max values with units.
  • Save work in Fusion: save both updated assemblies under their existing design names. No duplicate .f3d upload is required for this lesson.

Submission check: Your submission must show the exact numeric limits, not just the assembled model.
