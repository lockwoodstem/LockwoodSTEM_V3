Digital fit checks reduce failed prints.

Alignment should be inspected, not assumed.

Key Idea 3

Student Task

Check Alignment and Planned Fit

Use section views, measurements, and visual inspection to verify that connected components align and have the intended clearances.

  1. Identify the critical mating surfaces.
  2. Measure or inspect alignment between connected parts.
  3. Check for overlap, unintended gaps, or off-axis placement.
  4. Record one confirmed fit or one issue requiring revision.

Quality check: The fit check uses visible or measured evidence rather than appearance alone.
