IED Unit 3 • Lesson 3.14

Final Rocket Assembly Design Review

Focus question: How can engineers defend an assembly design using evidence from CAD, prototyping, testing, and documentation?

Go to AssignmentWhat to SubmitBack to Unit 3
Final Design Review

Present the final rocket assembly as an evidence-based engineering design, not simply a finished model.

Expected result: A concise final design review that uses the physical prototype, CAD assembly, drawing package, and revision evidence to support major claims.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Present the final assembly structure and key interfaces clearly.
  • Use digital and physical evidence to support design claims.
  • Explain one meaningful prototype-driven revision.
  • Respond to questions using the final engineering documentation.

Unit 3 Progression

Unit 3 concludes by bringing together component organization, assembly relationships, verification, prototype evidence, revision control, and professional documentation.

ComponentsAssembleVerifyPrototypeReviseDocument
Assignment

Present the Final Rocket Assembly

Use the final design package from Lesson 3.13 to present the completed project. Focus on evidence and engineering decisions rather than narrating every step.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Introduce the assembly: Identify the major components and explain the intended overall assembly structure.
  2. Show the CAD evidence: Use the final assembly to demonstrate component organization, joints/relationships, alignment, and key interfaces.
  3. Show verification evidence: Present one meaningful interference, section, measurement, or symmetry check.
  4. Show the physical prototype: Identify what the prototype confirmed and any issue it revealed.
  5. Explain the engineering change: Present the original problem, the documented revision, and the evidence that supports the final solution.
  6. Show the documentation: Use the assembly drawing, exploded view, BOM, balloons, notes, and revision information to explain how another person could understand the design.
  7. Answer questions: Defend major choices using specific project evidence rather than opinion.
  8. Submit the final presentation evidence: Follow the instructor's required format for the design review.

Before You Submit

  • The final CAD and prototype shown match the final revision.
  • At least one digital verification result is included.
  • The prototype-driven revision is clearly explained.
  • The assembly drawing/BOM is visible and readable.
  • Claims are supported by project evidence.
Submission

What You Will Submit

  • Final design review: instructor-observed presentation or assigned presentation file.
  • Final design package: confirm the Lesson 3.13 package is complete and accessible for grading.
