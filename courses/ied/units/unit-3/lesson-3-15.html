Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Parts lists connect item numbers to real components.

Key Idea 2

Balloons help readers identify which part is which.

Key Idea 3

Notes and revision blocks make drawings more usable and traceable.

Your Task

Complete Assembly Identification and Revision Information

Add balloons, a parts list, notes, and revision information so another person can identify and assemble the components.

  1. Create or verify the parts list from the assembly.
  2. Place non-overlapping balloons that match the item numbers.
  3. Add only the notes needed for assembly or interpretation.
  4. Complete the revision information for documented changes.

Quality check: Every balloon matches the parts list and the notes provide useful information not already obvious from the geometry.
