IED Unit 3 • Lesson 3.2

Slider Joints & Linear Motion

Focus question: How can an assembly constrain a part to move along a single straight path?

Go to AssignmentWhat to SubmitBack to Unit 3
Motion Foundations

Build a linear-slide mechanism using a slider joint instead of repeating rigid assembly setup.

Expected result: A grounded slide rail and carriage with only one intentional linear degree of freedom.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Distinguish linear translation from rotation.
  • Select and orient the axis for a slider joint.
  • Use rigid joints for nonmoving accessories.
  • Test and correct unwanted component movement.

Unit 3 Progression

Lesson 3.1 introduced rotation. This lesson adds a second fundamental type of constrained motion: translation.

Assignment

Linear Carriage: Slider Joint Challenge

Use the separate teacher-provided linear-slide kit: track/base, carriage, handle, and two travel stops (or the equivalent assigned components).

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Insert each supplied component into a new Fusion design named LastName_FirstName_3_2_Slider.
  2. Ground the track/base and use rigid joints for the handle and any fixed stops as appropriate.
  3. Create a slider joint between the carriage and the track with the motion axis along the rail.
  4. Test the joint by dragging or using Drive Joint; verify the carriage does not rotate or shift sideways.
  5. Inspect the mechanism from front, side, and isometric views and correct any joint orientation errors.
  6. Save the assembled mechanism and demonstrate its controlled linear movement.

Before You Submit

  • The track remains grounded and the carriage is guided along the rail.
  • One intended translation is possible with no unplanned rotation.
  • All fixed accessories remain attached to their intended components.
Submission

What to Submit

Upload the following specific items to the Lesson 3 assignment in Google Classroom:

  • Fusion file (.f3d): submit LastName_FirstName_3_2_Slider.f3d with a grounded track and correctly configured slider joint.
  • Motion recording (.mp4, 10–20 seconds): show the carriage moving back and forth along the track without rotating or moving sideways.

Submission check: Do not replace the slider relationship with a rigid joint: the carriage must visibly move.
