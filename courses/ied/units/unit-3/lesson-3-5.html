Interference, Clearance & Motion Verification

Concept

The engineering idea

A clearance is a nominal geometric gap; an interference is an overlap. Check both static geometry and possible positions during motion.

03 / Practical walkthrough

Follow the lesson workflow

Guided sequence from the assignment

  1. 1. Open the fault-check mechanism and identify the fixed and moving parts.
  2. 2. Use Measure and Section Analysis to document one critical interface clearance or alignment.
  3. 3. Run Interference to identify unwanted overlap; distinguish intentional contact from fit errors.
  4. 4. Drive the crank through its motion range and locate collision, binding, or incorrect motion.
  5. 5. State the root cause (geometry, joint origin, alignment, or clearance) with evidence.
  6. 6. Correct one fault and repeat the relevant inspection and motion test.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Use Inspect/Measure to evaluate critical interfaces.
  • Use Interference to identify overlapping components.
  • Use Section Analysis when internal interfaces cannot be inspected externally.
  • Check alignment and repeated-part symmetry using measurable references.

Assignment

Crank-Slider Digital Inspection and Fault Diagnosis

Inspect the instructor-provided faulty version of the Lesson 3.4 mechanism, then correct and verify one meaningful fault.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open the fault-check mechanism and identify the fixed and moving parts.
  2. Use Measure and Section Analysis to document one critical interface clearance or alignment.
  3. Run Interference to identify unwanted overlap; distinguish intentional contact from fit errors.
  4. Drive the crank through its motion range and locate collision, binding, or incorrect motion.
  5. State the root cause (geometry, joint origin, alignment, or clearance) with evidence.
  6. Correct one fault and repeat the relevant inspection and motion test.
  7. Save a corrected version without overwriting the instructor's original kit.

Before You Submit

  • A measured interface and section view are documented.
  • The fault and its cause are identified.
  • The corrected mechanism passes the applicable recheck.
