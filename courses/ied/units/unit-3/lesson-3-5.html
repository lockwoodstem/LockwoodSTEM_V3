IED Unit 3 • Lesson 3.5

Fit, Alignment & Interference Checks

Focus question: How can engineers detect assembly problems before any parts are manufactured?

Go to AssignmentWhat to SubmitBack to Unit 3
Assembly Foundations

Use digital inspection evidence to find fit and alignment problems before fabrication.

Expected result: A verified assembly with documented issues ready to be corrected during the rocket challenge.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Use Inspect/Measure to evaluate critical interfaces.
  • Use Interference to identify overlapping components.
  • Use Section Analysis when internal interfaces cannot be inspected externally.
  • Check alignment and repeated-part symmetry using measurable references.

Unit 3 Progression

With components and joints in place, the next step is proving that the assembly can actually fit together.

ComponentsAssembleVerifyPrototypeReviseDocument
Assignment

Run a Digital Assembly Inspection

Inspect the current rocket assembly systematically. Do not assume that components fit just because the model looks correct from the outside.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Measure critical interfaces: Check the diameters, depths, offsets, and other dimensions identified by the instructor.
  2. Run Interference: Use Fusion's interference analysis on the major component set and inspect every reported overlap.
  3. Create a section analysis: Cut through the assembly to inspect at least one hidden internal connection.
  4. Check axial alignment: Confirm that components intended to share a centerline are actually concentric/aligned.
  5. Check repeated geometry: Verify that fins or other repeated parts are evenly spaced and consistently oriented.
  6. Classify problems: Separate intentional contact from actual interference, misalignment, or missing clearance.
  7. Correct obvious setup errors: Repair joint or placement mistakes. Save design changes that affect part geometry for the challenge phase.
  8. Capture evidence: Save screenshots that clearly show the inspection result.

Before You Submit

  • Critical interfaces were measured.
  • Interference analysis was completed.
  • At least one section analysis was used.
  • Alignment and symmetry were checked with evidence.
  • Actual design problems are distinguished from intentional contact.
Submission

What You Will Submit

  • Inspection evidence: screenshots of the interference check and section/alignment check.
  • Updated Fusion archive: assembly with setup errors corrected, exported as LastName_FirstName_3_5_AssemblyCheck.f3d.
  • Issue list: a concise list of any remaining geometry or fit issues that must be addressed in the design challenge.
