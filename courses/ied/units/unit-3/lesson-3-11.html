IED Unit 3 • Lesson 3.11

Engineering Change & Revision

Focus question: How do engineers control design changes after prototype testing reveals a problem?

Go to AssignmentWhat to SubmitBack to Unit 3
Revision

Use prototype evidence to justify, document, and implement a controlled design revision.

Expected result: A revised rocket assembly with an Engineering Change Request-style record connecting the physical problem to the CAD change.

Lesson Overview

What You Are Learning

Learning Targets

  • Turn prototype observations into a specific engineering problem statement.
  • Identify the affected component and interface.
  • Document the proposed change before editing the model.
  • Verify that the revised assembly resolves the original issue without creating a new one.

Unit 3 Progression

Prototype testing only becomes engineering evidence when it leads to a documented and verified design decision.

ComponentsAssembleVerifyPrototypeReviseDocument
Assignment

Create and Implement an Engineering Change

Choose a meaningful problem identified during Lesson 3.10. Document the change before modifying the final CAD assembly.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select the issue: Choose one fit, alignment, interference, or assembly problem supported by physical evidence.
  2. Describe the current condition: Identify the affected component(s), interface, and observed problem.
  3. Propose the change: State exactly what geometry, dimension, relationship, or manufacturing feature should change.
  4. Predict the effect: Explain what the change is intended to correct and what other components could be affected.
  5. Record the revision: Complete the instructor's Engineering Change Request/revision record before editing CAD.
  6. Modify the CAD: Make the approved change in the correct component, not in an outdated copy.
  7. Reassemble and inspect digitally: Repeat the relevant Measure, Interference, Section, alignment, or joint check.
  8. Update revision information: Save the revised design using the required version/revision convention.

Before You Submit

  • The revision is supported by physical prototype evidence.
  • The affected component and interface are clearly identified.
  • The proposed change was documented before CAD editing.
  • The final assembly was rechecked after the change.
  • Revision/version information is clear.
Submission

What You Will Submit

  • Engineering Change Record: completed ECR/revision documentation.
  • Revised Fusion archive: updated rocket assembly exported as LastName_FirstName_3_11_RocketAssembly_Rev.f3d.
  • Verification evidence: screenshot showing the corrected interface or inspection result.
