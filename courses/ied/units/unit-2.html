Introduction to Engineering Design · Unit 2

CAD Parts & Additive Manufacturing

Build accurate standalone parts in Fusion, apply multiple CAD tools in a printed mini-project, and use additive-manufacturing evidence to improve future designs.

Unit emphasisParametric part modeling, design verification, and additive manufacturing.
Major projectsTwo-part condensation coaster and additive-manufacturing tolerance tester.

Unit 2 Lessons

Unit 2 focuses on individual parts, CAD design intent, and additive manufacturing. Rocket-specific parts and assemblies are reserved for Unit 3.

LessonTitlePrimary Goal
2.1Getting Started with FusionLearn the Fusion interface, file organization, and navigation.
2.2Sketch Planes and 2D SketchingCreate clean 2D geometry on intentional sketch planes.
2.3Sketch ConstraintsControl sketch relationships using geometric constraints.
2.4Dimensioned CAD SketchesCombine constraints and dimensions to create precise sketches.
2.5Creating 3D Parts with Extrude — Bracket 3Create the base Bracket 3 geometry from engineering documentation.
2.6Cut Features and Holes — Bracket 3Add functional cuts, openings, and holes to Bracket 3.
2.7Fillets, Chamfers, and Design Intent — Bracket 3Complete and verify Bracket 3 using edge treatments.
2.8Creating Rotational Parts with RevolveCreate rotationally symmetric parts from a half-profile.
2.9Mirror and Pattern ToolsUse feature reuse to model symmetry and repeated geometry.
2.10Condensation-Draining Coaster Mini-ProjectDesign a two-part coaster and submit it for instructor 3D printing.
2.11Physical Part Evaluation & CAD RevisionEvaluate the printed coaster and revise the CAD model from evidence.
2.12Design for 3D PrintingEvaluate and improve CAD geometry for FDM manufacturing.
2.13Slicer Basics and Print SettingsCompare manufacturing settings using Bambu Studio.
2.14FabLab 3D Printer CertificationComplete the existing 3D printer certification.
2.15Design & Print a Manufacturing Tolerance TesterDesign a tester for fit clearance and heat-set insert sizing.
2.16Tolerance Testing & Manufacturing RecommendationsUse physical testing to establish evidence-based CAD rules.
