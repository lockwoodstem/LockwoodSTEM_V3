Introduction to Engineering Design · Unit 2

CAD Parts & Additive Manufacturing

Build accurate standalone parts in Fusion, prepare them for FDM manufacturing, and use physical testing to improve future design decisions.

Unit emphasisParametric part modeling, model verification, and additive manufacturing.
Unit end pointA tested tolerance coupon with fit and heat-set insert recommendations.

Unit 2 Lessons

This unit focuses on individual parts. Rocket-specific modeling and assemblies are reserved for Unit 3.

LessonTitlePrimary Goal
2.1Getting Started with FusionLearn the Fusion interface, organize files correctly, and move confidently around a 3D model.
2.2Sketch Planes and 2D SketchingCreate clean 2D sketch geometry on an intentional origin plane.
2.3Sketch ConstraintsControl relationships between sketch entities so geometry behaves predictably when edited.
2.4Dimensioned CAD SketchesCreate a fully defined sketch from engineering documentation using dimensions and geometric constraints.
2.5Creating 3D Parts with Extrude — Bracket 3Use the existing Bracket 3 documentation from Lesson 1.5 to create the base solid in Fusion.
2.6Cut Features and Holes — Bracket 3Continue Bracket 3 by adding openings, cut features, and engineered holes.
2.7Fillets, Chamfers, and Design Intent — Bracket 3Finish Bracket 3 and verify the complete model against the original engineering documentation.
2.8Creating Rotational Parts with RevolveModel a standalone rotational component from a dimensioned half-profile.
2.9Mirror and Pattern ToolsUse feature reuse to model a patterned equipment mounting plate efficiently.
2.10Independent Parametric Part ModelingCreate a complete standalone manufactured component without a step-by-step modeling procedure.
2.11CAD Model Check and RevisionReview the Lesson 2.10 model for accuracy, robustness, and model organization.
2.12Design for 3D PrintingEvaluate whether a CAD model is appropriate for FDM manufacturing and improve problematic geometry.
2.13Slicer Basics and Print SettingsUse Bambu Studio to compare manufacturing settings through a controlled slicing investigation.
2.14FabLab 3D Printer CertificationComplete the existing LockwoodSTEM 3D Printer Certification.
2.15Design & Print a Manufacturing Tolerance TesterCreate and print a controlled test coupon for peg/hole clearance and heat-set insert hole sizing.
2.16Tolerance Testing & Manufacturing RecommendationsUse physical testing to establish evidence-based fit and heat-set insert design recommendations.
