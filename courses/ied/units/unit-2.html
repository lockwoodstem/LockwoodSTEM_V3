IED Unit 2

Parametric CAD and Part Design

Unit mission: Build accurate CAD parts, prepare models for fabrication, and use a 3D printed tolerance tester to recommend future assembly clearances.

Lesson MapBack to IED Hub
Unit Focus

Model, print, test, and revise using evidence from CAD and physical tolerances.

ModelPrintTest

Every lesson builds toward a printable tolerance tester and a clearance recommendation for future rocket assemblies.

Unit Overview

What Unit 2 Builds

Students move from sketch-based documentation into controlled, editable, fabrication-ready CAD models.

CAD Control

Sketch constraints, dimensions, parameters, and features make models precise and editable.

Aerospace Parts

Nose cones, fins, mounting features, and printed testers connect CAD tools to aerospace design decisions.

Fabrication Evidence

Slicer previews, print settings, measurements, and fit tests connect digital models to physical parts.
