IED Unit 2 • 16 Lessons

CAD Parts & Additive Manufacturing

Develop accurate parametric CAD models, apply those skills in a two-part printed coaster project, and use manufacturing evidence to improve future designs.

Start Lesson 2.1Back to IED Hub
Unit Progression

From CAD fundamentals to tested manufactured parts

SketchModelPatternPrototypeManufactureTest

Unit 2 focuses on standalone part modeling and additive manufacturing. Rocket-specific parts and assemblies remain in Unit 3.

How the Unit Fits Together

Four Focus Areas

Lessons 2.1–2.4

Control Sketches

Learn Fusion navigation, sketch planes, geometric constraints, and dimensioned CAD sketches.

Lessons 2.5–2.9

Build Parametric Parts

Use Extrude, Cut, Hole, Fillet, Chamfer, Revolve, Mirror, and Pattern in progressively more complete models.

Lessons 2.10–2.11

Prototype & Revise

Design a two-part condensation coaster, receive the printed prototype, and revise the CAD model from physical evidence.

Lessons 2.12–2.16

Manufacture & Test

Learn design for FDM, slicing, certification, tolerance testing, heat-set inserts, and evidence-based manufacturing recommendations.

Major Mini-Project

Two-Part Condensation-Draining Coaster

In Lesson 2.10, you will design a removable patterned insert and lower condensation tray within an 80 mm maximum footprint and 15 mm maximum assembled thickness. The instructor will print the design so you can evaluate and revise it in Lesson 2.11.
