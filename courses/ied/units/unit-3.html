IED Unit 3 • 14 Lessons

Assembly Design, Prototyping & Documentation

Unit mission: Build on the rigid-joint and exploded-view skills from Unit 2 by learning revolute joints, slider joints, motion limits, and mechanical verification; then design, manufacture, revise, and test a modular compressed-air rocket.

Unit Progression

From moving mechanisms to a flight-tested modular rocket

MotionJoint LimitsVerifyRocket CADManufactureFlight Test

Unit 3 applies the drawing and manufacturing skills from the Puzzle Cube Challenge to a real multi-part rocket assembly with removable interfaces, laser-cut fins, and a final compressed-air flight test.

How the Unit Fits Together

Four Focus Areas

Lessons 3.1–3.5

Motion-Based Assembly Skills

Build revolute and slider mechanisms from teacher-provided parts, apply joint limits, combine different relationships, and diagnose fit and motion faults.

Lessons 3.6–3.8

Compressed-Air Rocket Design

Plan the modular rocket, design the 3D-printed components and laser-cut fins, and complete a formal digital design review.

Lessons 3.9–3.11

Manufacture, Assemble & Revise

Prepare STL/DXF files, assemble and fit-test the physical rocket, and complete an engineering change before flight.

Lessons 3.12–3.14

Document, Inspect & Flight Test

Create the assembly drawing/BOM, pass a formal flight-readiness review, and complete the teacher-controlled compressed-air flight test.

Lessons 3.1–3.5 develop motion-based assembly skills using provided mechanisms; Lessons 3.6–3.14 form one continuous modular rocket project.

Major Project

Modular Compressed-Air Rocket Assembly & Flight Test Challenge

Students design a fully 3D-printed modular rocket with laser-cut fins, create at least two removable printed interfaces, verify compatibility with a 0.840 in (21.34 mm) OD PVC launch tube, manufacture and assemble the rocket, complete a prototype-driven engineering change, document the final assembly, and finish with a teacher-controlled compressed-air flight test.
