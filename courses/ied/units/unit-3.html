IED Unit 3 • 14 Lessons

Assembly Design, Prototyping & Documentation

Unit mission: Move from the multi-body modeling skills of Unit 2 into true component-based assemblies, then design, manufacture, revise, and flight-test a modular compressed-air rocket.

Start Lesson 3.1Back to IED Hub
Unit Progression

From components to a flight-tested modular rocket

ComponentsJointsVerifyManufactureReviseFlight Test

Unit 3 applies the drawing and manufacturing skills from the Puzzle Cube Challenge to a real multi-part rocket assembly with removable interfaces, laser-cut fins, and a final compressed-air flight test.

How the Unit Fits Together

Four Focus Areas

Lessons 3.1–3.5

Assembly Foundations

Move from bodies to components, organize assembly files, position parts, apply joints, and use interference/alignment checks.

Lessons 3.6–3.8

Compressed-Air Rocket Design

Plan the modular rocket, design the 3D-printed components and laser-cut fins, and complete a formal digital design review.

Lessons 3.9–3.11

Manufacture, Assemble & Revise

Prepare STL/DXF files, assemble and fit-test the physical rocket, and complete an engineering change before flight.

Lessons 3.12–3.14

Document, Inspect & Flight Test

Create the assembly drawing/BOM, pass a formal flight-readiness review, and complete the teacher-controlled compressed-air flight test.

Major Project

Modular Compressed-Air Rocket Assembly & Flight Test Challenge

Students design a fully 3D-printed modular rocket with laser-cut fins, create at least two removable printed interfaces, verify compatibility with a 1.050 in (26.67 mm) OD PVC launch tube, manufacture and assemble the rocket, complete a prototype-driven engineering change, document the final assembly, and finish with a teacher-controlled compressed-air flight test.
