IED Unit 3 • 14 Lessons

Assembly Design, Prototyping & Documentation

Unit mission: Move from the multi-body modeling skills of Unit 2 into true component-based assemblies, then verify, prototype, revise, and document a complete rocket assembly.

Start Lesson 3.1Back to IED Hub
Unit Progression

From parts to a verified and documented assembly

ComponentsJointsVerifyPrototypeReviseDocument

Unit 3 no longer repeats the drawing and STL-export skills introduced in the Puzzle Cube Challenge. Those skills are applied here at the assembly-system level.

How the Unit Fits Together

Four Focus Areas

Lessons 3.1–3.5

Assembly Foundations

Move from bodies to components, organize assembly files, position parts, apply joints, and use interference/alignment checks.

Lessons 3.6–3.8

Rocket Assembly Challenge

Plan, build, and formally verify the complete rocket assembly before manufacturing.

Lessons 3.9–3.11

Prototype & Revise

Prepare print files, assemble the physical prototype, collect fit evidence, and complete an engineering change.

Lessons 3.12–3.14

Document & Defend

Create assembly-level documentation with an exploded view, BOM, balloons, notes, revisions, and a final design review.
