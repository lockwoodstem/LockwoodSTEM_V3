IED Unit 5

Human-Centered Aerospace Design Capstone

Unit mission: Design, prototype, test, revise, and present an aerospace solution that responds to real user needs while considering safety, ethics, sustainability, life cycle, and impact.

Lesson MapResourcesBack to IED Hub
Unit Project

Complete a human-centered aerospace capstone and final engineering portfolio.

ResearchPrototypePresent

Every lesson builds evidence for the final portfolio, prototype review, and capstone presentation.

Unit Overview

What Unit 5 Builds

Students connect user research, ethics, sustainability, project management, prototyping, testing, feedback, revision, portfolio evidence, and professional presentation.

Human-Centered Research

Students define stakeholders, user needs, criteria, constraints, and a design brief before choosing a solution.

Prototype + Feedback

Students generate concepts, select a final direction, plan fabrication, build, test, collect feedback, and revise.

Responsible Communication

Students evaluate ethics, safety, sustainability, and life cycle, then assemble a portfolio and final presentation.
