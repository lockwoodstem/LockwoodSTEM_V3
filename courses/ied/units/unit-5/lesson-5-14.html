Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A working design is not automatically a responsible design.

Key Idea 2

Engineers consider who benefits, who may be affected, and what happens over the product life cycle.

Key Idea 3

Responsible design decisions should be stated clearly in the final documentation.

Your Task

Evaluate Responsible Design Impacts

Examine the current design for ethical, safety, sustainability, life-cycle, accessibility, and broader stakeholder effects.

  1. Identify hazards and who could be affected.
  2. Review materials, energy, waste, repair, and end-of-life considerations.
  3. Consider accessibility, fairness, privacy, or unintended consequences relevant to the project.
  4. Record one required responsibility-driven change or justified decision.

Quality check: The analysis addresses project-specific impacts and goes beyond a generic statement that the design is safe or sustainable.
