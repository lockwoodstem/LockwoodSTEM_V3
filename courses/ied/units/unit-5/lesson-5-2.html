Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Stakeholders are people affected by the design, not just people who use it directly.

Key Idea 2

User needs describe what people must accomplish, avoid, understand, or experience.

Key Idea 3

Better research leads to better criteria and more meaningful design decisions.

Student Task

Investigate Stakeholders and User Needs

Gather and organize information about the people affected by the design before defining the final problem.

  1. Identify primary and secondary stakeholders.
  2. Collect observations, interview responses, survey information, or credible secondary research.
  3. Separate stated wants from underlying needs.
  4. Summarize the most important needs and barriers.

Quality check: Each proposed user need is supported by research evidence rather than team assumptions.

Lesson Interactive

Turn Stakeholder Quotes into Design Evidence

Classify each interview statement so the team can separate user pain points, context, goals, and preferences.

Stakeholder statementEvidence type
“My fingers hurt after opening the latch several times.”
“I usually operate it outside while wearing thick gloves.”
“I need to finish the inspection in under two minutes.”
“I would rather feel a click than rely only on a light.”
Use open questions to learn about real experiences rather than confirm a solution idea.
Student Deliverables

  • One stakeholder map or research summary.
  • A prioritized list of evidence-supported user needs.
