What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Engineering teams need clear expectations, not just good intentions.

Key Idea 2

Project management helps teams turn large work into visible steps.

Key Idea 3

Accountability protects quality, fairness, and project momentum.

Your Task

Build the Team Project Plan

Establish team norms, roles, milestones, and a visible management system for completing the capstone.

  1. Agree on communication and decision-making norms.
  2. Assign initial roles and responsibilities.
  3. Break the project into milestones and due dates.
  4. Record risks, dependencies, and the next checkpoint.

Quality check: Every major task has an owner and the plan can be updated as evidence changes.

Lesson Interactive

Sequence a Team Project Plan

Move the project-management steps into an order that gives the team clear ownership, deadlines, and opportunities to adjust.

  1. Create a schedule with milestones and dependencies
  2. Define the project scope and required evidence
  3. Adjust assignments or timing using checkpoint evidence
  4. Assign responsibilities and decision ownership
  5. Review progress at a planned checkpoint

Planning rule

Define the work before assigning it. Schedule checkpoints early enough that the team can still respond to problems.
