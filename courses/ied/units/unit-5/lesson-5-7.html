Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Fabrication planning reduces wasted time and materials.

Key Idea 2

A build-ready plan includes parts, dimensions, materials, tools, and sequence.

Key Idea 3

Prototype planning should consider safety, access, and evidence collection.

Student Task

Plan CAD, Fabrication, and Prototype Work

Convert the selected concept into a practical build plan that identifies parts, materials, tools, interfaces, and readiness checks.

  1. Break the concept into parts or subsystems.
  2. Identify which items require CAD, fabrication, or purchased components.
  3. Choose available materials and tools.
  4. Create the build sequence and pre-build checks.

Quality check: The plan can be completed with available time, tools, materials, and team skills.
