4.17 Mechanical Assembly and Hand Test

Assemble the moving mechanism and test it by hand.

Today’s goal

Assemble the moving mechanism and test it by hand.

45-minute plan: 10 min demonstration, 30 min guided work, 5 min documentation and cleanup. Adjust for tool shutdown when needed.

Learn the concept

Hand-testing catches mechanical problems

Before adding electrical power, slowly move the mechanism by hand to identify friction, binding, wobble, and interference. Watch for shafts bending, belts slipping, or moving parts striking supports. Fix the simplest issue first and repeat the same hand test.

Example: If a Ferris wheel catches every half-turn, inspect the axle and cabin clearance.

Remember: Never force a stuck mechanism or use the motor to overpower a jam.

What to do

  1. Install the main moving parts.
  2. Rotate or move the mechanism by hand.
  3. Record any sticking or rubbing and adjust alignment.

Safety: Do not energize mechanisms while building, fitting, or adjusting parts. Use approved tools and fabrication procedures.
