IED Lesson 4.17 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Mechanical Assembly and Hand Test

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Install the drive and use an unpowered hand test to find binding or interference.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Binding is resistance that makes a mechanism difficult or impossible to move. Wobble is unwanted side-to-side motion. A hand test moves the mechanism slowly through its intended travel so you can see where the problem starts.

Worked Example

Observe
The wheel rubs at the same spot each turn.
Locate
Mark the spot. Check whether a cabin hits an upright or the axle shifts.
Retest
Reposition just the part involved and repeat the same turn.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

During an approved unpowered hand test, a wheel rubs at the same spot each turn. What evidence helps you troubleshoot?

What to Do

Safety: Keep fingers and clothing clear of pinch points. Use the approved hand-turn point and stop at resistance. Never use the motor to force past a jam.

  1. Mount the shaft, driven part, and motion-transfer parts on the prepared structure.
  2. Disconnect the power source. Ask your teacher before moving the mechanism slowly by hand through one full cycle.
  3. Record where it rubs, wobbles, or moves freely. Correct one fit issue if time allows, then repeat the same check.

What to Submit

One annotated sketch or photo of the assembly showing the motion path and one sentence stating what happened over the cycle, including the position of any problem.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.17.

Optional extension · up to +2 points

Plan a follow-up hand check

Use your assembly sketch to mark where you observed rubbing or wobble. If the motion was smooth, mark the smallest visible gap. Describe one unpowered check that would help you tell whether the fit changes through the cycle. Write a plan only.

Add your extension to the same submission under Extension.

+1 point: Mark the location you would check.
+1 point: Explain what observation would help you decide whether the fit needs adjustment.

Check Your Work

Can you point to where the issue happens or state explicitly that you checked the full cycle and found no contact?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
