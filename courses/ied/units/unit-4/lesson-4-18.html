IED Lesson 4.18 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Electrical Installation and Soldering

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Install the motor circuit and document the instructor’s pre-power inspection.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

The switch and motor share one circuit path. Insulation covers conductors so they cannot touch each other or the frame unintentionally. Strain relief keeps a wire from pulling on a connection. Route wires clear of moving parts and leave the switch easy to reach.

Worked Example

On a Swing Ride, a lead runs beside the rotating shaft. With power disconnected, reroute and secure it to a stationary upright, then check clearance through a full hand cycle before the instructor authorizes power.

What to Do

Safety: Under direct instructor supervision, solder only at a checked station with working local fume extraction, eye protection, and a heat-safe stand. Disconnect the source before wiring, never solder battery cells, and energize only after approval.

  1. Mount the motor and switch. With power disconnected, route wires away from the moving assembly.
  2. Make the connections using the approved method. Solder only after the instructor’s station and skills checks.
  3. Ask for a pre-power inspection. If approved, perform a brief ON/OFF test and disconnect power.

What to Submit

One annotated wiring photo or sketch labeling source, switch, motor, and wire routing. Add the instructor check outcome and observed ON/OFF result, or record why power was withheld.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.18.

Check Your Work

Are connections insulated, wires secured away from motion, and the switch reachable without crossing moving parts?

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
