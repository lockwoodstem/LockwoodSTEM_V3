IED Lesson 4.3 · Materials and Testing

Simple Materials Test

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Measure and compare the deflection of two samples in a fair test.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Deflection is the change in position under load. Measure the center position with no load, then measure it again with the load. Use the difference in millimeters. In a fair test, change only the sample material and keep the dimensions, support spacing, load, and measurement location the same.

Worked Example

Before loading
Center at 38 mm
With load
Center at 34 mm
Deflection
38 mm − 34 mm = 4 mm downward. A strip that moves 7 mm in the same setup bends more.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

A strip’s center reads 52 mm without a load and 47 mm with the load. What deflection should you record?

What to Do

Safety: Wear eye protection. Use the assigned load and a stable bench. Keep hands and feet away from suspended loads and stop if a sample cracks or slips.

  1. Set up two equal-size samples on the supports at your station and mark the center measurement location.
  2. Use the assigned load for both trials on each sample. Remove it between trials.
  3. Record all four deflections and identify which sample bent less overall.

What to Submit

One four-row table (material, trial, unloaded reading, loaded reading, and deflection in mm), plus one sentence comparing the samples.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.3.

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
Optional extension · up to +2 points

Compare repeat readings

Use your four existing trials. For each material, subtract the smaller deflection from the larger one. Explain which material’s repeated readings were closer, or whether they were tied.

Add your extension to the same submission under Extension.

+1 point: Show both differences with units.
+1 point: Use those differences to compare consistency without claiming either material is stronger.
