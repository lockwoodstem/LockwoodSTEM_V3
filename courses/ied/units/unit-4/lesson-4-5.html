IED Lesson 4.5 · Mechanisms and Motion

Types of Motion

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Classify common motions and mark each part’s path on a simple sketch.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Rotary motion turns around an axis, an imaginary line through the rotation. Linear motion follows a straight path. Repeated straight back-and-forth movement is reciprocating motion. Oscillating motion swings back and forth about a pivot without completing a turn.

Worked Example

A Pirate Ship’s motor shaft rotates continuously. The ship rocks around a fixed pivot, reverses direction at each end of its path, and oscillates. Draw a full curved arrow for the shaft and a two-ended arc at the ship pivot.

What to Do

Safety: Move classroom models slowly by hand with power disconnected. Keep fingers away from joints.

  1. Observe four teacher demonstrations or hand-operated models.
  2. Sketch the path of one moving part in each and mark direction with arrows.
  3. Label each term once and circle the example closest to your chosen or assigned ride’s main motion.

What to Submit

One page with four labeled path sketches and one short note connecting the circled sketch to a park ride.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.5.

Check Your Work

Can you tell from the arrows whether a part travels straight, turns continuously, or reverses direction?

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
