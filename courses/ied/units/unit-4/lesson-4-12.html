DuckWorks Amusement Park Design Brief

Today's goal

Plan one motorized attraction according to an assigned ride card.

Class plan

10 minutes demonstration · 30 minutes guided work · 5 minutes submission and cleanup (adjusted for safe equipment shutdown).

What to do

  1. Record the team's assigned ride and three performance requirements.
  2. Review the common 250 × 250 mm base constraint and motor/switch requirements.
  3. Draw a labeled concept with supports, motor, switch, power source, motion and one original part.

Safety: Instructor approves the model's mechanism and low-voltage electrical plan before fabrication.
