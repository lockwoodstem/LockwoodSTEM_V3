What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Concept generation prevents teams from locking into the first idea too soon.

Key Idea 2

A decision matrix turns criteria into a more transparent comparison.

Key Idea 3

Scores should be explained with evidence, not used as magic answers.

Student Task

Generate and Select a Mechanism Concept

Develop multiple system concepts and use a decision matrix to choose one based on evidence and challenge criteria.

  1. Create at least three meaningfully different concepts.
  2. Label the proposed mechanism and expected input/output.
  3. Establish weighted criteria using prior lesson evidence.
  4. Score the concepts and justify the selected direction.

Quality check: The selected concept follows the matrix evidence and is not chosen before alternatives are compared.
