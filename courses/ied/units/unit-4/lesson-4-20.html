IED Lesson 4.20 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Design Improvement and Retest

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Use a test result to make one change and evaluate it with a matched retest.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Iteration means improving a design using evidence from an earlier test. Change one feature at a time so you can connect the result to that change. The baseline and retest should use the same measurement and starting conditions.

Worked Example

Baseline
A swing chair contacts a support on 2 of 3 turns.
One change
With approval, move the support outward within the base.
Retest
If contact drops to 0 of 3 turns under the same conditions, the change helped in this run.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

Your ride rubbed on two of three turns before an adjustment. Which retest gives the clearest comparison?

What to Do

Safety: Disconnect power before any adjustment. Obtain a fresh safety check before energizing. Stop if the motion or wiring is unsafe.

  1. Choose one issue from 4.19 and record its baseline observation.
  2. Disconnect power. Ask your teacher to approve one adjustment, then make it.
  3. Repeat the same check under the same conditions after approval. Record the new observation and explain whether the change helped.

What to Submit

One before-and-after comparison for one requirement, with the change and a two-sentence explanation of the result and next step. If a retest is unsafe, mark it not tested and give the reason.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.20.

Optional extension · up to +2 points

Propose a follow-up test

Use your retest result to suggest one follow-up check at a different position or starting condition. State what stays the same and what measurement you would record. Write the procedure only.

Add your extension to the same submission under Extension.

+1 point: Describe one follow-up condition and a control.
+1 point: Choose a measurement that would help you learn whether the improvement holds.

Check Your Work

Did you compare the same measurement in both runs and report what happened even if the change did not help?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
