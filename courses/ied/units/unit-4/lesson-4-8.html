IED Lesson 4.8 · Mechanisms and Motion

Testing and Improving Mechanisms

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Measure the effect of one adjustment on how consistently a mechanism turns.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Friction resists relative movement between surfaces. Poor alignment may make moving parts rub or bind. A baseline is the result before a change. Keep the test method the same after the change so the results can help you judge its effect.

Worked Example

Before
2 of 5 turns without catching.
One change
Align one support. Keep the same operator and turning pace.
After
5 of 5 turns without catching.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

You want to learn whether reseating a shaft support reduces catching. What makes a useful retest?

What to Do

Safety: Disconnect power if present. Stop when the part catches and reset gently. Keep fingers out of the mechanism.

  1. Use your Lesson 4.7 build or the demo model and count catch-free turns out of five.
  2. Ask your teacher to approve one adjustment, such as reseating a shaft support, then make it.
  3. Repeat five turns with the same operator and pace. Compare the two counts.

What to Submit

One before-and-after record with the two counts out of five, the one adjustment, and one sentence stating what the comparison shows.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.8.

Optional extension · up to +2 points

Turn your counts into percentages

Convert your before and after counts out of five into percentages. State the change in percentage points and what the change says about the adjustment in your test.

Add your extension to the same submission under Extension.

+1 point: Show both percentages and the difference.
+1 point: Interpret the difference using your before-and-after evidence.

Check Your Work

Can you identify exactly one change and tell whether it helped using comparable measurements?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
