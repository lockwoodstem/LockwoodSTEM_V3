4.15 CAD: Moving Assembly

Model or mock up one moving component.

Today’s goal

Model or mock up one moving component.

45-minute plan: 10 min demonstration, 30 min guided work, 5 min documentation and cleanup. Adjust for tool shutdown when needed.

What to do

  1. Identify the moving part and its shaft or linkage.
  2. Make the component in Fusion or build a simple unpowered mock-up.
  3. Check clearance against the frame.

Safety: Do not energize mechanisms while building, fitting, or adjusting parts. Use approved tools and fabrication procedures.

Three quick questions

What to submit

CAD screenshot or physical mock-up photo with a clearance note.

Turn in today's work in Google Classroom. Evidence of completed daily progress counts even if the ride is not fully finished.

Daily rubric — 10 points: 4 task completion, 4 accurate work/evidence, 2 clear submission.
