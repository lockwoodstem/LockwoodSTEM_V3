IED Lesson 4.15 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

CAD: Moving Assembly

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Model the moving part and verify clearance at two positions along its path.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

An assembly includes separate parts placed together. A joint defines how parts move relative to each other. A revolute joint allows rotation about one axis. Clearance is the gap between parts. Interference occurs when parts overlap or hit each other.

Worked Example

Place a Carousel platform on a vertical shaft axis. At two angles, check whether a seat or platform edge contacts a post. If either position overlaps, shift the post or reduce the platform radius, then check both positions again.

What to Do

Safety: Keep powered parts disconnected during any physical fit comparison. Get instructor approval before fabricating.

  1. Add one moving component to the 4.14 assembly or teacher starter file.
  2. Set its axis or pivot and move it to two positions using a joint or temporary positioning.
  3. Inspect both positions for interference and mark one gap or needed change.

What to Submit

One annotated image with two CAD views showing the positions, the same axis or pivot, and a clearance or interference note at each position.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.15.

Check Your Work

Do your views show two different positions? Identify one place to check during the full hand test in 4.17.

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
