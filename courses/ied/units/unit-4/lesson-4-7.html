IED Lesson 4.7 · Mechanisms and Motion

Building a Simple Mechanism

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Assemble a hand-driven two-gear mechanism that turns without binding.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

A shaft carries a rotating part. Alignment means shafts and gears are positioned as intended. Clearance is the gap that lets parts move without rubbing. Gear teeth must mesh without being squeezed together or spaced too far apart.

Worked Example

Input gear
Turns clockwise.
Output gear
The meshing external gear turns counterclockwise.
If it catches
Check that the shafts are seated and parallel before changing the distance between them.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

Your unpowered gears catch as you turn the input. What should you do first?

What to Do

Safety: Keep the mechanism unpowered. Turn it by the intended handle, keeping fingers out of the mesh. Stop at a jam.

  1. Use the prepared kit to mount two shafts and fit one gear to each shaft.
  2. Turn the input slowly by hand for three turns and watch which way the output turns.
  3. If the gears catch, adjust one support or gear position and repeat the test.

What to Submit

One annotated sketch or photo labeling the input gear, output gear, and shafts. Add a note stating the output direction and whether the three-turn test was smooth, or where it caught.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.7.

Optional extension · up to +2 points

Predict a gear ratio

Sketch a 12-tooth input gear driving a 24-tooth output gear. Predict the output turns and direction for two complete clockwise input turns. Use the sketch or tooth counts as evidence.

Add your extension to the same submission under Extension.

+1 point: Show both gears and their tooth counts.
+1 point: Give the correct turn count and direction with a reason.

Check Your Work

Are the supports secure, and can the gears turn through the test without being forced?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
