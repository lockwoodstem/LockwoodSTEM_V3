Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Friction can provide grip, resistance, or unwanted energy loss.

Key Idea 2

Mechanism performance depends on contact surfaces, alignment, force, and material choice.

Key Idea 3

A friction problem should be diagnosed before parts are redesigned.

Your Task

Investigate Friction and Mechanism Performance

Compare mechanism performance under different contact, load, or lubrication conditions using a controlled test.

  1. Define the performance measure to observe.
  2. Change only the assigned friction-related condition.
  3. Record force, travel, time, or qualitative behavior consistently.
  4. Explain how friction helped or harmed performance.

Quality check: The conclusion identifies the tested condition and does not confuse friction with an unrelated design change.
