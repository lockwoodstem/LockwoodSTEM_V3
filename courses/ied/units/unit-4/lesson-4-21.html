Today’s goal

Demonstrate the ride and explain its motion.

45-minute plan: 10 min demonstration, 30 min guided work, 5 min documentation and cleanup. Adjust for tool shutdown when needed.

What to do

  1. Check safety and prepare your model.
  2. Demonstrate one operating cycle to the teacher or explain a documented safe failure.
  3. Answer three brief individual reflection questions.

Safety: Operate only after instructor safety approval; keep clear of all moving parts and disconnect power for modifications.
