IED Lesson 4.21 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Amusement Park Showcase

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Use test evidence to explain how your ride works and what it achieved.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

A demonstration shows how a system functions. A claim is what you say the ride achieved. Evidence is a specific observation that supports the claim. A limitation explains what you still cannot conclude or what needs improvement.

Worked Example

Claim and evidence
Our Pirate Ship reached five back-and-forth cycles in its last approved run.
How it moved
The motor drove a crank and rod that rocked the ship.
Next improvement
The frame shifted on the second run, so we would stiffen the support.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

Which showcase claim is backed by a specific test observation?

What to Do

Safety: Ask your teacher before powering the ride. Keep spectators clear of moving parts. Disconnect after the demonstration or use an unpowered walkthrough.

  1. Prepare your model and choose one observed result from 4.19 or 4.20.
  2. Show how your ride moves once your teacher says it is safe to run. If the model cannot run safely, use a disconnected walkthrough and describe the recorded result.
  3. Write your individual showcase note using the motion path, one requirement result, and one next improvement.

What to Submit

One individual three-sentence showcase note naming the motor-to-ride mechanism, a requirement with its observed result, and one next improvement. The demonstration supports this same 10-point assignment.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.21.

Check Your Work

Does your note explain what moved, cite an actual test result, and name a next change tied to that evidence?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
