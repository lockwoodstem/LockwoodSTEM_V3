IED Lesson 4.14 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

CAD: Base and Supports

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Model a dimensioned base and shaft supports that fit the allowed footprint.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

The footprint is the length and width a base occupies when viewed from above. A support holds a shaft or pivot in position and passes load into the base. Two supports for one straight shaft need holes on the same axis. Early CAD models should capture these key dimensions before adding detail.

Worked Example

A base measuring 240 × 230 mm fits the limit because both dimensions are at most 250 mm. If two stands support one wheel axle, place both hole centers at the same height and align them across the base.

What to Do

Safety: Work in CAD today. Show your teacher the material and dimensions you plan to use. Wait for approval before using fabrication tools.

  1. In Fusion, make a rectangular base and dimension its length and width in mm.
  2. Add the main support or supports and show where the shaft or pivot will sit.
  3. Measure the base and verify shaft alignment in the model.

What to Submit

One annotated Fusion screenshot showing the base length and width in mm, supports, and shaft or pivot location. Use a view that makes all labels readable.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.14.

Check Your Work

Are both base dimensions within 250 mm, and could a straight shaft pass through its intended supports?

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
