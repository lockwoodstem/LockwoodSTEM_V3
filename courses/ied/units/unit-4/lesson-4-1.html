IED Lesson 4.1 · Materials and Testing

Engineering Materials

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Choose a material for a model part by matching one property to the part’s job.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Materials are what parts are made from. A property describes how a material behaves. Stiffness is resistance to changing shape under a load. A part’s shape and size affect how easily it bends. Strength describes how much stress a material can take before yielding or breaking. Mass tells you how much matter a sample contains. Compare samples of similar shape and size before drawing conclusions.

Worked Example

A plywood strip and a foam-board strip of similar size are available. The plywood sample bends less in a gentle comparison. Choose it for a wheel support that needs to hold the axle in position. Use the lighter foam board for a sign that carries little load.

What to Do

Safety: Use provided samples only. Check with the instructor before handling pieces with sharp edges or splinters. Keep the samples intact.

  1. Examine the five labeled samples your teacher sets out.
  2. Record one observable feature for each sample.
  3. Pick one sample for a ride support and explain how a useful property matches that job.

What to Submit

One five-row sample list (name and observed feature per row), plus one sentence naming your support material, its property, and why it helps.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.1.

Check Your Work

Does your reason describe what the material does for the support? An observation such as “bends easily” alone cannot establish strength.

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
