Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A system combines parts that interact to perform a useful function.

Key Idea 2

Materials, motion, circuits, and data each influence whether the system works reliably.

Key Idea 3

The final prototype must be defended with evidence, not just shown as a finished object.

Student Task

Frame the Aerospace Systems Challenge

Define the Unit 4 challenge and identify the types of subsystem evidence the team will eventually need without selecting a final concept yet.

  1. Restate the system goal in your own words.
  2. Identify the expected input, action, and output.
  3. List the major topic areas the unit will investigate.
  4. Write two questions that must be answered before concept selection.

Quality check: The launch note defines the challenge without assuming an untested solution.
