Engineering Materials

Identify five common engineering materials and connect each to a useful property.

45 minutes10 pointsOne daily submission

Today's goal

Identify five common engineering materials and connect each to a useful property.

You will need

Instructor sample set: wood, acrylic, PLA, aluminum, steel; comparison sheet.

Your 45-minute plan

10 min: Watch the demonstration and ask questions.
30 min: Complete the three steps below.
5 min: Document your work, submit, and clean up.

Instructor adjusts timing for equipment shutdown and soldering safety.

Learn the concept

Material properties

Engineers choose materials based on the job. Strength means resistance to failure; stiffness means resistance to bending; mass describes how much matter is present. Ease of shaping and joining matters too.

Worked example: A ride support might need stiff plywood while a decorative sign can be foam board.

Remember: Strong and stiff are not identical.

What to do

  1. Examine five labeled material samples or teacher-provided photographs.
  2. Match each material with a property: rigid, flexible, light, strong, or easy to fabricate.
  3. Choose one material for a model ride base and explain why.
Safety: Handle samples carefully; no material destruction or sharp-edge testing.
