The Park Design Brief

Today's goal: understand your team's assigned attraction and sketch a design that follows the project rules.

View all ride requirements

What to do (30 min)

  1. Write your team members' names and assigned ride.
  2. Read your three ride-specific requirements in the challenge hub.
  3. Draw one large side-view sketch of your attraction.
  4. Label the motor, switch, power source, rotating or moving part, and support structure.
  5. Circle one part your team might design in Fusion.

What to submit

  1. A photograph or screenshot of your labeled sketch.
  2. A completed checklist confirming the three ride-specific criteria and 250 × 250 mm footprint.

Grading: 4 points completed checklist, 4 points labeled sketch, 2 points readable evidence.
