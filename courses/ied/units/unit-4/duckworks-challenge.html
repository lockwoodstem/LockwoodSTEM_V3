IED Unit 4 · DuckWorks

DuckWorks Amusement Park

Design, build, and test one motorized tabletop ride in your assigned team of 2–3.

Start Lesson 4.12

Requirements for every ride

  • Keep the base within a 250 × 250 mm footprint.
  • Use the standard team kit, including the supplied low-voltage motor, matching instructor-approved power source, and on/off switch.
  • Transfer motion using a mechanical system and include at least one original student-designed part.
  • Meet the three performance targets for your assigned ride below.

You can use Fusion CAD, 3D printing, and instructor-approved laser cutting. Ask the instructor to approve the construction and inspect the wiring before any powered test.

Keep hands and spectators away from the moving model. Solder only after the instructor’s skills and station checks, with direct supervision, eye protection, and working local fume extraction. Disconnect power before soldering or adjusting the model.

Ride performance targets

Use the three targets for your ride. Review with the instructor what counts as a pass for visible checks before testing.

Ferris Wheel

  1. The wheel completes three continuous rotations.
  2. Four suspended cabins are included.
  3. Cabins stay approximately upright as the wheel turns.

Swing Ride

  1. The central structure completes three rotations.
  2. At least four chairs hang securely from the ride.
  3. Chairs clear the support frame during operation.

Pirate Ship

  1. The ship swings back and forth without rotating completely.
  2. The ship completes five controlled back-and-forth cycles.
  3. The support structure remains stable.

Carousel

  1. The platform completes three continuous rotations.
  2. At least four seats or figures are attached.
  3. The platform remains reasonably level during movement.

Spinning Teacups

  1. The main platform rotates through three revolutions.
  2. At least three cups are secured to the platform.
  3. All cups stay attached and clear the frame during motion.
