IED UNIT 4 · PHASE 4

DuckWorks Amusement Park

One class. Five different rides. Each team designs and builds a safe, motorized scale-model attraction for our collaborative amusement park.

Teams of 2–3 · 250 × 250 mm maximum base footprint · 10 project lessons · 10 points per daily submission

Start lesson 4.12

The five assigned attractions

Rotary motion

Ferris Wheel

  1. The wheel completes three continuous rotations.
  2. Four suspended cabins are included.
  3. Cabins stay approximately upright as the wheel turns.
Rotary motion

Swing Ride

  1. The central structure completes three rotations.
  2. At least four chairs hang securely from the ride.
  3. Chairs clear the support frame during operation.
Oscillating motion

Pirate Ship

  1. The ship swings back and forth without rotating completely.
  2. The ship completes five controlled back-and-forth cycles.
  3. The support structure remains stable.
Rotary motion

Carousel

  1. The platform completes three continuous rotations.
  2. At least four seats or figures are attached.
  3. The platform remains reasonably level during movement.
Rotary motion

Spinning Teacups

  1. The main platform rotates through three revolutions.
  2. At least three cups are secured to the platform.
  3. All cups stay attached and clear the frame during motion.

Performance criteria are prototype targets and should be confirmed using instructor reference builds.

Requirements for every ride

  1. Use the assigned ride type.
  2. Use the supplied low-voltage motor, approved power source, and on/off switch.
  3. Show controlled mechanical movement using gears, pulleys, shafts, or linkages.
  4. Stay within the 250 × 250 mm base footprint.
  5. Include at least one original student-designed part.
  6. Include safe wiring; solder only under instructor supervision after passing the soldering check.

All attractions are tabletop models. No humans may ride or touch moving mechanisms while powered. Instructors approve construction and energizing.

Daily grading

10 points each: 4 completion, 4 correct evidence / practical work, 2 clarity and submission. The separate final demonstration is 20 points. A photo of meaningful progress can earn credit on fabrication days even if the complete model is not functional.

Daily pacing: approximately 10 minutes of demonstration, 30 minutes of guided work, 5 minutes to submit and clean up. Soldering sessions have additional supervised shutdown time.
