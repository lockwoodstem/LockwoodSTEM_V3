IED Lesson 4.19 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Functional Testing

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Use three short trials to gather evidence against your three ride requirements.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

A trial is one run using a stated procedure. A pass criterion describes the evidence needed to mark a requirement met. Use the same starting conditions each time and record observations before deciding pass, fail, or not tested.

Worked Example

Target
Three continuous turns.
Observation
The model stops after two turns from the reference mark.
Record
2 turns · Fail. The count shows why the criterion was missed.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

A required three-turn test stops after two turns. What should you record?

What to Do

Safety: Get instructor approval before powering the model. Keep clear of moving parts. Stop for wobble, rubbing, a stall, or hot wiring. Disconnect power before touching or adjusting the model.

  1. Turn the three requirements from your ride card into counts or visible checks.
  2. Once your teacher says it is safe to test, run three brief trials under the same starting conditions. Record all three checks per trial, counting fixed features once and noting that they were unchanged.
  3. Mark each result pass, fail, or not tested and circle one improvement priority.

Check your ride requirements.

What to Submit

One three-row trial table with a column per requirement, the observation and status for each, and one improvement priority below it. Mark any unrun trial with the reason.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.19.

Optional extension · up to +2 points

Summarize reliability across trials

Pick one requirement. Use your table to report passes out of the trials that were actually run, and list any trials you could not run separately. If you ran none, write “not tested.” Explain what you can conclude about repeatability.

Add your extension to the same submission under Extension.

+1 point: Give an accurate pass count, trial total, and separate note for any unrun trials.
+1 point: Explain what the observations support about repeatability.

Check Your Work

Can someone tell from your table what happened in each trial and why you chose that priority?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
