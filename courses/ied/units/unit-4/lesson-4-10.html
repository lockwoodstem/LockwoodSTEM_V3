Introduction to Soldering

Produce two acceptable through-hole joints safely under direct supervision.

Today's goal

Produce two acceptable through-hole joints safely under direct supervision.

Class plan

10 minutes demonstration · 30 minutes guided work · 5 minutes submission and cleanup (adjusted for safe equipment shutdown).

What to do

  1. Review PPE, point-source fume extraction, safe iron handling and shutdown with the teacher.
  2. Make two supervised joints on a training PCB.
  3. Ask teacher to inspect for good wetting and no bridges.

Safety: Eye protection and point-source fume extraction are required; hot tools remain under instructor supervision. Never solder energized circuits or directly onto batteries.

Daily questions

What to submit

Practice-board photo, three answers, instructor skills check.
