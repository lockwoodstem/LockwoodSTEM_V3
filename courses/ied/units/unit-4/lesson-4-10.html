Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Motors are energy converters inside electromechanical systems.

Key Idea 2

A motor output is only useful if it is connected to a mechanism that performs the needed function.

Key Idea 3

Mounting, load, speed, direction, and power source affect motor performance.

Student Task

Control Mechanical Motion with a Motor

Connect a motor to a safe low-voltage circuit and observe how electrical input affects mechanical output.

  1. Identify the motor terminals and intended motion.
  2. Connect the motor using the approved circuit.
  3. Test direction and response using brief power intervals.
  4. Record one variable that affects speed, torque, or useful motion.

Quality check: The motor is operated safely and the output observation is linked to a defined electrical or mechanical condition.
