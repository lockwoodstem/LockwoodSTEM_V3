IED Lesson 4.13 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Ride Mechanism Planning

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Plan how your motor’s rotation will move your assigned ride.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

The drivetrain transfers motion from the motor to the ride. The input is usually a rotating shaft. The output may turn continuously or swing about a pivot. A crank is an arm that rotates with a shaft. A connected linkage can use this rotation to rock a part back and forth.

Worked Example

A Pirate Ship motor turns a crank. A rod links the crank to an arm on the ship. As the crank turns, the rod pushes and pulls the arm, so the ship swings about its fixed pivot. The sketch needs room for both the crank and the moving ship.

What to Do

Safety: Keep any demonstrator unpowered. Identify pinch points and keep fingers clear while checking the motion.

  1. Choose the output motion from your ride requirements.
  2. Draw a path from the motor through one mechanism to the moving ride part.
  3. Mark where the shafts or pivots are supported and one place that needs clearance. Review it with the instructor.

What to Submit

One annotated mechanism sketch showing the input, transfer parts, output and direction, supports, and one clearance location.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.13.

Check Your Work

Can you trace the full motion path and point to the feature that keeps the moving part from striking the frame?

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
