Ride Mechanism Planning

Choose how a motor will move your assigned ride.

Today's goal

Choose how a motor will move your assigned ride.

Materials: Ride assignment card, sketch from 4.12, kit inventory, pencil.

45-minute plan

10 min demonstration and directions
30 min guided team activity
5 min daily evidence and cleanup

Fabrication and electrical days may require an adjusted schedule for safe shutdown.

Learn the concept

Plan a power path through the mechanism

Your motor supplies rotation, but a ride may need that rotation delivered to a larger wheel, shifted to another shaft, or changed into a back-and-forth motion. Draw the input, the motion-transfer parts, and the output. Plan where bearings, supports, and clearance are needed.

Example: A pirate ship can use motor rotation to drive a crank and linkage that swings the ship through an angle.

Remember: Choose the simplest mechanism that can satisfy your ride requirements.

What to do

  1. Identify the ride's input motion (motor rotation) and required output motion.
  2. Sketch a simple motor-to-ride connection using gears, belt/pulley, shaft, or linkage.
  3. Label at least three major parts and describe one likely design difficulty.

Safety: Do not connect or energize motors during planning.

Answer briefly in the space below or in your engineering notebook.

What to submit
