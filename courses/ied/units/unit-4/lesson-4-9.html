Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A circuit must have a complete path for current to flow.

Key Idea 2

Electrical safety includes low-voltage habits, correct polarity, secure connections, and inspection before power.

Key Idea 3

A prototype circuit should be tested before it is integrated into a larger system.

Student Task

Build and Verify a Safe Simple Circuit

Construct the assigned low-voltage circuit and verify current path, polarity, and safe component use before connecting it to a prototype.

  1. Identify the source, load, conductors, and control component.
  2. Sketch or follow the approved circuit diagram.
  3. Build the circuit with power disconnected.
  4. Perform the safety check, power it briefly, and record the result.

Quality check: The circuit operates as intended with no exposed short circuit, reversed polarized component, or unsafe connection.

Lesson Interactive

Complete a Safe LED Circuit

Close the electrical path and include the component that protects the LED.

Status: Open or incomplete
