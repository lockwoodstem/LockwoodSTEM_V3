IED Lesson 4.6 · Mechanisms and Motion

Exploring Mechanical Systems

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Practice check Activity Submission Final check

Today’s Goal

Trace how three mechanisms transfer or change a motion input.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

An input is motion entering a mechanism. An output is what the driven part does. Gears transfer rotation through meshing teeth, and a belt carries motion between pulleys. A cam pushes a follower as it turns. A linkage uses connected bars to carry or change motion.

Worked Example

Input
A handle turns the cam.
Transfer
The high spot lifts the follower. The follower returns as the high spot moves away.
Output
A follower guided in a straight line moves back and forth.

Practice check

Make a choice and check your answer before you start the activity.

A motor turns a pulley. A belt carries that motion to a second pulley. What is the output motion at the second pulley?

What to Do

Safety: Use unpowered models and keep hands, sleeves, and hair away from gears, belts, and pinch points.

  1. Observe three of the mechanisms set out for class: gears, pulleys, cams, or linkages.
  2. At each station, follow the input through the transfer part to the output.
  3. Sketch a motion arrow in each row and name one mechanism that could drive a park ride.

What to Submit

One three-row mechanism chart. In each row include the mechanism, input, transfer part, and output with a motion arrow. Add one sentence linking one mechanism to a ride.

Submit one file and include your name. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.6.

Check Your Work

Can a reader trace movement from the handle or motor shaft to the part that actually moves?

10-point rubric
  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.

Finish the practice check to mark your work ready.
