Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

Hands-on exploration reveals motion issues that diagrams may hide.

Key Idea 2

Each mechanism type has tradeoffs in complexity, control, reliability, and buildability.

Key Idea 3

Quick sketches help translate a mechanism idea into a build plan.

Your Task

Explore Physical Mechanism Behavior

Manipulate linkages, hinges, cams, gears, and pulleys to compare range, direction, speed, and limitations.

  1. Operate each assigned mechanism safely.
  2. Record the input and output behavior.
  3. Identify backlash, binding, limited travel, or another constraint.
  4. Sketch one promising mechanism arrangement.

Quality check: Observations connect physical behavior to a possible design use and a known limitation.
