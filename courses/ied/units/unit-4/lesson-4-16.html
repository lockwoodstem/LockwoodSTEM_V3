IED Lesson 4.16 · DuckWorks Amusement Park

Manufacturing: Base and Structure

45 minutes · 10 points

Concept Activity Submission

Today’s Goal

Make and dry-fit a base and its main supports to the CAD plan.

Learn and see a demonstration (10 min), work (30 min), then submit and clean up (5 min).

Learn the Concept

Fabrication turns dimensions into physical parts. A dry fit is a trial assembly before permanent fastening. Measuring now helps catch fit errors before joining. A brace stiffens a flexible frame, but accurate support placement still matters.

Worked Example

The plan puts two shaft centers 55 mm above the base. The dry fit measures 55 mm at one support and 58 mm at the other. Adjust the higher support before fastening so the axle can seat without binding.

What to Do

Safety: Wear required eye protection and use only approved tools and materials. Get your teacher’s approval and supervision before cutting or printing. Use the laser cutter only if you have permission, your teacher is supervising, and your material is approved.

  1. Mark the base and support locations from your plan on approved stock.
  2. Make the base and main supports using the prepared parts or tools your teacher has approved. Dry-fit them before joining.
  3. Measure base length and width and one support location. Ask your teacher to check the fit before you fasten the parts.

What to Submit

One annotated photo or sketch of the dry fit showing both measured base dimensions, one measured support dimension, and one fit result or needed correction.

Submit one file for your team and include all team members’ names. Turn it in through Google Classroom for Lesson 4.16.

Check Your Work

Do your measurements come from the physical frame? Make sure supports hold their positions before preparing the drive.

10-point rubric

  • 4 points · Completion. Include all requested parts of today’s submission.
  • 4 points · Engineering accuracy. Use correct concepts and labels. Support your result or decision with the evidence requested.
  • 2 points · Clarity. Make the evidence readable and include the required name or team names.
