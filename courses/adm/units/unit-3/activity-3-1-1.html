Objective

Work toward the lesson goal: Digital Twin Development. Use the directions and success criteria in the activity below.

Materials

For the online work: a browser and your engineering notebook. Follow the activity’s materials list for any physical build, and get teacher approval before using equipment.

Plan your time

Suggested work block: 45–60 minutes. Physical builds and projects may span several classes; follow your teacher’s schedule.

Lesson Overview

Learn what a digital twin is and how virtual models can represent real manufacturing systems.

Use the focus question, lesson notes, and deliverables below to guide your work. Follow the class files, demonstrations, and approved references connected to this lesson.

Notes
