ADM Unit 4

Securing the Supply Chain

You will explore cybersecurity, data integrity, secure software, demand planning, procurement, production scheduling, packaging, accessibility, and resilient manufacturing.

🚀 Engineering Challenge Hub View Lessons Unit Challenge Back to ADM
Unit Goal

Protect the system

This unit focuses on how manufacturers protect products, data, people, timelines, quality, and delivery systems from disruption.

Unit pathway

Lesson Sequence

Open each lesson in order and document your work as you move through the unit.

Lesson Type Title Focus Open
Cybersecurity, Data Integrity & Secure Software You will explore how cybersecurity affects connected machines, sensors, software, data, people, and production environments.
Activity 4.1.1 Activity CIA Triad Learn how confidentiality, integrity, and availability apply to connected manufacturing systems and production data. Open Lesson
Activity 4.1.2 Activity Human in the Lab Explore how human decisions, access, mistakes, and procedures affect the safety and security of a manufacturing system. Open Lesson
Activity 4.1.3 Activity Malware Investigate how malicious software can disrupt machines, corrupt data, delay production, or create unsafe conditions. Open Lesson
Activity 4.1.4 Activity Problems With Data Analyze how missing, incorrect, corrupted, or poorly formatted data can affect manufacturing decisions and product quality. Open Lesson
Activity 4.1.5 Activity Secure Software Explore how software design, updates, access control, and secure coding habits help protect automated systems. Open Lesson
Project 4.1.6 Project Malfunctioning Lab Analyze a lab scenario where a system is malfunctioning due to data, software, cybersecurity, or human-factor issues. Open Lesson
Demand, Procurement, Scheduling & Packaging You will explore how manufacturing teams plan for demand, choose suppliers, schedule production, and package products for shipping and use.
Activity 4.2.1 Activity Determining Demand Investigate how manufacturers estimate demand and use that information to plan materials, production, staffing, and delivery. Open Lesson
Activity 4.2.2 Activity Procurement Explore how companies select, purchase, and manage materials, parts, tools, and suppliers needed for production. Open Lesson
Activity 4.2.3 Activity Production Scheduling Analyze how manufacturing teams organize time, machines, people, and materials to meet production goals. Open Lesson
Activity 4.2.4 Activity Packaging Study how packaging protects products, supports shipping, communicates information, and affects cost and sustainability. Open Lesson
Activity 4.2.5 Activity Accessibility in Packaging Evaluate how packaging design affects usability, accessibility, safety, and customer experience. Open Lesson
Project 4.2.6 Project Packing Optimization Improve a packing or packaging solution by balancing space, protection, cost, usability, accessibility, and shipping efficiency. Open Lesson
Resilient Manufacturing Challenge Problem You will combine cybersecurity, supply chain planning, packaging, accessibility, scheduling, and risk analysis in a final unit challenge.
Problem 4.3.1 Problem Resilient Manufacturing Analyze or design a manufacturing process that can prevent, detect, respond to, and recover from disruptions while protecting production, data, products, and people. Open Lesson

Essential Questions

  • Why is cybersecurity important in connected manufacturing?
  • How can data, software, and human decisions disrupt production?
  • How do demand, procurement, scheduling, and packaging affect supply chain performance?
  • How can manufacturers design systems that prevent, respond to, and recover from disruptions?

Learning Targets

  • I can explain the CIA triad in a manufacturing context.
  • I can identify data, software, cybersecurity, and human-factor risks.
  • I can analyze how demand, procurement, scheduling, and packaging decisions affect production.
  • I can recommend improvements that increase manufacturing resilience.

Major Deliverables

  • Engineering notebook entries
  • CIA triad or cybersecurity analysis
  • Demand/procurement/schedule evidence
  • Packaging or accessibility review
  • Risk analysis and recommendations
  • Resilient Manufacturing reflection
