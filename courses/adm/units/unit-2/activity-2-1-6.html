ADM Activity 2.1.6

Pneumatics & Automated Actuation

Use compressed-air components as programmable actuators for loading, diverting, and positioning products inside the workcell.

Unit 2 Progression

Sense → Decide → Move → Communicate → Monitor → Optimize → Integrate

Unit 2 applies the programming foundation from Unit 1 to increasingly connected manufacturing systems.

Your lesson guide

1. Goal & materials2. Activity3. Check your work4. Report

Objective

Work toward the lesson goal: Pneumatics & Automated Actuation. Use the directions and success criteria in the activity below.

Materials

For the online work: a browser and your engineering notebook. Follow the activity’s materials list for any physical build, and get teacher approval before using equipment.

Plan your time

Suggested work block: 45–60 minutes. Physical builds and projects may span several classes; follow your teacher’s schedule.

Learning Targets

By the end of this lesson

  • Identify the function of the compressor, air tank, tubing, solenoids, and cylinders.
  • Explain how a pneumatic circuit produces extension and retraction.
  • Program pneumatic actuators used by the Disk Loader and Diverters.
  • Coordinate pneumatic motion with conveyor movement and sensor feedback.
Equipment & Systems

What you will use

  • VEX CTE Workcell
  • Compressor and air tank
  • Solenoids and tubing
  • Pneumatic cylinders
  • Disk Loader / Diverters
  • Distance Sensor
Student Workflow

What You Will Do

  1. Trace the pneumatic circuit from the air source to a cylinder and identify how the solenoid changes the air path.
  2. Operate and program individual pneumatic actuators.
  3. Use the Distance Sensor or other workcell feedback to determine when a product is positioned for the next operation.
  4. Integrate a pneumatic action with conveyor movement and sensor logic.
  5. Test for jams, missed products, or incorrect timing and revise the sequence.
Programming Focus

How coding supports the lesson

Use clearly named functions where appropriate so repeated pneumatic actions are separated from the main process sequence. Continue applying prior sensor and conditional logic.
