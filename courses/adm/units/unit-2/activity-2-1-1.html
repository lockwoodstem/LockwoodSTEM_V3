ADM Activity 2.1.1

Anatomy of an Automated Workcell

Understand how the controller, robotic arm, conveyors, sensors, actuators, and operator function as one manufacturing system.

Unit 2 Progression

Sense → Decide → Move → Communicate → Monitor → Optimize → Integrate

Unit 2 applies the programming foundation from Unit 1 to increasingly connected manufacturing systems.

Learning Targets

By the end of this lesson

  • Identify the major subsystems of an automated workcell.
  • Explain the role of the EXP Brain as the workcell controller.
  • Trace material flow and information flow through the system.
  • Create a system diagram showing inputs, decisions, and outputs.
Equipment & Systems

What you will use

  • VEX CTE Workcell
  • EXP Brain / controller
  • 6-Axis Arm
  • Conveyors
  • Workcell sensors and actuators
Student Workflow

What You Will Do

  1. Inspect the complete VEX CTE Workcell and identify each major subsystem.
  2. Choose one product path and trace what moves the product, what senses it, and what controls the next action.
  3. Create a labeled system diagram showing the controller, inputs, outputs, material movement, and operator interaction.
  4. Identify at least two failure points and predict how each would affect production.
Programming Focus

How coding supports the lesson

Programming is supporting evidence in this lesson. Variables, loops, conditionals, and robot motion from Unit 1 are prerequisite skills and are not retaught here.

What You Will Submit

What You Submit

  • Labeled workcell system diagram
  • Input → decision → output explanation
  • Two workcell failure-point analyses
  • Any requested demonstration code or screenshots
