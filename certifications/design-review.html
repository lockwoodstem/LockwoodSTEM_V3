Hands-on requirement

After passing the online test, participate in or submit a teacher-reviewed design review checkpoint for a current project.

Student must demonstrate

  • Explain the problem, constraints, users, criteria, and current design direction.
  • Use sketches, CAD, photos, calculations, test data, or prototypes as evidence.
  • Identify risks, tradeoffs, and unresolved questions honestly.
  • Respond professionally to feedback and ask clarifying questions.
  • Record action items, owners, due dates, and planned revisions.
  • Update documentation after the review so decisions are traceable.

Badge unlock rule

  • Online test score of 80% or higher
  • Teacher-reviewed design review performance or artifact
  • Evidence-based engineering communication
  • Professional feedback and action-item habits
