One-day engineering challenge

PUNKIN CHUNKIN

Design it. Build it. Launch it. Improve it.

Just for fun 🎃

01 Group size

2–3 students

Work together to build and test one pumpkin launcher.

02 The goal

Build a working PVC catapult that launches a small 3D-printed pumpkin toward the target using stored elastic energy — not a hand throw.

03 Building materials

  • 1/2-inch PVC pipe in assorted lengths
  • PVC elbows, tees and connectors
  • Rubber bands and elastic cord
  • Small cloth pieces for a sling or cradle
  • Tape and a 3D-printed pumpkin

04 Success criteria

  • The launcher is stable and holds the pumpkin before launch.
  • Elastic energy — not a hand throw — launches the pumpkin.
  • The pumpkin lands inside the designated testing area.
  • Your team tests the design and makes at least one improvement.
⚠ Launch safety Safety glasses on · Teacher checks launcher · Nobody in the launch area · Fire only on the signal
No grades. No points. Just engineering and flying pumpkins.
