Worksheet Roadmap

Complete the Investigation in Four Parts

The page and worksheet use the same sequence so you can move between them without hunting for the next step.

Part A

Predict

Predict speed, torque, and direction for three gear pairs, then check each prediction in the simulation.

Part B

Explore Gear Pairs

Run four trials: a reduction, a speed-up, a 1:1 pair, and one system of your choice.

Part C

Build a Gear Train

Change the idler size in a 20T → idler → 40T train and determine what does and does not change.

Part D

Make a Mission Decision

Compare three lunar sample-storage door options and defend the strongest choice with numerical evidence.

Core Math

Use the Simulation to Check the Equations

Gear RatioGR = driven teeth / driver teeth
Output Speedrpm_out = rpm_in × (driver / driven)
Ideal TorqueT_out = T_in × (driven / driver)

Direction Rule

Every external gear mesh reverses rotation direction. One mesh reverses once. Two meshes reverse twice. Track direction separately from the ratio.

Mission Challenge

Lunar Sample-Storage Door

Use the third simulation mode and the worksheet table to make an engineering recommendation.

Design Need

The motor operates efficiently near 1200 rpm. The storage door should rotate slowly and gain ideal torque so it is less likely to stall in lunar dust.

  • Option A: 12T driver → 24T driven
  • Option B: 12T driver → 48T driven
  • Option C: 24T driver → 36T driven

Your recommendation must use at least two numerical pieces of evidence.
