POE Unit 2 | Mission Brief 2.5

Structural Efficiency Cup

Build the highest-scoring structure based on load capacity, mass, and dimensional compliance.

Advanced2 class periodsCompetition
Back to Challenge HubDownload Challenge Brief (PDF)
Engineering Design Brief

Work independently or with an approved partner.

Read the complete brief before gathering materials or beginning construction.

Background

Engineering Context

A launch provider is selecting a support frame design. Teams will compete using a scoring formula that rewards high load capacity and low structure mass.

Objectives

What You Will Practice

  • Optimize structural geometry
  • Use test data to revise a design
  • Calculate an efficiency score
  • Conduct a fair engineering competition
Materials

What You Need

  • Standardized structural materials
  • Adhesive
  • Mass scale
  • Load-test fixture
  • Official dimension gauge
  • Engineering notebook
Procedure

Instructions

  1. Review the official span, height, and material limits.
  2. Sketch three possible structural layouts.
  3. Use a quick model or member-count estimate to select one.
  4. Build the structure and allow joints to cure.
  5. Measure mass and verify dimensions before the official test.
  6. Predict failure location and maximum load.
  7. Complete the official load test.
  8. Calculate the competition score using the provided formula.
  9. Document one redesign for a future round.
Requirements

Engineering Requirements

  • Meet all dimensional limits
  • Use only the issued quantity of material
  • Structure must be free-standing in the fixture
  • Official score uses successful load divided by structure mass
  • No changes after official inspection
Constraints

Constraints & Safety

  • Follow destructive-test safety boundary
  • Do not stand over the structure
  • Use only approved loading increments
  • Wear eye protection
Deliverables

What You Submit

  • Three concepts
  • Inspection record
  • Mass and load results
  • Efficiency score
  • Failure analysis
  • Redesign sketch
Reflection

Reflection Questions

  1. Which design choice contributed most to the score?
  2. Where did mass fail to contribute useful strength?
  3. Did the actual failure match the prediction?
  4. How could joint consistency be improved?
Download PDF
Engineering Tip

Build and inspect one representative joint early; weak joints can dominate the entire structure.

Common Mistake

Optimizing only for maximum load can produce a heavy structure with a poor efficiency score.

Stretch Goal

Use the same material allowance to create a second structure with a different geometry.

Career Connection

Structural engineers use mass efficiency and margin of safety to compare competing aerospace concepts.

Before You Submit
  • Notebook evidence included
  • Requirements checked
  • Testing documented
  • Reflection completed
