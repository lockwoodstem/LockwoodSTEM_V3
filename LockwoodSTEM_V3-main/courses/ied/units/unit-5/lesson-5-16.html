Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

A portfolio is a design story supported by evidence.

Key Idea 2

Strong portfolios include problem definition, concepts, testing, revisions, final design, and reflection.

Key Idea 3

The goal is clarity, not just including every file.

Your Task

Curate the Final Engineering Portfolio

Organize the project evidence into a clear chronological story from user need through final revision.

  1. Select evidence for research, definition, concepts, planning, building, testing, responsibility, and revision.
  2. Remove duplicates and label each artifact.
  3. Write concise explanations connecting evidence to decisions.
  4. Check that limitations and team contributions are represented honestly.

Quality check: A reader can follow how the design evolved and verify the claims without needing the team to explain missing context.
