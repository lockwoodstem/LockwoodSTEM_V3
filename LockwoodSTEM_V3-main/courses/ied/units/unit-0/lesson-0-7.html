Fabrication Readiness

Focus Question: How do safe tool use and careful planning prepare a design for fabrication?

Mission Objective

Demonstrate safe hand and cutting tool practices and prepare the approved launch-pad design for fabrication.

Suggested pacing: 1 × 45-minute class period

Every task advances the same Rocket Launch Pad Design Challenge.

Learning Targets

  • Complete the required Hand & Cutting Tool Certification before building.
  • Create an efficient foam-board cutting layout.

Safety Focus

Demonstrate safe fabrication habits

Cutting-tool readiness

  • Use the correct cutting surface.
  • Keep hands out of the cutting path.
  • Cut away from the body and other people.
  • Use controlled passes instead of excessive force.
  • Inspect tools before use and return them correctly.

Hot-glue readiness

  • Keep the heated tip away from skin and materials not being joined.
  • Use only the amount of glue needed for the joint.
  • Allow joints to cool before loading them.
  • Keep cords and tools organized at the workstation.
  • Clean the work area before leaving.
Cutting Layout

Plan the 12 in × 18 in foam-board sheet

Before cutting, sketch or mark a layout showing every foam-board component your design requires.

  • Fit all planned foam-board parts on the single 12 in × 18 in sheet.
  • Label critical dimensions.
  • Check the layout against the final design drawing.
  • Leave enough room for safe, accurate cuts.
  • Do not cut until the teacher approves the readiness check.
Readiness Check

Ready means design + tools + materials + sequence

  • Final design is teacher-approved.
  • Hand & Cutting Tool Certification is complete for students performing those operations.
  • Foam-board cutting layout is complete.
  • Material quantities fit within the team kit.
  • Build sequence is clear.
  • Rocket-fit checkpoints are identified.

Standard Team Materials

  • 1 × 12 in × 18 in foam-board sheet
  • 20 craft sticks
  • 10 straws
  • 48 in string
  • Hot glue as needed

Core Constraints

  • Use the provided rocket; do not modify it.
  • 12 in × 12 in maximum footprint.
  • 12 in maximum pad height, not including the rocket.
  • Rocket must be installable and removable.
  • Use only approved team-kit structural materials.
