Engineering Foundations & Rocket Launch Pad Challenge

Lessons 0.1–0.3 establish course routines and safety. Beginning with Lesson 0.4, you will use the Engineering Design Process to design, build, test, improve, and defend a launch pad for the instructor-provided rocket.

Unit Project

Rocket Launch Pad Design Challenge

Final Evidence: Hand & Cutting Tool Certification + Final Launch Pad + Qualification Data + Engineering Design Review

Challenge Snapshot

One project. One complete engineering design cycle.

Design

Define the problem, generate three concepts, evaluate them, and develop a buildable final plan.

Fabricate

Complete the Hand & Cutting Tool Certification, then build using the standardized foam-board, craft-stick, straw, string, and hot-glue kit.

Test + Defend

Qualify the launch pad, make one evidence-based improvement, then compete for maximum stability and explain the final design.
