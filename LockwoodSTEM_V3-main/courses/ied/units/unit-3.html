IED Unit 3

CAD Assemblies, Prototyping & Technical Drawings

Unit mission: Build a full rocket CAD assembly, check fit and alignment, prepare parts for printing, assemble a physical prototype, and communicate the final design package.

Lesson MapBack to IED Hub
Unit Focus

Turn individual parts into an assembly, prototype, drawing package, and final presentation.

AssemblePrototypeDocument

Every lesson builds evidence for the final rocket assembly presentation and design package.

Unit Overview

What Unit 3 Builds

You will move from part modeling into assembly relationships, prototype fit evidence, and professional technical drawings.

CAD Assemblies

You will organize components, place parts, apply relationships, check alignment, and create exploded views.

Prototype Evidence

You will export, slice, print, assemble, fit-test, and revise a physical rocket prototype.

Drawing Package

You will create drawing sheets with views, parts lists, balloons, notes, and revision evidence for presentation.
