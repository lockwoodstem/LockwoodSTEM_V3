CAD Model Check and Revision

Focus question: How do engineers check a model before fabrication?

Mission Focus

45-Minute Flight Plan

Use this page with the lesson presentation, Fusion 360, and your engineering notebook.

Learning Targets

  • Check model size, units, features, and printability.
  • Compare CAD evidence to design intent.
  • Document at least one revision or confirmation.

Success Looks Like

Your model is checked before it becomes a printed mistake.

LaunchLearnModelCheckSubmit
Opening Prompt

Why is checking a CAD model before printing usually faster than fixing a bad print?

Think first, then share a response that connects today’s CAD skill to accuracy, editability, printability, or evidence quality.

Reveal: useful CAD thinking move

CAD changes are usually faster and cheaper than wasting print time, material, and queue space on a part with avoidable errors.

Core Skill

What We Are Practicing

Key Idea 1

CAD review prevents printing the wrong part.

Key Idea 2

Models should be checked for size, units, features, and printability.

Key Idea 3

Revision is normal engineering work.

Interactive Check

Which check should happen before export?

AUnits, dimensions, features, and printability
BOnly the model color
COnly the file size
DOnly the teacher’s opinion
Reveal Answer

A — Units, dimensions, features, and printability

A complete check catches problems before they become fabrication failures.

Engineering Workflow

Use These Moves

  1. Inspect dimensions and units.
  2. Compare the model to the design intent.
  3. Revise the model before exporting.
Student Task

Apply the Skill

Complete a CAD review checklist and make or document one model revision.

Checkpoint: be ready to show the CAD evidence that proves your work is controlled, accurate, printable, or ready to revise.

Evidence Challenge

What evidence would you need?

Claim: “My cad model check and revision work is accurate and ready to move forward.”

AA screenshot or model view that shows the feature
BA statement that it looks good
CRelevant dimensions, settings, or test results
DA random file name
Reveal Answer

A + C are the strongest evidence pair. They show what was created and whether it meets the requirement.

Scenario Response

What should the engineer do next?

A model is 25.4 times too large in the slicer. What likely caused it?

AA unit mismatch between inches and millimeters
BToo many fillets
CA missing screenshot
DA slow laptop
Reveal Answer

A — A unit mismatch between inches and millimeters

The strongest next step records the issue, identifies the likely cause, and turns the result into useful CAD evidence.

Student Deliverables

What You Submit

  • At least one revision is documented.
  • Final screenshot shows the corrected model.
  • Exit response connecting the skill to CAD evidence or model quality
