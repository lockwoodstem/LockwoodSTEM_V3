Cornerstone Charter Academy

Academy of Advanced Aerospace Engineering

A four-year engineering pathway connecting design, manufacturing, automation, aerospace systems, flight training, competitions, certifications, and senior capstone work.

Four-Year Pathway

Build skills year by year

Students progress from engineering design fundamentals into systems, manufacturing, aerospace applications, and capstone-level work.

Introduction to Engineering Design

Technical communication, sketching, CAD, fabrication, assemblies, testing, and engineering documentation.

Foundation

Principles of Engineering

Mechanisms, structures, materials, control systems, VEX systems, analysis, and increasingly open-ended design work.

Systems
11

Advanced Manufacturing

Automation, Python, industrial robotics, production systems, manufacturing documentation, inspection, and digital workflows.

Production
12

Aerospace + Capstone

Advanced aerospace engineering electives, independent engineering work, portfolio development, and a senior capstone experience.

Credentials

Certification Pathway

Fusion credential, FabLab safety, FAA TRUST, OSHA-10, CPR/First Aid, Lean Six Sigma White Belt, and later FAA Part 107 opportunities.

Aerospace Training

Cirrus G7 Flight Simulator

Students complete orientation, guided 30-minute flight lessons, Free Flight practice, and maintain a paper flight record.

Competition

Robotics & Drone Team

Students can apply engineering skills through drone competition, robotics, design showcases, and other team-based challenges.

Academy Distinction

What students work toward

  • Complete the engineering course sequence.
  • Complete a senior capstone.
  • Participate in at least one competition, showcase, internship, mentorship, or presentation experience.
  • Maintain appropriate conduct and academic standing.
College & Career Connections

Beyond the classroom

The pathway is designed to connect students with engineering, aviation, manufacturing, and technical career opportunities while building a portfolio of authentic work.

Partnership and postsecondary connections include opportunities associated with Embry-Riddle, UCF, Valencia College, and industry/community partners.
